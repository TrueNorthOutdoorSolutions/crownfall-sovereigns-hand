# Running the paper playtest

## Printing (about 15 minutes)

Everything you need is in `print/`.

1. Print at **100% / Actual size** on US Letter. Do not use "fit to page", or the cards will come out the wrong size.
   - `00-rulebook.pdf`: 1 page. Print one per table.
   - `01-cards.pdf`: 9 sheets. Banner of the Realm (38 cards), Blood & Ember (38 cards), and 3 reference cards.
   - `02-card-backs.pdf`: optional. Only needed if you aren't using sleeves.
   - `03-tokens.pdf`: Command tracks, a round tracker, Shield tokens and "this turn" markers.
   - `04-feedback-sheet.pdf`: 2 pages. One per player per session.
2. Cut along the crop marks. Each card is 63 × 88 mm, standard TCG size.
3. **Sleeve each card in an opaque sleeve with any old card behind it.** This keeps Crown Shards and Schemes hidden and makes shuffling easy.
4. Keep each deck's 8 ★★/★★★ cards (marked "ASCENSION" on the art) apart from its 30-card deck.

The portraits come from Crownfall's game-resolution art (256 px), so printed art looks slightly soft. That's fine for a test.

## Each session

- Two players. **At least half the testers should not have helped design the game.** Include at least two people who rarely play card games.
- Teach using only the rulebook's first paragraph, then let them read the rest. Start a stopwatch when they open the rules.
- Play game 1. Write down the start and end time. Ask "why did you win or lose?" **before** discussing anything.
- Ask whether they want a rematch **right away**, before any discussion. Then play game 2 with the decks swapped.
- Each player fills in the feedback sheet alone.

## Pass gates (after at least 12 games and 6 different players)

| Gate | Target | If it fails |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Immediate rematch: "Yes" | ≥ 70% of players after game 1 | Change the core loop. Don't add content. |
| Could explain how to win after game 1 | ≥ 90% | Rewrite the goal and turn summary. |
| Could say why they won or lost | ≥ 80% gave a concrete reason | Make combat and shard breaks more visible. |
| Match length | Median 15–20 min; ≤ 10% over 25 min | Tune Command cap, shard count or ★★★ damage. |
| Ascending felt like a decision | Median ≥ 3 of 5 | Revisit the sideways cost and timing. |
| Fun overall | Median ≥ 4 of 5 | Find out why before expanding. |

Record the results in a copy of the feedback summary, then bring them back for the next design pass.
**Do not expand to more champions or Origins until every gate passes.**
