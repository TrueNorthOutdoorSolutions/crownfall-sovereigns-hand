# Sovereign's Hand — Set 1: The Shattered Crown

**256 card faces** (248 collectible + 8 tokens): 165 champions, 8 tokens, 77 tactics and schemes, 6 edicts.
Rules version 0.2-paper-1. Generated from the game's card data and Crownfall commit `dc59846`; do not edit by hand. The spreadsheet is `master-card-list-set1.csv` (it has a Variants column for alt and full arts).

**How to read it**

- Names, tiers, Origins, Classes, ability names, Crown Powers, items, Edicts, Monsters and Guardians are canonical Crownfall.
- Champion flavour is canon: ★ lore, ★★ the "gains a star" line, ★★★ the ultimate line (or victory line). Champion art briefs are Crownfall's own star-form descriptions.
- Anything marked **PROPOSED** is my suggestion and needs your approval. Cards marked **Draft** work in the engine but haven't been balance-tested in a deck, so numbers may change; names and art won't.
