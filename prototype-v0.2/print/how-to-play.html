Sovereign's Hand

How to Play · 2 players · about 20 minutes
Crownfall · Set 1: The Shattered Crown
Break all five of your rival's Crown Shards. Deploy champions, Ascend them from ★ to ★★★, and attack your rival's Crown. But a champion that attacks turns sideways and can't guard your Crown on your rival's turn — every attack leaves you open.

What you need

  • One starter deck each: 30 cards + 8 Ascension cards (★★ and ★★★ forms).
  • A Command tracker each and Shield tokens (on the counters sheet). A coin for the first-player toss.
  • Reference cards A and B for quick look-ups.

Your play area

DeckYour face-down 30 cards.
Crown5 face-down shards from your own deck.
Ascension PileYour 8 ★★/★★★ cards. You never draw from it.
Champion Zones4 spaces for champions in play.
Scheme Zones3 spaces for face-down Schemes.
Fallen pileDefeated champions and used Tactics/Schemes, face-up.

Setup

1

Shuffle your deck. Put the top 5 cards face-down as your Crown — don't look.

2

Flip a coin. The winner goes first.

3

First player draws 5. Second player draws 6.

4

Once each, you may shuffle your hand back and redraw the same number.

5

First player skips their first draw. Second player gets +1 Command on their first turn.

Command

Command is what you spend to play cards. It goes up by 1 each turn (max 8) and refills. Unspent Command stays — save some to spring Schemes and cast Swift Tactics on your rival's turn.

Your turn

1

Rise. Stand all your sideways cards back up (ready). Raise Command by 1 (max 8) and refill it. From round 10: break one of your own shards first — "The Crown grows impatient."

2

Draw 1 card. Empty deck? Break one of your own shards instead.

3

Main. In any order, as long as you can pay:

  • Deploy a ★ champion into an empty Champion Zone. It can block right away, but can't attack this turn unless it has Charge.
  • Cast a Tactic, do what it says, then put it in your Fallen pile.
  • Set a Scheme face-down for free (it can't be sprung the turn you set it).
  • Ascend one champion (once per turn).
4

Battle. Attack with your champions one at a time (see Combat).

5

After battle. If you haven't Ascended this turn, you may Ascend now.

6

End. All "this turn" changes end. Discard down to 8 cards. Leftover Command stays on your tracker.

Card types

Champion ★Deploy it to fight. Pay the green cost.
Tactic ✦Cast on your turn.
Swift Tactic ⚡Cast on your turn, or during a fight on either turn.
Scheme ◈Set face-down free; spring it on your rival's turn by paying its cost when its condition happens.

How to Play · continued

Ascending ★ → ★★ → ★★★

Once per turn, in your Main step or after battle:

  1. Pick a champion that was already on the field when your turn began, whose next form is in your Ascension Pile.
  2. Pay the Ascend cost (the gold ↑ gem).
  3. Place the new form on top. Any Shield stays.
  4. Turn it sideways. It can't attack or block until your next turn. (A champion that already attacked is sideways anyway, so it can still Ascend.)
  5. Resolve its Ascend: text, if any.

The choice: Ascend before battle to use the effect now, after battle with a champion that already attacked, or not at all so it can guard your Crown.

Combat

1

Attack. Pick a ready champion. Target your rival's Crown or any rival champion. Turn your attacker sideways; resolve its Attack: text.

2

Block (only when the Crown is attacked). Your rival may block with one ready champion, turning it sideways. Resolve its Block: text.

3

Respond. Defender may spring 1 Scheme or cast 1 Swift Tactic. Then the attacker may cast 1 Swift Tactic.

4

Clash — both at the same time:
Attacker's Might ≥ defender's Guard → defender is defeated.
Defender's Might ≥ attacker's Guard → attacker is defeated.
0 Might defeats nothing.

5

Break shards. Unblocked Crown attack: break 1 (or 2 for a ★★★ — the ♛2 symbol). Breakthrough: if the attacker beats its blocker and survives, it still breaks 1.

Defeated champions go to the Fallen pile with their whole stack. Damage never stays — every clash is fresh.

Breaking a shard

Take the top shard into your hand — it was always one of your own cards. If it has Shardfall, you may cast or set it right away for free, even on your rival's turn.

You lose the moment your last shard breaks.

Quick answers

  • Attack a champion instead of the Crown? Yes — standing or sideways. No shards break, but it clears blockers.
  • Can a new champion block? Yes, the turn it arrives.
  • Who responds first? The defender, then the attacker. One response each per attack.
  • Ability names? The bold name (e.g. Refuse Death.) is just a title. The text after it is what it does.
