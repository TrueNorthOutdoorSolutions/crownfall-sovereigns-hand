# Sovereign's Hand — Set 2: Beneath the Arena

**270 card faces** (266 collectible + 4 tokens): 165 champions, 4 monsters, 6 guardians, 4 tokens, 30 tactics and schemes, 58 relics, 3 edicts.
Rules version 0.2-paper-1. Generated from the game's card data and Crownfall commit `dc59846`; do not edit by hand. The spreadsheet is `master-card-list-set2.csv` (it has a Variants column for alt and full arts).

**How to read it**

- Names, tiers, Origins, Classes, ability names, Crown Powers, items, Edicts, Monsters and Guardians are canonical Crownfall.
- Every Set 2 champion is a **new card** for a returning champion: new epithet (PROPOSED, awaiting approval), new abilities, new art scene. Flavour uses canon voice lines Set 1 didn't use, where any are left.
- Set 2 art briefs combine a PROPOSED underground-arena scene with the champion's canon look for that star form, so the character stays on-model.
- Anything marked **PROPOSED** is my suggestion and needs your approval. Cards marked **Draft** work in the engine but haven't been balance-tested in a deck, so numbers may change; names and art won't.
