# Sovereign's Hand — Lore Bible and Campaign Plan

Owner direction 2026-10-11. Built on Crownfall's canon (`Crownfall/docs/PROLOGUE_CANON.md`, champion data, the 116 recorded announcer lines). Labels as in Crownfall:

- **[ESTABLISHED]** already in shipped text or recorded audio; can't change.
- **[PROPOSED]** new; needs Jerian's approval.
- **[MYSTERY]** must stay unanswered for now (see the end).

Nothing here changes the Crownfall game. Once approved, these become the shared Bible for both games.

---

## 1. The world in one page

- [ESTABLISHED] There is one Crown. It is not whole: it hangs above the Arena of Ascension, its shards orbiting it. Pieces of it exist across the realms (Crown Shards, Crown Artifacts, the Crown Core). The Crown seems to act on its own: "The Crown is watching." "The Crown grows impatient." "The Crown defends itself."
- [APPROVED as common usage] "The Crownfall" means three things: the ancient event when the Crown fell and broke; the floating kingdom raised where it came to rest; and the contest held there.
- [ESTABLISHED] Sovereigns, small ancient beings who wander between realms, compete for the Crown. They do not fight. Champions from every realm and age fight for them, each with a reason of their own.
- [ESTABLISHED] The Arena sets its own terms (Edicts), and something stirs beneath it: the Guardians.
- [PROPOSED] In a game of Sovereign's Hand, each player is a Sovereign and their deck is their banner. The five Crown Shards each player guards are their claim; break all five of a rival's and their claim falls.

## 2. Ascension: the words of the world

| Game | In-world word | When it is used | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ★ | **Called** (a champion who has answered a Sovereign's call) | A champion as they first arrive | [PROPOSED] |
| ★★ | **Ascension**: "they have Ascended" | The Crown raises a champion into more of themselves | [ESTABLISHED] announcer: "Ascension." |
| ★★★ | **Awakening**: "they have Awakened", the Ascension beyond Ascension | The champion becomes their full legend | [ESTABLISHED] announcer: "A legend awakens." |
| Winning the Arena | **Crowned** | A Sovereign accepted by the Crown | [ESTABLISHED] "All hail the Crowned Sovereign." |

[PROPOSED] Common belief: an Ascension is the Crown answering a champion's purpose. Every champion's Ascension and Awakening is a personal moment, remembered and retold: where it happened, what they lost or chose, and the line they spoke. Those moments are the heart of each character's lore (see `champions/`).

## 4. Act I · The Shattered Crown (Set 1 cards)

Eight chapters. Each introduces a group of champions through story Bouts, and ends in **the Crowning** against one Tier V legend. Every one of the 55 champions appears. The player starts with a starter deck and earns cards as they go, so by the end of Act I they have met all 55.

| Chapter | Title [PROPOSED] | Champions introduced | The Crowning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I | The First Light | Solara, Solenne Ash, Kael, Rowan, Elara, Aurelia, General Varr | **Sol** |
| II | Blood and Ember | Fen, Grizz, Rask, Emberling, Mirella, Ignara, Pyrax | **Aurex** |
| III | The Iron Covenant | Pip, Bolt, Gearwick-9, HEX-3, Professor Spark, Titan Rho, Torren, Borin Ironbelly, Knuckle | **ATLAS-Ω** |
| IV | The Greenwall | Bramble, Thorn, Gaia Prime, Hexie, Maeve, Vessimer | **Mother Verdant** |
| V | The Unremembered | Grimm, Dreadmouth, Morrow | **The Unwritten King** |
| VI | The Court of Night | Nyx, Shade, Queen Vespera | **Noctara** |
| VII | Storm and Rift | Kira Volt, Ronin Zero, Lady Volt, Dax Meridian, Captain Blacktide, Zyrak, Veilwalker | **Veyra Sol** |
| VIII | The One Who Refused | Seren, Valkara, Orion, Astryon, Elysian | **Crownless** |

[PROPOSED] Each chapter: Trial 1 meets its champions one by one (small story Bouts, some with puzzle rules), Trial 2 has them test your deck with their own, Trial 3 is a gauntlet with an Edict in play, and the last Bout is the Crowning. Roughly 10–12 Bouts per chapter, about 90 for Act I.

Champions met in a chapter join the player's collection (their ★ card, standard finish), so the campaign is also how a new player builds a collection.

## 5. The Crownings and the three difficulties

[PROPOSED, owner design] Each Tier V legend is the boss of one chapter. Beaten once in Act I, the legends **can't be challenged again until Act II is complete**. Then every Crowning reopens at three difficulties, and the boss plays its legend with that difficulty's printing of its card:

| Difficulty | Boss strength | Boss's legend card art |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Contender** | Standard deck | The normal printing |
| **Masterwork** | Stronger deck, an Edict in its favour | The **Masterwork** printing (East Asian art style) |
| **Omega** | Hardest deck and rules | The **Omega** printing (black and gold, signed) |

This is how the Masterwork and Omega art debuts in game: the player first sees it in the AI boss's hands. Set 1's Masterworks/Omegas cover Sol, Aurex, Noctara and Crownless; Set 2's cover ATLAS-Ω, the Unwritten King, Veyra Sol and Mother Verdant, so all eight are ready once Act II is done. Beating a Crowning on Masterwork or Omega gives prestige rewards (titles, card backs, a Sovereign cosmetic); the cards themselves stay collectible from packs.

## 6. Act II · Beneath the Arena (Set 2 cards)

[PROPOSED] Something stirs beneath the Arena. The Act goes down through the vaults, tunnels, forges and sunken halls under the Crownfall. The same 55 champions return, changed by what they found below (their Set 2 versions, new classes and titles), and the six Guardians are the great threats of the depths. Eight chapters again, each ending in a Guardian or a returning legend, and the finale reopens the Crownings (section 5).

## 7. Writing rules

- Ceremonial and clear; short sentences in narration; each champion speaks in their own voice (use their recorded lines).
- Never contradict ESTABLISHED text. Quote canon lines exactly.
- Everything new is [PROPOSED] until approved.
- Never answer a [MYSTERY]. Characters may hint, disagree or lie.
- Voice-overs: recorded voice or silence, never synthetic voices (Crownfall rule). Every story beat works as text first.

## 8. Mysteries that stay unanswered

From Crownfall's prologue canon: the true nature of the Crown; the full truth of the Crownfall and who built it; the Unwritten King and Crownless; Mother Verdant's ancient knowledge; Aurex and the dragons; Noctara and the Nothing; who built ATLAS-Ω before; Sol and the First Light; Veyra Sol and other realities; the true purpose of Ascension; who the announcer is.
