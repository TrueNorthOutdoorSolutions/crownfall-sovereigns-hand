# Pyrax — Dragon-Blooded
*Elemental · Bruiser · Tier IV · Chapter II · Blood and Ember*

> Status: PROPOSED lore (draft for Jerian's approval). Canon quotes are marked [canon].

## Who they are

"Pyrax's grandmother was a dragon. Pyrax intends to be one too." [canon]

Pyrax is a dragon-blooded warrior with ember scales running up her arms, a wide grin and the confidence of someone who has decided exactly who she is going to be. She is strong, loud, quick to laugh and quicker to fight. She tries to breathe fire roughly twice a day.

She is not a dragon yet. She is very clear that "yet" is the important word.

## Before the Crownfall

Pyrax grew up in the Elemental realm, in the ash-valleys at the foot of the fire mountains, where people with a little dragon in their blood have always lived.

Her grandmother was a dragon. Pyrax never met her. One day, long ago, her grandmother flew towards the sky and did not come back. Pyrax's family never knew why. They kept one of her scales on the mantelpiece, as big as a dinner plate and still warm.

Pyrax grew up staring at that scale. She decided, at about age six, that she would one day be a dragon herself.

## Why they answered the call

Pyrax heard about the Crownfall from a wandering bard who sang about the last dragon, Aurex, fighting in the Arena.

She answered a Sovereign's call for two reasons. The first is that the Crown is said to raise champions into more than they are, and Pyrax wants to be raised into a dragon. The second is quieter: she wants to find Aurex and ask him about her grandmother. She has a feeling, deep down, that he knows something. She may be right. Whether he will ever tell her is another matter.

## The Ascension

It was a fierce bout against a rival who mocked her. "Dragon-blooded?" the rival laughed. "You're a lizard with a temper." The crowd laughed too.

Pyrax did not laugh. She thought of the scale on her family's mantelpiece. She thought of her grandmother flying towards the sky.

Heat rose through her chest. Her scales spread up her arms, across her shoulders, down her side, until half her body glittered with ember-coloured scale. Two curved horns pushed up through her hair. A long tail swept out behind her, burning at the tip.

She grinned so wide her new teeth showed.

"The dragon wakes." [canon]

Fire burst from her in a wave and swept across every rival champion on the field. It was not a full dragon's breath, but it was real fire, and it was hers. The crowd stopped laughing. The rival stopped laughing too.

That night, Pyrax wrote a letter home. It said only: *Halfway.*

## The Awakening — the Ascension beyond Ascension

Pyrax's Awakening came in a bout where Aurex was watching from the stands. She did not know he was there until halfway through. When she saw him, her heart nearly stopped.

She fought harder than she ever had. Every blow, every leap, every breath of fire, she did for the dragon watching her. And for her grandmother.

When the moment came, she felt it in her bones. They grew longer. Her scales covered her completely. Her face stretched into a snout, her claws sharpened, her whole body became a dragon in all but wings. On her back, two small hard bumps pushed up and ached.

The wings are coming. She could feel it.

She threw back her head and roared.

"Breathe fire!" [canon]

Real dragonfire poured out of her, a great gold-and-red torrent, scorching every rival and striking her opponent hard. When it was over, she looked up at the stands. Aurex was already gone.

"Scorched." [canon]

## Voice and temperament

Pyrax is bold, cheerful, competitive and full of energy. She is always testing her strength. Her greeting is "Grandmother would be proud." [canon] Underneath it all she is a little bit afraid that she will never be enough of a dragon to deserve the name.

## Bonds and rivalries

- **Aurex** — The last dragon. Pyrax has asked Aurex about her grandmother six times. Each time, he goes very quiet and walks away. She has decided to keep asking. He has not told her to stop.
- **Ignara** — Pyrax asked Ignara to teach her how to breathe fire. Ignara said fire is not taught. Pyrax practises near her anyway, and Ignara has started giving her small, grudging tips.
- **Fen** — The wolf hunter once tracked her for two days, thinking she was a monster. When he found her, she offered him half her lunch.

## Beneath the Arena (Set 2)

When the banners went down beneath the Arena, Pyrax did not walk down the stairs. She dug. She burrowed straight through stone with her claws, following the heat, until she broke into the deep forge-caverns, glowing and molten.

Below, she became the **Wyrm of the Deep Forge**, a Sapper burrowing through molten caverns with walls collapsing in her wake. Her ability, **Undermine**, breaks things: rivals' Relics, their hidden Schemes, and anything else they were counting on. At her strongest, she scorches the whole rival banner and brings down their plans at the same time.

- **Trial 1 · Bout 7 — "Lizard With a Temper":** Pyrax's introduction. She challenges the player to a straight fight and taunts them throughout.
- **Trial 2 · Bout 7 — "The Dragon-Blooded Test":** Pyrax tests the player with an aggressive fire deck that Scorches every rival champion when her champions rise.
- **Trial 3 · Bout 7 — "Halfway" (Edict in play):** The last Bout before Aurex's Crowning. The Edict gives the player's champions +1 Might each time one of them Ascends. Pyrax fights on the player's side, and she wants Aurex to see.
