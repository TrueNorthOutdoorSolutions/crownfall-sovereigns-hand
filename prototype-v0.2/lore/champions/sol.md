# Sol — The First Light
*Dawnforged · Ascendant, Mystic · Tier V · Chapter I · The First Light (The Crowning)*

> Status: PROPOSED lore (draft for Jerian's approval). Canon quotes are marked [canon].

## Who they are

"Before anything, there was Sol. Sol would like it back that way." [canon]

Sol looks like the first light given a body: a tall, bright figure whose edges are hard to see, as if someone were standing in front of the morning sun. Sol walks slowly, speaks softly and seems faintly amused by almost everything.

No one agrees on what Sol is. The Dawnforged temples sing that Sol is the first sunrise. Kingdom scholars say Sol is simply a very old mystic who has had a long time to practise. Some champions whisper that Sol is not a champion at all.

## Before the Crownfall

This is where the stories stop agreeing.

The Dawnforged say that before the realms had names, there was only one morning, and it was Sol. They say the light was taken, or broken, or given away, and that Sol has been waiting ever since. Solara's order goes further and says the Crown itself is that first sunrise, stolen.

The Kingdom's oldest books mention a "bright traveller" who appears at the start of great ages and leaves before they end.

Sol, asked directly, said only: "I remember the dark. It was very quiet."

## Why they answered the call

Sol's canon lore is the only clue anyone has: Sol would like it back the way it was before anything. What "it" means, Sol will not say.

Why a being that old would need a Sovereign at all is a question many have asked. Sol only smiles.

## The Ascension

No living champion saw Sol's first Ascension. But many saw the day Sol was raised again in the Arena, in front of every Sovereign, and they describe it the same way.

It was dawn. The Arena was cold and grey. Sol walked to the centre of the sand, alone, and looked up at the Crown. The shards stopped turning. For one long breath, nothing in the Arena moved, not even the wind.

Then light gathered behind Sol in a ring, a corona as bright as noon. Four wings of light unfolded from it, slow and enormous, until their tips brushed the Arena walls. Every champion on Sol's banner was wrapped in a shield of warm gold. The cold went out of the morning.

"Brighter than the first morning." [canon]

Solara, watching from the stands, began to cry and did not know why. Sol looked up at the Crown for a moment longer, as if waiting for an answer. If one came, Sol never shared it.

## The Awakening — the Ascension beyond Ascension

Sol's Awakening is the moment every champion in the Crownfall hopes not to face from the other side.

It happened during a Crowning, late in the day, with the sun already low. Sol's Sovereign was nearly beaten. The rival's champions had surrounded Sol, and the light around the old being had dimmed to a candle's glow.

Sol closed their eyes and smiled.

"Let there be DAWN!" [canon]

A miniature sun rose above the Arena. It was small enough to fit between the towers and bright enough to turn night into noon. Its heat swept across the sand and scorched every rival champion at once. Then the same light turned gentle and mended a broken shard of Sol's own claim.

When it faded, Sol was standing alone in the centre of the sand.

"The light endures." [canon]

Some of the Sovereigns watching that day say Sol looked sad. Others say Sol looked like someone remembering.

## Voice and temperament

Sol is calm, gentle, ancient and quietly unsettling. Sol rarely raises their voice and never seems hurried. Sol's greeting to a Sovereign is "Before anything, there was me." [canon] It sounds more like a reminder.

## Bonds and rivalries

- **Solara** — The young novice whose order sings hymns about the first light. Sol is kind to her and has never answered any of her questions.
- **Aurex** — The last dragon and the other great legend of the first two chapters. Aurex and Sol are very old and very wary of each other. Aurex once said Sol "remembers too much and says too little." Sol said Aurex "says too much and remembers it all."
- **Noctara** — Light and night. Sol and Noctara have never fought in the Arena. When they pass each other, both stop, and neither speaks.

## Beneath the Arena (Set 2)

When the banners went down beneath the Arena, Sol went deeper than anyone. Champions who followed found Sol in the deepest cavern of all, standing in total darkness, then saw the first light rising inside it, turning the dark to gold.

Below, Sol became the **Dawn Beneath the World**, an Ascendant and Restorer whose ability, **SECOND DAWN**, mends, shields and lifts an entire banner at once. Why Sol's light rises so easily in a place that has never seen the sun, Sol does not say.
