# Ignara — The Living Pyre
*Elemental · Vanguard · Tier III · Chapter II · Blood and Ember*

> Status: PROPOSED lore (draft for Jerian's approval). Canon quotes are marked [canon].

## Who they are

"Ignara was born in a volcano. She considers everywhere else cold." [canon]

Ignara is a fire elemental warrior in cooling obsidian armour, tall and still and quietly smouldering. She does not shout and does not hurry. She simply stands in front of her friends, and anything that strikes her gets burned.

She is also always, always cold. Everywhere outside a volcano feels to her like a winter morning. She wraps herself in her own heat and wonders how anyone else manages.

## Before the Crownfall

Ignara was born in the heart of Mount Kharr, the greatest volcano of the Elemental realm, where fire spirits rise from the lava like bubbles in a pot. She grew in the deep heat for a hundred years before she ever left.

When she did, she found the outside world strange and chilly. She wandered the slopes, then the lowlands, guarding villages near the volcano from rockfalls and lava flows.

She became the Living Pyre: a guardian, a wall, a fire that never goes out.

## Why they answered the call

Ignara heard that the Crown above the Arena glows. She wants to know if it is hot.

That is the simple version, and she says it with a straight face. The real reason is quieter. In a hundred years of guarding the volcano's villages, she never once felt she belonged anywhere but inside the mountain. In the Crownfall, champions from every realm stand together, all of them far from home. She wanted to see if she could belong there.

## The Ascension

It was a cold, windy bout, and Ignara's armour had cooled almost to grey. Her banner was being driven back, step by step, across the frozen sand. A rival champion had broken through to the Crown Shards, and Ignara stepped into its path.

It struck her. Her armour cracked.

Then the cracks began to glow.

Heat roared up from inside her, through every split in the obsidian, until her armour was a lattice of black stone and bright orange flame. The rival champion leapt back, scorched. The air around Ignara rippled. For the first time since leaving the volcano, she felt warm.

"Burn brighter!" [canon]

She took another blow, and another, and every rival who struck her was burned for it. When the bout ended, she stood in the middle of the sand, her armour cracked and roaring, and smiled. It was the first time any of her friends had seen her smile.

## The Awakening — the Ascension beyond Ascension

Ignara's Awakening came on the coldest night the Arena had ever seen. An Edict had turned the air to frost. Her friends were shivering, their hands too cold to hold their weapons. Even Emberling had shrunk to a flicker.

Ignara looked at them and understood. They were cold too. They always had been, in a way. They had just never told her.

She let go of her armour.

It fell away in glowing pieces, and what was left was a pillar of fire in the shape of a woman. The air around her burned. Frost turned to steam. Her friends gathered close, warming their hands, and rival champions who tried to reach them found only flame.

Every rival who attacked her, or who even came near enough to be attacked, was scorched.

When dawn came, Ignara stood alone in a circle of warm, dry sand. Every rival champion was gone.

"Only ash." [canon]

## Voice and temperament

Ignara is calm, stoic and dryly funny. She speaks rarely and briefly. Her greeting is a complaint: "Everywhere else is cold." [canon] She is fiercely protective and quietly lonely. She softens around small creatures, especially small fiery ones.

## Bonds and rivalries

- **Emberling** — The little living flame follows Ignara everywhere and thinks she is wonderful. Ignara pretends this is embarrassing. She has quietly decided that anything that tries to put Emberling out will have to go through her first.
- **Mirella** — Water to her fire. Mirella makes everything damp; Ignara makes everything dry. They argue constantly and fight beautifully side by side.
- **Pyrax** — The dragon-blooded warrior asked Ignara to teach her how to breathe fire. Ignara said fire is not taught. Pyrax has been trying anyway, near Ignara, for months. Ignara has started giving her small tips, grudgingly.

## Beneath the Arena (Set 2)

When the banners went down beneath the Arena, Ignara felt it immediately: heat. Deep under the Crownfall were the deep forges, great furnaces older than any realm she knew, still burning after untold ages.

For the first time in years, she did not feel cold at all.

Below, she became the **Heart of the Forge**, a Channeler burning at the heart of the deep forges with molten relics floating around her. Her ability, **Forgefire**, sends a burst of flame at a rival every time her banner casts a Tactic. At her strongest, she stands as a wall again, hard to get past.

## In the campaign

- **Trial 1 · Bout 6 — "The Wall of Fire":** A puzzle Bout teaching Block abilities. The rival must attack Ignara each turn. Win by letting the rival burn itself out against her.
- **Trial 2 · Bout 6 — "The Pyre's Test":** Ignara tests the player with an Elemental deck of fire blockers. The lesson: use Scorch and Tactics instead of attacks.
- **Trial 3 · Bout 6 — "The Coldest Night" (Edict in play):** The Edict gives each champion −1 Might. Ignara is the only champion on either side not affected. She fights on the player's side.
