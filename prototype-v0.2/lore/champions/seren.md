# Seren — Keeper of Starlings
*Astral · Summoner · Tier II · Chapter VIII · The One Who Refused*

> Status: PROPOSED lore (draft for Jerian's approval). Canon quotes are marked [canon].

## Who they are

"Seren catches falling stars. Some of them want to fight." [canon]

Seren is a star-child with a lantern staff, a cheerful laugh and a flock of small, bright, bird-shaped friends she calls her Starlings. She is the youngest of the Astral champions and by far the friendliest.

## Before the Crownfall

PROPOSED: Seren grew up on the highest hills of the Astral realm, where the night sky hangs close enough to touch. Every so often a star loses its grip and falls. Most people make a wish. Seren runs after it with a net.

The stars she catches are small and frightened at first. She keeps them in her lantern until they feel brave, then lets them out. Some fly home. Some stay. A few, she has discovered, are very eager to fight anything that frightens her, and these became the first Starlings.

## Why they answered the call

PROPOSED: Seren had noticed that, every year, more stars were falling toward the same part of the sky: toward the Crownfall. She followed them. When a Sovereign called her, she said yes at once.

She wants to know why the stars are falling toward the Crown. She hopes the answer is a nice one.

## The Ascension

PROPOSED: Seren's first Bout in Chapter VIII took place at night, under an open sky. Her rival's champions were big and loud, and her Starlings huddled behind her lantern, flickering.

"It's all right," she told them. "It's all right. I'm here."

But her lantern was empty, and her little flock was frightened.

Then a star fell. Not a small one. It came down slow and golden out of the night sky, straight toward her, and Seren, without thinking, raised her lantern like a net.

The star settled inside, warm and bright as morning. A real star. Her lantern blazed. The night itself slipped off the sky and fell around her shoulders as a cloak, dark blue and scattered with tiny lights.

The Starlings stopped flickering. One by one, then two by two, they lifted off.

"More stars, more friends!" [canon]

The rival champions had not expected to be swarmed by birds made of light. Seren apologised to each of them afterwards. She meant it.

## The Awakening — the Ascension beyond Ascension

PROPOSED: Seren Awakened during the darkest Bout of the chapter, when the Arena had changed its terms and every light on the field had gone out.

Her Starlings were the only light left. Her Sovereign could not see the board. Her friends could not find each other.

So Seren lifted her lantern and began to draw. She traced a line from one Starling to the next, and the line stayed, glowing in the air. Then another. And another. Constellations sketched themselves across the dark, connecting every friend on her side of the field, showing them where to stand and where to go.

Overhead, a whole flock of light-birds circled, wing to wing, more than she had ever caught. They sang as they flew, a bright, high sound like bells far away.

The Arena could see again, and what it saw was a sky full of friends.

When the last rival Shard fell, Seren clapped her hands.

"Twinkle, twinkle!" [canon]

## Voice and temperament

Sunny, chatty and fearless in a way that worries the adults. She is the kindest champion in Chapter VIII, and she does not think of it as a choice.

- "The starlings are hungry." [canon]
- "More stars, more friends!" [canon]

## Bonds and rivalries

- **Orion** (PROPOSED): Orion fires stars; Seren catches them. She thinks this is terribly sad. He thinks it is terribly practical. She has started catching the stars he fires and giving them back to him with names on, which has helped him more than he admits.
- **Elysian** (PROPOSED): Elysian looks after Seren like an older sister, and Seren lets her, mostly. They agree that no one should be hurt in the Crownfall. They agree on very little else about how to manage it.
- **Zyrak** (PROPOSED): The only champion who understands Zyrak without squinting. To Seren, Zyrak is "just a very bright star with good manners."

## Beneath the Arena (Set 2)

**Starlight in the Dark · Delver.** Under the Arena there is no sky at all, which frightens most Astral champions badly. Seren is frightened too. So she brings her own. Her Set 2 card shows her Starlings lighting a vast underground cavern like a night sky.

She becomes a delver below, a champion who finds the way. Her ability, **Starlit Path**, draws you a card when she arrives. Ascended, she draws a card and summons a Starling; Awakened, she draws two, lighting the path ahead.

The deeper she goes, the more she finds old stars, dim and buried in the rock. She has started to wonder who put them there.

## In the campaign

- **Trial 1 · Bout 1 — "Falling Star."** Chapter VIII opens with Seren. A gentle tutorial-style Bout about Summoning: win using only Starlings. Seren joins your banner and gives your Sovereign a name for its first Starling.
- **Trial 2 · Bout 1 — "The Flock."** Seren tests your deck with a Summoner deck that floods the field with Starlings.
- **Trial 3 · Bout 1 — "No Light Left."** A gauntlet Bout under a proposed Edict, *Lights Out*: you cannot see your rival's champions until they attack. Seren draws constellations to reveal one each turn.
