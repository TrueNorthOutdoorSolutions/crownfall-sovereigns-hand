# Dreadmouth — The Hunger Below
*Hollow · Bruiser · Tier III · Chapter V · The Unremembered*

> Status: PROPOSED lore (draft for Jerian's approval). Canon quotes are marked [canon].

## Who they are

"It ate a kingdom once. It is still hungry." [canon]

Dreadmouth is an enormous hollow creature that is mostly jaw. It has no real face, no real body to speak of, and only a handful of words. What it has is appetite: patient, bottomless and old. It does not hate the things it swallows. It does not hate anything. It is simply hungry, the way the sea is simply wet.

[PROPOSED] Champions who have fought beside it say that it is oddly gentle with the things it does not mean to eat. It will carry a lost child across a river in its mouth without so much as a nibble. It will wait for hours outside a door if it has been told to wait. But it is always, always listening for the next meal.

## Before the Crownfall

[PROPOSED] Nobody knows which kingdom Dreadmouth ate, and Dreadmouth is not telling, partly because it does not tell anyone anything, and partly because it is not sure itself. Kingdoms, to a creature that size, taste much the same.

[PROPOSED] What the Hollow realm remembers is this: there was once a country with walls and towers and a great deal of bread, and then there was a very large mouth, and then there was only a valley full of old teeth. The Hollow folk call that valley the Gullet and do not go there after dark.

[PROPOSED] Some in the Arena whisper that the kingdom Dreadmouth ate was the very one whose king was later erased from every record. Others whisper it was the empire Noctara lost. Both the Unwritten King and Noctara have heard these rumours. The King says, flatly, "Not mine." Noctara says nothing at all. Dreadmouth, when asked, only licks its teeth. None of it has ever been proven, and the Arena seems to like it that way.

## Why they answered the call

[PROPOSED] Dreadmouth did not exactly answer the call. It followed it, the way a hound follows the smell of a kitchen. Sovereigns call champions from every realm, and the Arena is full of the most powerful creatures that have ever lived. To Dreadmouth, the Crownfall smells like a feast.

[PROPOSED] Underneath that there may be something more. The Crown is said to grant what a champion truly wants. Dreadmouth has eaten a kingdom and is still hungry. Perhaps the Crown is the only thing large enough to fill it. Perhaps it is the only thing that could make it stop wanting. Ask Dreadmouth which it would prefer and it will say, "Hungry…" [canon], which is not an answer.

## The Ascension

[PROPOSED] It happened in a Bout against a champion of iron and steam, a great clanking thing from the Iron Covenant that had never lost a fight. Dreadmouth bit it and broke a tooth. Then another. The crowd laughed. A jaw that cannot bite is not much of a jaw.

Dreadmouth sat back on the sand, mouth open, ghost-light dim. For a moment it looked almost puzzled, as if no one had ever said no to it before.

Then the Crown looked down.

Pale bone grew across its hide in overlapping plates, like armour that had been waiting to be asked. Its jaw split, and behind the first set of teeth a second jaw unfolded, row on row, newer and sharper. Deep in its throat a fire kindled: not warm and orange, but cold and green, the ghostfire of the Hollow.

Dreadmouth looked at the iron champion. The iron champion, for the first time in its life, took a step back.

"MORE TEETH." [canon]

The crowd stopped laughing. It was over very quickly after that.

## The Awakening — the Ascension beyond Ascension

[PROPOSED] Dreadmouth Awakened in the Gullet itself, the valley of old teeth in the Hollow realm, where a strange Edict had carried a whole Bout. The arena floor became the valley. The valley was littered with the ancient teeth of everything Dreadmouth had ever bitten.

The rival Sovereign's legion advanced. Dreadmouth opened its mouth to meet them.

And the valley answered.

Every tooth in the Gullet rose from the earth at once: thousands of them, white and grey and yellow with age, sweeping up into the air like a flock of startled birds. They spiralled around Dreadmouth and came to rest along its jaws, its back, its sides, until the creature was no longer a beast with teeth but teeth shaped like a beast. A leviathan of the dead. An entire graveyard of teeth, swimming through the air.

The rival champions did not stand their ground. Nobody could have. When the dust settled, Dreadmouth drifted gently back down to the valley floor, ghostfire purring in its throat.

"Delicious." [canon]

And then, after a long pause, so quietly that only its Sovereign heard, it said, "Hungry…"

## Voice and temperament

Dreadmouth says almost nothing, and everything it says is about food.

- "Hungry…" [canon]
- "MORE TEETH." [canon]
- "Delicious." [canon]

[PROPOSED] The trailing dots in "Hungry…" are the most important part of its voice. It is not a roar. It is a sigh. Dreadmouth's hunger is less a threat than a sorrow it has stopped noticing.

## Bonds and rivalries

- **Grimm** [PROPOSED]. Grimm is the only champion Dreadmouth has never tried to eat. Grimm says this is because he is "all bone and no flavour." Dreadmouth seems to regard him as something like a fence post: reliable, familiar and not food. The two of them often stand watch together in silence.
- **Morrow** [PROPOSED]. Morrow raises the fallen to fight again. Dreadmouth makes the fallen very difficult to raise. They have argued, in their way, ever since Morrow reached for a soldier Dreadmouth had already finished with. Morrow calls it "a waste." Dreadmouth calls it "Delicious."
- **Noctara** [PROPOSED]. Something about Noctara makes Dreadmouth uneasy. It will not go near her. Some say a creature of endless hunger recognises something even emptier than itself. Others say it simply does not like how she smells. Noctara has never commented.

## Beneath the Arena (Set 2)

In Act II Dreadmouth becomes **Devourer of Guardians**, a **Hunter**. [canon scene] It is seen "rising from black water with a Guardian's armour still in its teeth."

[PROPOSED] Below the Arena lie flooded halls of black water where the Guardians sleep. Dreadmouth went down there first, before any Sovereign sent it, following the smell of something enormous. The champions who came after found it surfacing from a drowned vault with a piece of a Guardian's shell in its jaws. Nobody knows if the Guardian is still alive. Dreadmouth isn't saying, and it is still hungry.

Its ability, **Guardian Feast**, makes it far stronger when it fights big prey: Monsters, Guardians, and champions who have already Ascended or Awakened. At its fullest, every champion it defeats on your turn draws you a card, because a good meal sharpens the mind. **Ambush** lets it rise out of the dark before anyone sees it coming. Dreadmouth has stopped being a brute and become a hunter: it waits, it chooses, and it goes for the biggest thing in the room.

- **Trial 1 · Bout 2 — "Something in the Gullet"** [PROPOSED] The player crosses the valley of old teeth and something very large follows them. A puzzle Bout: the player must feed Dreadmouth three defeated champions before it agrees to join the banner. Its line on joining: "Hungry…"
- **Trial 2 · Bout 2 — "The Unbitten"** [PROPOSED] Dreadmouth's deck against the player's. Each champion it defeats makes it harder to stop. The lesson: never give a hungry thing an easy first meal.
- **Trial 3 · Bout 1 — "Nothing Left to Raise"** [PROPOSED] A three-way story Bout. Morrow raises fallen soldiers on one side; Dreadmouth devours them on the other; the player has to win while both of them argue over the same Fallen pile.
- **Act II hook — "Black Water"** [PROPOSED] The first Bout beneath the Arena: Dreadmouth surfaces with Guardian armour in its jaws, and the player realises something below has already been woken up.
