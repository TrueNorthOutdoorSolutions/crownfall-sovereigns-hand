# Torren — Shield of the Deep
*Titanborn · Warden · Tier I · Chapter III · The Iron Covenant*
> Status: PROPOSED lore (draft for Jerian's approval). Canon quotes are marked [canon].

## Who they are
"The Titanborn say a shield is a promise. Torren has never broken one." [canon]

Torren is a stone-skinned shield-bearer who carries a slab of rock the size of a door and speaks only when it matters. He is not the strongest of the Titanborn, nor the loudest. He is the one who stays, and the champions behind him trust that he will still be standing when the dust settles.

## Before the Crownfall
The Titanborn are "Mountains wearing people" [canon], and their home is Titan's Deep, a realm of great caverns, underground rivers and halls carved into living rock. [PROPOSED] Torren grew up in a mining village at the edge of the Deep, where the tunnels shift and settle and sometimes collapse. His work was to stand in the dangerous places, holding up the roof with his shield while others ran to safety. Each time, he promised: "I will not move until you are clear." He never did.

## Why they answered the call
[PROPOSED] When a Sovereign's call echoed down into the Deep, Torren's village elders said a Titanborn should go, someone who would hold the line for the realm. They chose Torren, because he had never broken a promise. He made them one more promise before he left: that every champion who stood behind his shield would walk home. He means to keep it.

## The Ascension
The rival champions were throwing everything at Torren. Fire, steel, spells and stones crashed against his slab shield, and with every blow a new crack ran through the rock. Behind him, two smaller champions crouched low, too hurt to move.

The shield split down the middle.

Torren did not step aside. He pressed his broken shield together with both hands and said, under his breath, the old Titanborn words: a shield is a promise.

The Crown heard him. Light ran down into the cracks of the stone and filled them with gold. The two halves grew together and kept growing, broadening and rising until Torren held not a slab but a gate: a great carved door of stone, its surface covered in old Titanborn patterns, every line glowing with warm golden veins.

"Stone endures." [canon]

The rival's next strike broke against the gate and fell away. Behind it, his allies stood up again, sheltered and strong, and the Bout began to turn.

## The Awakening — the Ascension beyond Ascension
Torren's Awakening came in a Bout where his whole banner was failing. One by one his allies fell, until only he and a single small champion still stood. The rival's strongest fighter stepped forward to finish it.

Torren set down his gate, knelt, and placed one hand on the Arena floor, as if listening to the mountain beneath it.

Then he rose, and the mountain rose with him.

The Crown's light lifted him until he seemed to be a whole peak made small. Crystal spires burst from his shoulders, clear and shining. His gate shield deepened into the colour of old granite, and in its center a single rune began to move, turning slowly, alive. Wherever its light touched, his allies were wrapped in stone-bright shields.

The rival's champion struck the living rune with everything it had. Torren did not move. Not a step. Not an inch.

When the Bout ended, the small champion behind him walked off the field without a scratch. Torren lowered his shield.

"Promise kept." [canon]

## Voice and temperament
Torren is calm, gentle and slow to speak. He says what he will do and then does it. His first words to any ally are always the same: "Stand behind me." [canon] He is fierce only when someone he protects is in danger.

## Bonds and rivalries
- **Borin Ironbelly** [PROPOSED]: a cousin from the forges of Titan's Deep. Borin talks; Torren listens. Borin forged the first rim for Torren's old slab shield and still claims the gate is "mostly my work."
- **Knuckle** [PROPOSED]: Torren and Knuckle grew up in different corners of the Deep. Knuckle thinks shields are for people who cannot hit hard enough. Torren has never answered. Knuckle has never got past him.
- **Bolt** [PROPOSED]: two guardians who agree on everything. Bolt calls Torren's shield a door. Torren calls Bolt's door a promise.

## Beneath the Arena (Set 2)
Beneath the Arena, Torren finds the drowned halls of the Deep and a sealed vault door that no one has opened in ages. He becomes the **Keeper of the Deep Vault**, a **Sentinel**. [PROPOSED] Torren does not know what lies behind the door, only that someone, long ago, promised to keep it closed. He has taken that promise as his own. His **Vault Ward** weakens every champion that strikes at his Sovereign's Crown.

## In the campaign
- **Trial 1 · Bout 6: "Stand Behind Me"** [PROPOSED]: Torren's introduction. A puzzle Bout: protect two weak allies for three turns using his Bulwark and Guard.
- **Trial 2 · Bout 1: "Stonewall"** [PROPOSED]: Torren's deck of Guard boosts and Shields forces the player to find a way around a wall that will not move.
- **Trial 3 · Bout 6: "Promise Kept"** [PROPOSED]: an Edict says the first champion to fall loses their Sovereign a Shard. Torren fights beside the player, and keeping every ally alive is the whole point.
