# Elysian — Grace of the Firmament
*Astral · Mystic · Tier IV · Chapter VIII · The One Who Refused*

> Status: PROPOSED lore (draft for Jerian's approval). Canon quotes are marked [canon].

## Who they are

"Elysian does not believe anyone deserves to die in the Crownfall. The Crownfall disagrees." [canon]

Elysian is a serene celestial healer with feathered wings and a voice like early morning. She is the gentlest champion in the Arena and also one of the most stubborn. She fights by refusing to let anyone fall.

## Before the Crownfall

PROPOSED: Elysian comes from the Firmament, the high, bright edge of the Astral realm where dawn begins. Her people are healers and keepers of the morning.

For a long time, Elysian tended the sick and the hurt of the Astral realm, and she was very good at it. But she kept hearing stories of the Crownfall: of champions who went there and were not seen again, of battles that left good people lost. The stories bothered her more than any wound she had ever tended.

So she flew to see for herself.

## Why they answered the call

PROPOSED: Elysian did not come to win. She came to make sure no one is lost on her watch.

She answered a Sovereign's call because she knew she could not change the Crownfall from the stands. A healer has to be where the hurt is. If the Crownfall insists on being dangerous, Elysian insists on being there, wings open, between the danger and everyone else.

She has a second hope, which she shares with very few. She would like to know what happens to those who are crowned. Nobody has been able to tell her.

## The Ascension

PROPOSED: Her Sovereign's side of the field was failing. One champion after another was struggling, battered and nearly beaten, and the rival's champions were closing in on them all.

Elysian could not reach them all. She had only two wings.

She stood in the middle of her friends and spread her wings as wide as they would go, and it was not wide enough. She shut her eyes and refused to accept that.

Something answered her. A second pair of wings unfolded from her back, slow and shining, and wrapped around her side of the field. Above her head, a halo appeared, written in a soft gold script that no one in the Arena could read.

The rival champions struck the wings and could not get through.

"My wings shelter many." [canon]

When the Bout ended, every one of Elysian's friends was still standing. She checked each of them, one by one, before she let herself smile.

## The Awakening — the Ascension beyond Ascension

PROPOSED: It was the closest Elysian had ever come to breaking her promise.

The Arena had changed its terms. A great blow was falling on one of her friends, too fast and too strong to stop, and Elysian was on the far side of the field.

She did not think. She flew.

And as she flew, the sky behind her changed colour. Pink, then gold, then the clear pale light of first morning. Her wings were no longer feathers. They were dawn itself, wide and warm, and when she reached her friend she folded the light around them both like a blanket.

The blow struck the dawn and faded into it, like a shadow when the sun comes up.

Elysian looked down at her friend, battered but whole, and spoke more fiercely than anyone had ever heard her speak.

"Not yet — live!" [canon]

Her friend did. Every one of her friends did. When the Bout ended, she turned to her Sovereign, tired and shining.

"All of you, safe." [canon]

## Voice and temperament

Calm, warm and immovable. Elysian is never angry, but she is never, ever persuaded to give up on anyone.

- "No one dies today." [canon]
- "My wings shelter many." [canon]

## Bonds and rivalries

- **Valkara** (PROPOSED): Her oldest friend and fiercest argument. Valkara believes some deserve to fall after a fair hearing; Elysian believes no one does. They have argued for centuries and would be lost without each other.
- **Seren** (PROPOSED): Elysian watches over Seren like an older sister, which Seren puts up with, mostly.
- **Crownless** (PROPOSED): Crownless is the only champion Elysian cannot read. When she offered to heal the cracks in that broken regalia, Crownless said nothing. The cracks remained. She wonders whether Crownless wants them there.

## Beneath the Arena (Set 2)

**Light of the Lost Halls · Seer.** Beneath the Arena are forgotten halls full of old traps, and Elysian goes down first, so that no one else has to. Her Set 2 card shows her descending into the halls, foreseeing every trap before it springs.

Below, she becomes a seer. Her ability, **Prophecy**, makes your Schemes cheaper to spring and draws you a card when she arrives. Ascended, she shields each champion you control; Awakened, your Schemes become cheaper still.

## In the campaign

- **Trial 1 · Bout 5 — "No One Dies Today."** Elysian's introduction. A puzzle Bout: win without any of your champions being defeated, using Shields. Elysian joins your banner with a promise.
- **Trial 2 · Bout 5 — "Sheltering Wings."** Elysian tests your deck with a protective deck that Shields every champion it controls.
- **Trial 3 · Bout 5 — "First Morning."** The last gauntlet Bout before the Crowning, under a proposed Edict, *Mercy*: defeated champions return to their owner's hand instead of the discard. Afterwards Elysian walks with you to the edge of the dark, where Crownless waits.
