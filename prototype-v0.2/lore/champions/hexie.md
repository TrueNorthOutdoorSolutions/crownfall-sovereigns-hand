# Hexie — Apprentice of Mischief
*Hexbound · Arcanist · Tier I · Chapter IV · The Greenwall*
> Status: PROPOSED lore (draft for Jerian's approval). Canon quotes are marked [canon].

## Who they are
"Hexie is not supposed to be here. Neither was anything she has ever turned into a toad." [canon]

Hexie is an apprentice witch in a hat several sizes too big, with a crooked staff, a quick laugh and a talent for turning trouble into more trouble. Her spells go wrong about half the time. The other half, they go wrong in useful ways.

## Before the Crownfall
The Hexbound are "Cursed and cursing" [canon], the witches and hex-makers who live at the edges of other realms. [PROPOSED] Hexie grew up in the Hexmire, a misty marshland along the Greenwall's southern edge, where the Hexbound brew in hollow trees and the toads are very, very nervous. Her stern teacher taught her every rule of hexcraft. Hexie broke them one at a time, to see what would happen. Most of what happened was toads.

## Why they answered the call
[PROPOSED] The call was never meant for Hexie. That is the whole point of her. When a Sovereign's call reached the Hexmire, it was meant for her teacher. Hexie read the summons first, packed her teacher's best hat by mistake and arrived at the Crownfall before anyone noticed she was gone. The Arena did not send her home. The Crown, perhaps, found her interesting. Now she is determined to prove she belongs, and to find out what kind of toad a Crown would make.

## The Ascension
Hexie's first Ascension came in the middle of a spell going badly.

She had aimed a hex at a rival knight. The hex had bounced off his shield, ricocheted around the Arena, and was now heading straight back toward her. Hexie squeaked, ducked and clutched her hat over her ears.

The Crown above, which may have been laughing, sent down a thread of light.

The hex hit her hat, and the hat ate it.

Hexie peeked out. Her hat had grown. It was wider, taller and much floppier, and two big round eyes had blinked open near the brim. They looked down at her. They looked very unimpressed. Her staff twisted in her hand, and from somewhere inside it came a small, offended voice saying that her aim was terrible.

Hexie stared at her hat. Then at her staff. Then she burst out laughing.

"My hat has opinions!" [canon]

The hat narrowed its eyes at the rival knight. The staff sighed and pointed itself. Hexie fired, and this time, with a little help from her new advisors, the hex landed square. The knight's strength drained away, and his swing barely ruffled her robes.

## The Awakening — the Ascension beyond Ascension
Hexie's Awakening came during a Bout under a strange Edict that made every spell more powerful and less predictable. Every other witch would have been careful. Hexie had never been careful once in her life.

She started casting everything.

The Crown answered by giving her something to ride. A big iron cauldron rose out of the Arena floor, bubbling with green brew, and floated up beneath her. Hexie hopped in. Her hat cheered. Her staff complained. Around her, a swarm of glowing hexes appeared, drifting and blinking like fireflies on a summer night.

She had become a chaos witch, and she was having the best time of her life.

The cauldron swooped across the field. The hexes flew wherever she pointed, and wherever they landed, rival champions shrank, wobbled and stumbled. One very large one turned briefly into something green and surprised.

When it was over, Hexie leaned out of her cauldron and waved at the crowd.

"Ribbit. Ha!" [canon]

## Voice and temperament
Hexie is mischievous, bold and good-hearted. She jokes in the middle of danger and apologises in the middle of victories. Her favourite question is, "Who wants to be a toad?" [canon] Underneath the chaos is a young witch who wants to be taken seriously, and is learning that she already is.

## Bonds and rivalries
- **Maeve** [PROPOSED]: the ancient forest witch is both Verdant and Hexbound, and the closest thing Hexie has to a hero. Maeve once offered Hexie a bargain. Hexie said no. Maeve has respected her ever since.
- **Vessimer** [PROPOSED]: the masked chemist of the Hexbound thinks Hexie's spells are sloppy. Hexie thinks Vessimer needs to have more fun. They trade ingredients and insults.
- **Bramble** [PROPOSED]: she once turned him into a toad by accident. He was a very sturdy toad. They are friends now, mostly.

## Beneath the Arena (Set 2)
In the caves beneath the Arena, Hexie becomes the **Reader of Bones**, a **Seer**, casting knucklebones across the stone floor. [PROPOSED] The bones glow with the rival's next move, and for the first time Hexie's magic tells her something before it happens. Her hat is deeply impressed. Her **Bone Reading** makes her banner's Schemes cheaper to spring, and at its fullest, they cost nothing and can be sprung the moment they are set.

## In the campaign
- **Trial 1 · Bout 2: "Not Supposed to Be Here"** [PROPOSED]: Hexie's introduction. A puzzle Bout where her Might-draining hexes must be used on the right rival at the right time.
- **Trial 2 · Bout 2: "Who Wants to Be a Toad?"** [PROPOSED]: Hexie's deck keeps shrinking the player's strongest champions. The player learns to go wide rather than tall.
- **Trial 3 · Bout 2: "Ribbit. Ha!"** [PROPOSED]: an Edict doubles every Might reduction. Hexie joins the player's banner, and her small hexes become huge ones.
