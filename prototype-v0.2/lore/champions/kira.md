# Kira Volt — Arc-Tech Prodigy
*Voltborn · Ranger · Tier I · Chapter VII · Storm and Rift*

> Status: PROPOSED lore (draft for Jerian's approval). Canon quotes are marked [canon].

## Who they are

"Kira rebuilt her first pistol from a lightning rod. She intends to rebuild the Crown the same way." [canon]

Kira Volt is the youngest gunner ever to answer a call into the Crownfall, and she would like everyone to stop mentioning it. She is a tinkerer first and a fighter second. Where other champions see a broken thing, Kira sees parts.

## Before the Crownfall

Kira grew up in a Voltborn storm-town of copper roofs and lightning rods, where the sky was something you harvested. One night a bolt split the oldest rod in town down the middle. Everyone else saw a ruined rod. Kira saw a barrel, a grip and a coil. By morning she had her first pistol.

The elders told her that some broken things should stay broken. Kira took that as a challenge.

## Why they answered the call

Kira wants to rebuild the Crown. [canon reason]

She has studied the shards through a homemade scope and filled a notebook with sketches of how they might fit together. She is fairly sure she is wrong about most of it. She is completely sure she is not wrong about all of it.

## The Ascension

Kira's first true fight in the Arena went badly. Her rival's champion was armoured, patient and far too strong. Her single pistol fired until the coil glowed red, and then it stopped.

She ducked behind a broken pillar and did what she always does. She took things apart.

Her coat had a spare coil sewn into the lining "for emergencies." She tore it free. She had the dead pistol in one hand and the coil in the other, and above her, through a gap in the pillar, the Crown's shards turned slowly in the sky.

The air went still. A thin thread of light ran down from the shards and touched her hands.

When she looked again, there were two pistols, both humming, both clearly hers. The coil hung from her coat, glowing like a captured storm.

Kira grinned and stood up from behind the pillar.

"Double the volts!" [canon]

The armoured champion did not get to be patient much longer. Afterwards, Kira insisted she had built the second pistol herself. The Crown, as usual, said nothing to correct her.

## The Awakening — the Ascension beyond Ascension

It happened in a storm. The Arena had changed its terms that day, and the clouds had come down low and black over the Crownfall. Every other champion took cover.

Kira climbed the tallest tower on the Arena's rim.

For weeks she had been building little brass orbs meant to catch a lightning strike and hold it. None had ever worked. She set them in a ring around her anyway and raised both pistols to the sky.

The lightning came. It struck the tower, then the orbs, then her. For one bright moment she could not see.

When the light faded, the orbs were floating. They circled her slowly, crackling, each one full of storm. She fired once across the Arena, and the shot left a trail of lightning hanging in the rain behind it like a crack in the sky.

Far below, a rival Sovereign's banner fell. Kira blew a little smoke from one barrel.

"Nobody out-sparks me." [canon]

## Voice and temperament

Quick, cheerful and loud, with the confidence of someone who has never failed at anything big enough to stop her. She talks to her tools and names her batteries.

- "Charged and ready!" [canon]
- "Nobody out-sparks me." [canon]

## Bonds and rivalries

- **Lady Volt** (PROPOSED): Kira's hero, though she would never say so. Lady Volt is graceful and undefeated; Kira is messy and loud. Lady Volt finds her amusing, which Kira finds unbearable.
- **Ronin Zero** (PROPOSED): Kira badly wants to open his frame and see how it works. He politely declines. She leaves him small repaired gifts anyway.
- **Dax Meridian** (PROPOSED): The two gunslingers keep a running score of trick shots. They disagree on the score.

## Beneath the Arena (Set 2)

**The Long Shot · Sharpshooter.** Below the Arena, Kira learns patience. The flooded caverns are too wide and dark for quick pistol work, so she builds a rifle from salvaged vault rails and learns to wait, perched on a girder high above the water. Her ability, **Railshot**, is one careful shot at one rival champion instead of a spray of sparks. Awakened below, Railshot can defeat a lightly guarded champion outright, from any distance.

In the depths she finds vault mechanisms that look strangely like the Crown's shards. She does not know what that means. She writes it down.
