# Gaia Prime — The Worldroot
*Verdant · Warden · Tier IV · Chapter IV · The Greenwall*
> Status: PROPOSED lore (draft for Jerian's approval). Canon quotes are marked [canon].

## Who they are
"Gaia Prime was old when the Crownfall was built. She remembers who built it." [canon]

Gaia Prime is an ancient living tree titan, so old that her bark holds the shapes of forgotten seasons. She speaks rarely, and when she does, other champions stop to listen. She knows something about this place that nobody else does. She has said so plainly. She has never said what it is.

## Before the Crownfall
[PROPOSED] The Verdant say "The forest mends its own." [canon] Gaia Prime is where that saying began. Long ago, before the Greenwall had its name, she was the first great tree to stand at its heart, and the forest grew outward from her roots. Every guardian of the Greenwall, from Bramble to the oldest oaks, can trace its seed back to her shade.

## Why they answered the call
[PROPOSED] Gaia Prime has not said why she came, but she has said where. Her arrival line is a warning, a promise or both: "I remember who built this place." [canon] Some think she has come to see whether old work still holds; others, to keep a promise made before the Crown fell. Mother Verdant, who calls every forest her child, has asked Gaia directly. Gaia only answered, "Not yet." [MYSTERY: who built the Crownfall stays unanswered. Gaia hints; she never tells.]

## The Ascension
Gaia Prime's first Ascension came in a Bout so quiet that most of the crowd missed it.

She had stood unmoving for three turns, taking blow after blow, while the rival's champions hacked at her bark. Then a small bird, frightened by the noise, flew down from the Arena's high walls and landed on her shoulder.

Gaia turned her great head to look at it. The Crown, high above, turned with her.

Light trickled down her trunk like rain through leaves. Along her limbs, buds swelled and burst into blossom, pink and white, until she was covered in flowers. Above her head, her branches knit into a wide canopy crown, green and gold and full of light. In the hollows of her shoulders, nests appeared, and birds came from every corner of the Arena to settle in them, singing.

Gaia Prime took one slow step forward, and the whole Arena felt it.

"The forest walks with me." [canon]

Her roots spread outward beneath the sand, and the rival champions felt the ground itself push back against them. Behind her, her allies stood taller, sheltered by her shade.

## The Awakening — the Ascension beyond Ascension
Gaia Prime Awakened on the day the Arena floor cracked.

An Edict had turned the field to dry, broken stone, and her Sovereign's champions were weakening one by one, like plants in a drought. Gaia lowered her roots as deep as they would go, into the old foundations of the floating kingdom, and listened.

Whatever she heard there, she did not share it. But the Crown answered.

The light rolled outward from her like spring. Her body widened into a walking forest of trunks and canopies, moss and fern, flowers and falling leaves. Where she stood, the cracked stone turned green. Grass rushed outward across the whole field, around her allies, under their feet, healing as it went.

She raised her ancient arms, and the earth rose with them.

"Worldroot!" [canon]

Roots thick as towers burst from the green ground and struck every rival champion at once. When the dust settled, the Arena was a meadow, and Gaia Prime stood at its heart, as she once stood at the heart of the Greenwall.

"Grow." [canon]

## Voice and temperament
Gaia Prime is patient, wise and gently unknowable. She speaks in very few words. She is kind to young things, with a quiet sadness, like a tree that has watched too many winters. Pressed about the past, she simply smiles.

## Bonds and rivalries
- **Mother Verdant** [PROPOSED]: two of the oldest beings of the Verdant. Mother Verdant calls every forest her child. Gaia, who is older than many forests, has never quite agreed to be one. Their respect is deep and their silences are long.
- **Bramble** [PROPOSED]: her favourite seedling. He once asked her who built the Crownfall. She told him to grow taller first.
- **Maeve** [PROPOSED]: the forest witch has tried to bargain with Gaia for her memories. Gaia has always refused, kindly. Maeve has never stopped asking.

## Beneath the Arena (Set 2)
Beneath the Arena, Gaia's roots are found already wrapped around the foundations of the floating kingdom, holding the cracked stone together. She is the **Root of the Arena**, a **Restorer**. [PROPOSED] No one knows how long her roots have been there. Gaia will only say she is "mending." Her **Deep Roots** shields every champion of her banner and Mends her Sovereign's broken Crown Shards.
