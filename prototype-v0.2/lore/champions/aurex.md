# Aurex — The Last Dragon
*Wyrmblood · Ascendant, Bruiser · Tier V · Chapter II · Blood and Ember (The Crowning)*

> Status: PROPOSED lore (draft for Jerian's approval). Canon quotes are marked [canon].

## Who they are

"Every dragon died for the Crown. Aurex is here to find out whether it was worth it." [canon]

Aurex is a massive dragon warrior, scaled in deep red and gold, who walks on two legs and carries the weight of an entire people on his back. He is the last of the dragons.

He is not here for glory. He is here for an answer, and he is not sure he wants it.

## Before the Crownfall

Aurex comes from the Wyrmblood realm, the old home of the dragons: a land of high red peaks, great nests carved into mountains, and skies that were once full of wings.

What happened to the other dragons is not something Aurex speaks of easily. All that is known is that every dragon died for the Crown. How, and why, and whether they chose it, Aurex will not say. The stories told in other realms disagree. Some say the dragons flew to the Crown together, long ago, and never came back. Aurex has heard all of these stories. He has not corrected any of them.

## Why they answered the call

Aurex's reason is canon, and he says it plainly to anyone who asks: he wants to find out whether the dragons' deaths were worth it. "The last dragon answers." [canon] That is how he greets a Sovereign, as if a call had reached him across ages.

He answers a Sovereign because no champion can face the Crown alone, not even a dragon. He hopes that if he gets close enough, it will tell him what his people's sacrifice bought. He fears it will tell him nothing at all.

## The Ascension

It was Aurex's first bout in the Arena, and the crowd had come to see a dragon. He gave them one. He tore through the rival banner with fire and claw, and still the Crown hung silent above him.

He stopped, in the middle of the sand, and looked up.

"Answer me," he said. The Crown's shards kept turning.

Then heat rose from within him, the old heat of the red peaks. Molten armour began to form over his folded wings, glowing like cooling metal, sealing them in shining plates. Fire leaked from every seam. He spread his armoured wings for the first time in ages, and they caught the air.

"My wings remember the sky." [canon]

He rose, just for a moment, above the Arena floor. The crowd gasped. Then he came down, fire pouring from every seam, and struck the next defender hard. It was not an answer. But it was something. The Crown had noticed him. Aurex was not sure whether to be glad.

## The Awakening — the Ascension beyond Ascension

Aurex's Awakening is spoken about in every realm.

It happened during a Crowning. The rival had built a banner of champions meant to bring down a dragon. Aurex was wounded. His Sovereign was close to losing. And for a moment, high above the Arena, the Crown's shards seemed to slow, as if waiting.

Aurex stopped fighting. He looked up at the Crown and asked, aloud, in a voice that shook the stands, the question he had carried across all those years.

The Crown did not answer.

So Aurex answered for it.

His body grew and changed until he was a full celestial dragon, vast and blazing. The sky above the Arena turned red. A wall of flame rose around the sand. He opened his jaws.

"Let the world burn!" [canon]

Fire swept the field. Every weaker rival champion was gone in a moment. When the flames died down, Aurex stood alone on the scorched sand and looked at the Crown for a long time.

"Worth it? Perhaps." [canon]

## Voice and temperament

Aurex is grave, proud, weary and quietly sorrowful. He speaks slowly and deeply, and he does not waste words. He carries grief like armour. Even his victories sound like questions: "Worth it? Perhaps." [canon]

## Bonds and rivalries

- **Pyrax** — The dragon-blooded warrior whose grandmother was a dragon. She has asked Aurex about her grandmother six times. Each time he goes silent and walks away. He has never told her to stop asking.
- **Sol** — The other great legend of the first chapters. Aurex once said Sol "remembers too much and says too little." Sol said Aurex "says too much and remembers it all."
- **General Varr** — The old soldier who has held every line and never been given the Crown. Aurex respects him more than almost any champion in the Arena.

## Beneath the Arena (Set 2)

When something stirred beneath the Arena, Aurex went down alone, deep into the Guardians' lair. What he found there, and what he did, other champions only saw afterwards: the last dragon standing over a fallen Guardian.

Below, he became the **Bane of the Guardians**, an Ascendant and Hunter whose ability, **GUARDIAN'S BANE**, makes him deadly against Monsters, Guardians and anyone who has already Ascended. At his strongest, his fire sweeps the field as it once did above.

He has not said why he hunts the Guardians so fiercely.
