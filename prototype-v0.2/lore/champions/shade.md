# Shade — The Mask With No Face
*Umbral · Assassin · Tier II · Chapter VI · The Court of Night*

> Status: PROPOSED lore (draft for Jerian's approval). Canon quotes are marked [canon].

## Who they are

"Shade has worn many faces. The mask is the only one that stayed." [canon]

Shade is a masked assassin with a red-lacquered blade, and nobody knows who is behind the mask. Possibly nobody does. Shade moves without sound, speaks without warmth and chooses targets the way a judge chooses a verdict: carefully, finally, and without apology.

[PROPOSED] In the Arena, Shade's "marks" and "executions" are a duellist's terms for defeating a champion in a Bout: champions who fall in the Crownfall return to their Sovereigns, and the game is about pride, not graves. But Shade speaks of them with a seriousness that makes even veterans uneasy.

## Before the Crownfall

[PROPOSED] In the Umbral realm, there was once a guild of masked duellists who served the Court of Night: the Faces. Each wore a different mask, and each mask came with a name, a voice, a history. A Face could become someone else entirely by changing masks. The best of them changed often.

[PROPOSED] Shade was the best of them. Shade wore the Smiling Face, the Weeping Face, the Face of the Old Man, the Face of the Young Prince, a hundred faces more, and lived each life fully while it lasted. Somewhere along the way, the face Shade was born with was set down, and forgotten, and lost.

[PROPOSED] Then came a night when the Faces were disbanded. Nobody in the Court will say why. Every mask was burned except one: a plain, smooth, blank mask that none of the Faces had ever worn, because it did not belong to anyone. Shade picked it up. It stayed.

## Why they answered the call

[PROPOSED] Shade does not explain. "A mark has been chosen" [canon] is the closest thing to an answer anyone has received.

[PROPOSED] The guess most often made in the Court of Night is that Shade wants a face back. Not one of the hundred masks: their own face, the first one, the one that was set down and lost. The Crown is said to grant what a champion truly wants. Perhaps it could return a face that even its owner has forgotten.

[PROPOSED] Another guess, quieter: that Shade wants to see what the Crown looks like when it is not being looked at. Some of the Court of Night believe the Crown, like their own court, is different when no one is watching. Shade intends to find out.

## The Ascension

[PROPOSED] Shade's Ascension happened in silence. Of course it did.

It was a Bout in the Court of Night's mirror hall, a long chamber walled with tall black mirrors. Shade's rival was a champion who could take anyone's appearance, and the rival had chosen to look like Shade: same mask, same red blade, same stillness. The crowd could not tell them apart.

The two masks faced each other. Shade looked into the copy, and for the first time in a very long time, wondered what was really underneath.

A sound broke the silence: a small, sharp *crack*.

A thin line had opened down the middle of Shade's mask, and from it spilled a crimson glow, warm and steady, like an ember under ash. Not a face. Not yet. But something. Something real.

The copy's mask stayed whole. It had nothing underneath at all. The crowd could tell them apart now.

Shade raised the red blade.

"The mask cracks. Good." [canon]

The copy did not last long after that.

## The Awakening — the Ascension beyond Ascension

[PROPOSED] Shade Awakened during the hardest Bout in the Court of Night's memory: a duel against all of the old masks at once.

An Edict had called up the Faces, every one of the hundred lives Shade had once worn, rising from the burned guild hall as shadows wearing their own masks. The Smiling Face. The Weeping Face. The Old Man. The Young Prince. All of them reaching for Shade, all of them saying, *come back, be us again.*

Shade did not fight them one at a time. Shade simply let go.

The cloak unravelled into ribbons. The ribbons became knives. The hood fell back, and there was nobody inside it: an empty hood, and floating before it, the cracked mask, glowing crimson from within. Shade had become a wraith of ribbons and knives that answered to one face only, the one that had stayed.

The hundred old masks fell one by one to the ribbons. None of them was Shade anymore. None of them ever had been.

When the hall was silent, the mask turned toward the rival Sovereign.

"Executed." [canon]

## Voice and temperament

Cold, clipped, final. Shade says as little as possible and means all of it.

- "A mark has been chosen." [canon]
- "The mask cracks. Good." [canon]
- "Executed." [canon]

[PROPOSED] The "Good." is the only warmth Shade ever shows. Every crack in the mask is a step toward something real, and Shade welcomes every one.

## Bonds and rivalries

- **Nyx** [PROPOSED]. Nyx once stole Shade's mask and saw what was (or was not) behind it. She gave it back immediately and has never told anyone. Shade has never thanked her, never punished her, and never let her out of sight since. It might be a rivalry. It might be the closest thing Shade has to a friend.
- **Queen Vespera** [PROPOSED]. The Faces once served Vespera's court. Shade still does, sometimes, when a mark is worth it. Vespera calls Shade "my last Face." Shade has never answered to it.
- **Solenne Ash** [PROPOSED]. Solenne has sworn to burn away every shadow in the Crownfall and has started a list. Shade is on it, near the top. Shade has a list too.

## Beneath the Arena (Set 2)

In Act II Shade becomes **Mask of the Depths**, a **Rogue**. [canon scene] It is seen as "a faceless mask hanging in the dark of the lowest halls, blades already drawn."

[PROPOSED] In the lowest halls under the Arena there are no lights and no echoes, and things that hang there in the dark seem to be waiting for someone. The champions who went down found a mask hovering in the blackness, blades drawn, and did not know whether it was Shade or something wearing Shade's face. Shade does not say. Shade has stopped being only an assassin; in the depths, Shade has become a thief of secrets.

Shade's ability, **Masked Theft**, makes your rival discard a card at random every time Shade defeats a champion on your turn: a secret lost, a plan undone. At its fullest, Shade can also take a Relic from a rival champion when attacking, and gains **Flight** to cross the bottomless halls. **Ambush** is always with Shade. You never see Shade until it is already too late.

## In the campaign

Chapter VI · The Court of Night.

- **Trial 1 · Bout 2 — "A Mark Has Been Chosen"** [PROPOSED] The player's strongest champion is marked by Shade before the Bout begins. A puzzle Bout: protect the marked champion for six turns. Survive, and Shade lowers the blade and joins the banner.
- **Trial 2 · Bout 3 — "The Mirror Hall"** [PROPOSED] Shade's deck against the player's, in a hall where one of the rival's face-down cards is a copy of the player's own champion. The lesson: know your own deck better than your rival does.
- **Trial 3 · Bout 1 — "A Hundred Faces"** [PROPOSED] A gauntlet of the old Faces of the guild, each a different rival deck in a single Bout. Shade fights beside the player and does not speak once.
- **Act II hook — "Something Hanging in the Dark"** [PROPOSED] In the lowest halls the player meets a mask with blades drawn. The Bout begins before it says a word. When it ends, the player still isn't sure it was Shade.
