# Thorn — Blighted Bloom
*Verdant · Saboteur · Tier II · Chapter IV · The Greenwall*
> Status: PROPOSED lore (draft for Jerian's approval). Canon quotes are marked [canon].

## Who they are
"Not every forest wants visitors." [canon]

Thorn is a thorned plant warrior with a violet flower for a head and a temper as sharp as his spines. Where Bramble welcomes strangers, Thorn warns them. Where the rest of the Greenwall heals, Thorn stings. He is the part of the forest that remembers every axe, every fire and every careless boot, and he has decided that the best guest is one who never arrives.

## Before the Crownfall
[PROPOSED] The Verdant say "The forest mends its own." [canon] But mending is slow, and the Greenwall has not always been safe. Thorn grew in a dark hollow on the Greenwall's outer edge, where travellers once cut a road through the trees. The road is gone now; the forest took it back. Thorn was what grew in its place: a guardian of violet blooms and long poisoned spines, planted where the forest had been hurt the most. He has guarded that hollow ever since, and nothing has cut a road there again.

## Why they answered the call
[PROPOSED] Thorn did not want to go. When the Greenwall chose to send champions into the Crownfall, he said the forest should keep its roots at home and its gates shut. Then he heard who else was going: Bramble, the smallest guardian, alone. Thorn grumbled, sharpened every spine and followed him. He tells everyone he is in the Arena to make sure no realm ever comes to the Greenwall with axes again. He has never admitted that he came to look after Bramble.

## The Ascension
Thorn's first Ascension came when a rival champion laughed at him. It was a big, loud fighter who said that a flower was no warrior, then reached out to pluck him.

Thorn did not move. He simply waited.

The Crown above felt his anger, slow and deep as old sap, and answered it. Thorn's violet petals widened and darkened, and from their tips came a thick purple poison, dripping onto the Arena floor and hissing where it landed. Along his back, a ridge of new thorns burst out one after another, a spine of hooked points from his neck to the ground.

The big fighter's hand stopped an inch from his petals.

Thorn's flower-head tilted slowly toward the crowd.

"In full bloom." [canon]

The rival struck anyway. Thorn blocked, and the spines did the rest. The fighter staggered back, scorched by poison, and every champion on that side of the field felt a sting in the air. From then on, nobody in the Arena laughed at Thorn. Most of them stopped reaching for him, too.

## The Awakening — the Ascension beyond Ascension
Thorn Awakened in a Bout where Bramble fell.

A rival banner had broken the little guardian's line and was marching on his Sovereign's Shards. Thorn stood over Bramble's fallen form, and every petal on his head turned the colour of a storm.

The Crown answered him with everything the hurt places of the forest had ever felt.

Thorn grew. His stem swelled into a great trunk of twisted green. His flower opened wide, and wider, until it was a carnivorous bloom the size of a gatehouse, ringed with teeth of thorn. From the heart of it came a long, slow exhale: a cloud of violet spores that drifted across the Arena like mist.

Where the spores touched rival champions, they faltered and fell back. Where they touched Bramble, nothing happened at all. Thorn had made sure of that.

When the field was clear, the great bloom folded slowly closed.

"The garden wins." [canon]

Bramble, sitting up, said thank you. Thorn told him not to mention it. Ever.

## Voice and temperament
Thorn is prickly, suspicious and protective. He speaks softly, in short warnings, and his favourite greeting is a threat dressed as an invitation: "Step closer…" [canon] He trusts almost no one, but those few he trusts, he would fight the whole Arena for.

## Bonds and rivalries
- **Bramble** [PROPOSED]: his neighbour, his rival and the one champion he would do anything for. He will deny this if asked.
- **Maeve** [PROPOSED]: the old forest witch has offered Thorn a bargain more than once. He has never accepted. He knows what her bargains cost.
- **Vessimer** [PROPOSED]: the masked chemist of the Hexbound keeps asking for a sample of Thorn's poison. Thorn keeps answering with one very pointed no.

## Beneath the Arena (Set 2)
Beneath the Arena, Thorn's blight finds the cracks in old stone and creeps in. He becomes **Rot Beneath the Stone**, a **Sapper**, crumbling vault walls into green dust. [PROPOSED] Thorn says the vaults remind him of the road through the hollow: something made by others, pushing into places that were not theirs. His **Creeping Rot** breaks a rival's hidden Schemes and Relics, and at its fullest it does both at once, while scorching every rival champion.

## In the campaign
- **Trial 1 · Bout 3: "Step Closer…"** [PROPOSED]: Thorn's introduction. A puzzle Bout where every attack the player makes into Thorn costs them; they must win without touching him.
- **Trial 2 · Bout 1: "Toxic Bloom"** [PROPOSED]: Thorn's deck scorches the whole board on arrival and punishes every Block. The player learns to strike first.
- **Trial 3 · Bout 3: "The Garden Wins"** [PROPOSED]: an Edict makes every Block deal 1 extra Scorch. Thorn guards the player's line, and attacking into it becomes a terrible idea for the rival.
