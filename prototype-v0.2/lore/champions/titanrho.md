# Titan Rho — The Last Fortress
*Gearbound · Warden · Tier IV · Chapter III · The Iron Covenant*
> Status: PROPOSED lore (draft for Jerian's approval). Canon quotes are marked [canon].

## Who they are
"Titan Rho guarded a city for ten thousand years. It does not know the city fell." [canon]

Titan Rho is an enormous ancient war machine that walks on tread-legs, slow as a glacier and just as hard to stop. It was made for one purpose, to keep a city safe, and it has never stopped doing it. In the Arena it still speaks of "the perimeter" and "the city," and it holds the line as if streets and families stand behind it. Nobody argues. Some are grateful.

## Before the Crownfall
[PROPOSED] Titan Rho comes from the oldest corner of the Brasslands, the Gearbound realm where "Machines of every age" [canon] lie half-buried in the hills. Its city was a place of towers and bridges between two rivers, and for ten thousand years Titan Rho turned back every army that came. Then came a silence its sensors could not explain: no attack, no alarm. Titan Rho decided the city was sleeping. It kept guard so the city could rest.

## Why they answered the call
[PROPOSED] When a Sovereign's call reached the empty plain, Titan Rho heard it as a request for reinforcements. It reasoned that a city long without messages must be under siege, and that the place sending the call, the Crownfall, must be the front line. It marched to the Arena to hold that line. It has never asked what the city is doing. Perhaps it fears the answer.

## The Ascension
The Bout had gone on too long. A rival Sovereign's strikers had been battering Titan Rho's line for hours, and the old machine's treads were cracked and smoking. Behind it stood its Sovereign's Crown Shards, and to Titan Rho they looked like the lights of a distant city.

It would not step back. It never had.

Above the Arena, the Crown turned toward the ancient machine, and its light ran down Titan Rho's hull like water. Hatches along its shoulders clanged open. Tall cannon towers unfolded from inside, one on each side, rising like the turrets of a fortress wall. On its head a reactor crown flared to life, a ring of steady gold that hummed louder with every rival blow it absorbed.

Titan Rho ran a slow check of its systems. Everything was older. Everything was stronger.

"Fortress systems expanded." [canon]

The next striker that charged its line was met by both cannon towers at once. The Bout turned in a single moment. Afterwards, Titan Rho turned toward the Shards behind it, counting the lights. All still burned.

## The Awakening — the Ascension beyond Ascension
Titan Rho's Awakening is told as a story of homecoming, though it never went home.

In a Bout where every champion of its banner was falling, the old machine stopped, planted its treads, and spoke the command it had not used in ten thousand years: "Fortress mode." [canon]

The Crown answered with everything it had. Titan Rho's hull widened into walls. Its back rose into battlements and gatehouses, towers and walkways, until it was no longer a machine but a walking citadel. From doors along its sides poured a garrison of tiny brass drones, buzzing and whirring, taking up their posts on the walls as if they had been waiting for this order forever.

Every champion behind the citadel was wrapped in a shield of gold light. Every rival who struck the walls was struck back.

When the Bout ended, the drones lined the battlements and the old fortress stood silent. Then it gave its report, quietly, to a city that was not there.

"Perimeter held." [canon]

## Voice and temperament
Titan Rho is solemn, steady and unshakeably loyal. It speaks in military reports, slowly, with long pauses. Its arrival line says everything about it: "City status: unknown. Defending anyway." [canon] There is sadness under its words, never despair. To Titan Rho, guarding is a kind of love.

## Bonds and rivalries
- **Bolt** [PROPOSED]: Bolt knows Titan Rho's city is gone. It has chosen never to say so. Titan Rho treats the little automaton like a junior officer, and Bolt quietly accepts every order.
- **Torren** [PROPOSED]: the Titanborn shield-bearer sees in Titan Rho the promise he keeps. The two often hold the same line without a word between them.
- **ATLAS-Ω** [PROPOSED]: two war machines of very different ages. Titan Rho guards; ATLAS-Ω waits. When they stand together, other machines fall silent. Neither will say what they know of the other.

## Beneath the Arena (Set 2)
Deep under the Arena, Titan Rho finds a vault gate and seals it with its own body. It becomes the **Vault Bastion**, a **Sentinel**. [PROPOSED] Its **Bastion Protocol** saps the strength of any champion that attacks its Sovereign's Crown, and when it Ascends, every champion of its banner is shielded. To Titan Rho, the vault is simply a new district of the city.
