# Knuckle — Pit King
*Titanborn · Bruiser · Tier III · Chapter III · The Iron Covenant*
> Status: PROPOSED lore (draft for Jerian's approval). Canon quotes are marked [canon].

## Who they are
"Knuckle has never lost a fight in the Underpits. He is here to see if the surface is any harder." [canon]

Knuckle is an enormous stone pit fighter with taped fists, a crooked grin and no patience for anyone who will not fight back. He is certain he is the strongest champion in the Arena, and nobody has yet proved him wrong.

## Before the Crownfall
The Titanborn are "Mountains wearing people" [canon], and deep below Titan's Deep lie the Underpits: fighting rings carved into the bottom of the world. [PROPOSED] The Underpits are where Titanborn settle arguments, test their strength and win a name for themselves. Knuckle arrived there as a young boulder with nothing but his fists and left as the Pit King, with no one left to fight.

## Why they answered the call
The canon reason: to see if the surface is any harder. [PROPOSED] His old rivals said surface fighters were soft. Knuckle is not here for the Crown; he is here for the fight that finally makes him sweat. His Sovereign promised him the hardest Bouts in any realm. He intends to collect.

## The Ascension
Knuckle's first Ascension came in a Bout where a surface fighter finally made him stagger.

She was a swordswoman of the Kingdom, fast and precise, and she struck three times before his fist could land once. Knuckle felt his granite skin crack. He felt his knees bend. For the first time in his life he heard a crowd go quiet in the wrong way.

Then he started laughing.

"Finally," he said.

The Crown answered with gold. It ran into the cracks of his stone skin and filled them, glowing, until he was laced with bright golden seams, as if the mountain inside him had been mended with treasure. Around his waist, heavy belts appeared, shining and battered, every one a title he had won below. Across his shoulder, a new belt with no name yet waited for its first victory.

Knuckle cracked his neck, raised both taped fists and grinned at the crowd.

"Champion of two worlds." [canon]

The swordswoman struck again. This time the blow barely moved him. His answer moved her clean across the Arena. Afterwards, Knuckle helped her up and told her she was the best surface fighter he had met.

## The Awakening — the Ascension beyond Ascension
Knuckle's Awakening came against the hardest wall in the Iron Covenant: a line of shields that would not break, with Titan Rho standing at its heart. Every fist he threw bounced off. His knuckles were bleeding dust.

He stopped, took a long breath, and decided not to throw a punch.

He decided to throw himself.

The Crown's light poured over him, and the ground began to shake. Knuckle grew, rumbling, until he was no longer a fighter but a living landslide, an avalanche of stone with a grin at the top. His fists swelled into great boulders, blunt and heavy as siege rams. He charged.

The shield wall held for one moment, and then it did not. Every defender he struck staggered back, their strength draining from them as the landslide rolled over the line.

When the dust settled, Knuckle stood in the middle of the Arena, breathing hard, golden seams shining. He looked around for his next opponent.

"Next!" [canon]

## Voice and temperament
Knuckle is cocky, cheerful and blunt. He mocks every new opponent, starting with "Surface fighters. Cute." [canon] But he respects anyone who stands up to him, and he never kicks someone who is down. His heart is bigger than his boasting.

## Bonds and rivalries
- **Torren** [PROPOSED]: the one Titanborn Knuckle has never been able to move. Knuckle claims it does not count because Torren never hits back. He keeps trying anyway.
- **Borin Ironbelly** [PROPOSED]: Borin forges Knuckle gauntlets; Knuckle breaks them. It is the friendliest feud in the Deep.
- **Grizz** [PROPOSED]: the great bear of another chapter is called "The Mountain That Walks." Knuckle, an actual mountain, takes that title personally. Their rematches are the loudest Bouts in the Arena, and Grizz usually falls asleep afterwards.

## Beneath the Arena (Set 2)
Below the Arena, Knuckle finds the deepest fighting pit of all, a ring older than the Underpits, and claims it. He becomes the **King Under the Pit**, a **Gladiator**, seated on a throne built from the old, stony bones of a long-fallen Guardian. [PROPOSED] Knuckle did not defeat that Guardian; it fell long before he came. He sits on its throne to show that the pit belongs to him now. His **Pit King's Right** makes him stronger whenever he attacks a champion, and at his fullest, anyone who attacks him loses their strength as they swing.

## In the campaign
- **Trial 1 · Bout 8: "Surface Fighters. Cute."** [PROPOSED]: Knuckle's introduction. A puzzle Bout: survive his huge Attacks by using Shields and Might reductions to make his blows fall short.
- **Trial 2 · Bout 3: "Arena Breaker"** [PROPOSED]: Knuckle's deck of bruisers weakens every defender. The player learns to block with their sturdiest champions and accept trades.
- **Trial 3 · Bout 8: "Next!"** [PROPOSED]: an Edict gives every attacking champion +1 Might. Knuckle fights at the player's side, and the whole Bout becomes a brawl.
