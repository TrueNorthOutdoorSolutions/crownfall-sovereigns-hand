# Pip — Demolitions Enthusiast
*Gearbound · Saboteur · Tier I · Chapter III · The Iron Covenant*
> Status: PROPOSED lore (draft for Jerian's approval). Canon quotes are marked [canon].

## Who they are
"Pip was told the Crownfall was a place of great explosions. Pip was not told correctly, so Pip brought his own." [canon]

Pip is a tiny goblin-like sapper with a satchel of fizzing bombs and no sense of danger at all. He talks about himself by name, laughs at every bang, and treats the Arena as the biggest, best workshop he has ever been allowed into. Most champions see him coming by the smoke. Most are too late anyway.

## Before the Crownfall
The Gearbound are "Machines of every age" [canon], but not every Gearbound is a machine. Some, like Pip, are the small folk who keep the machines running. [PROPOSED] Pip grew up in the Brasslands, a realm of foundries, rail-yards and half-buried engines from wars nobody remembers. His job was to clear old wreckage and make room for new builders. He was very good at it, and even better at making the blasts bigger than anyone asked for.

## Why they answered the call
[PROPOSED] A travelling tinker told Pip that the Crownfall was a floating kingdom where great powers clashed and the sky itself shook. Pip heard only one word: explosions. When a Sovereign's call reached the Brasslands, Pip packed before anyone had finished reading it. He does not want the Crown. He wants to see what happens when you light a fuse where the rules are made of magic.

## The Ascension
Pip's first Ascension came in the middle of a lost Bout. His Sovereign's line had been broken, and a shielded knight was marching on the Crown Shards. Pip had one bomb left. He looked at it. He looked at the knight. He shook his head.

"Too small," he muttered.

The Crown above the Arena turned, slow and bright, and a thread of gold light fell across the little sapper. His satchel split open and filled with something heavier. Brass goggles snapped over his eyes. A bandolier of fat, round bombs wrapped across his chest, and a smoking launcher unfolded on his back with a cheerful clank. Pip sniffed the smoke. He liked it.

He did not think. Pip rarely thinks first. He aimed the launcher at the sky, pulled the cord, and shouted "BIGGER BOOM!" [canon]

The volley arched over the Arena and came down in a ring of flame around the knight's whole company. When the smoke cleared, the Shards still stood. Pip was already reloading, giggling, asking if anyone had more fuses.

## The Awakening — the Ascension beyond Ascension
Pip's Awakening is the story the Iron Covenant tells most often, mostly because nobody can tell it without laughing.

It was the final Bout of a long Trial. Pip's Sovereign was down to a single Shard. The rival line was a wall of iron and stone. Pip stood in front of it, bombs gone, launcher empty, and he looked up at the Crown and asked, very politely, for a ride.

The Crown answered. Gears fell out of the light like rain and assembled around him: tread wheels, a tiny turret, a crooked chimney, and, at the back, a bomb chute with a lever just the right height for a goblin. It was a ridiculous miniature war machine. It was perfect. Pip climbed into the seat and pulled the lever.

Bombs tumbled from the chute in a long, rolling line. The iron wall did not fall all at once. It fell one cheerful explosion at a time. When the last of it toppled, the Arena was silent except for Pip, hanging out of his little machine, waving at the stands.

"Pip wants to go again!" [canon]

## Voice and temperament
Pip is joy with a short fuse. He never sulks and never plans more than one blast ahead. He is not cruel; he simply cannot imagine anyone disliking explosions. "Pip brought explosives!" [canon]

## Bonds and rivalries
- **Professor Spark** [PROPOSED]: the closest thing Pip has to a teacher. Spark gives him parts; Pip gives Spark new reasons to be expelled from places.
- **HEX-3** [PROPOSED]: Pip is fascinated by the careful prototype and keeps asking what happened to prototypes one and two. HEX-3 does not enjoy these questions.
- **Torren** [PROPOSED]: the Titanborn shield-bearer has stood between Pip and his own blasts more than once. Pip calls him "the wall that says please."

## Beneath the Arena (Set 2)
Below the Arena, Pip becomes the **Tunnel Blaster**, a **Sapper** working the narrow mine shafts under the Crownfall. [PROPOSED] The vaults are full of relics the Guardians once held, and Pip has found a new favourite target. His **Tunnel Charge** is built to crack a rival's Relic loose and send it rattling down the stone. Down there, every boom echoes for a very long time. Pip considers this an improvement.

## In the campaign
- **Trial 1 · Bout 2: "Pip Brought His Own"** [PROPOSED]: a puzzle Bout. Pip's opening Scorch must be timed so it clears the rival line without wasting his only charge.
- **Trial 2 · Bout 5: "Special Delivery"** [PROPOSED]: Pip's own deck of cheap, fast Scorch effects tests whether the player can protect a line of low-Guard champions.
- **Trial 3 · Bout 1: "Bigger Boom"** [PROPOSED]: an Edict makes every Scorch deal 1 extra. Pip appears as an ally, and the gauntlet rewards anyone who lets him off the leash.
