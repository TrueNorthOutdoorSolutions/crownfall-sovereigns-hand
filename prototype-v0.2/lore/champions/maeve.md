# Maeve — The Old Bargain
*Verdant · Hexbound · Mystic · Tier III · Chapter IV · The Greenwall*
> Status: PROPOSED lore (draft for Jerian's approval). Canon quotes are marked [canon].

## Who they are
"Maeve always keeps her bargains. That is what makes them frightening." [canon]

Maeve is an ancient forest witch who carries a staff of living wood and a smile that never quite reaches her eyes. She offers help to anyone who asks. She always delivers exactly what she promises. And she always, always collects. She is not wicked. She is fair, the way winter is fair.

## Before the Crownfall
Maeve belongs to two peoples. The Verdant say "The forest mends its own" [canon]; the Hexbound are "Cursed and cursing." [canon] [PROPOSED] Maeve lives where those two worlds meet: deep in the Greenwall, at the place where its roots reach the misty Hexmire. Travellers who get lost there sometimes find her cottage, half-grown into a great tree. She will grant them anything. Then she names her price, always something they did not expect to miss.

## Why they answered the call
[PROPOSED] Maeve says she made a bargain long ago with someone she will not name, and that the Crownfall is where the bargain comes due. Whether she has come to pay a debt or collect one, she does not say. Some champions believe she has come for the Crown itself. Others believe she wants something more interesting: a bargain with the Crown. When asked directly, Maeve only smiles. "Every gift has a price." [canon]

## The Ascension
Maeve's first Ascension came during a bargain.

A wounded young champion of her banner lay on the Arena floor, and Maeve knelt beside him. "I can lift you up," she said softly. "But you will owe me one favour, to be named later." The young champion, frightened, agreed.

Maeve laid her hand on him, and the Crown above leaned in to watch.

Its light ran up her staff of living wood and into her. Branches sprouted from her brow, twisting together with pale bone into a great crown of antlers. Her cloak darkened like bark. And behind her, her shadow stretched across the Arena, no longer the shape of a woman but the shape of a tall, old tree with its branches spread wide.

The young champion rose, whole again, and the rival champion before him staggered as if something had taken its strength.

Maeve stood slowly. She smiled her quiet smile.

"The old bargain holds." [canon]

The young champion never forgot that moment. He also never forgot that he still owed her a favour. Maeve has not named it yet.

## The Awakening — the Ascension beyond Ascension
Maeve Awakened on the night the forest itself came to watch.

It was a Bout under the moon, and her Sovereign was losing. Maeve walked to the center of the Arena, alone, and spoke a few quiet words into the dark. No one heard what she said. Many believe she was making a bargain with the Crown.

Whatever she offered, the Crown accepted.

The edges of the Arena darkened, and the great trees of the Greenwall seemed to lean in through the walls to listen. Moths made of light rose up from the sand, hundreds of them, silver and gold, and began to circle her crown of antlers in slow, glowing rings.

Then the moths flew.

Wherever they touched a rival champion, it was scorched. Wherever they touched an ally, it was shielded. The field turned silver, and then it was quiet.

Maeve stood among the drifting moths and turned to the rival Sovereign with her calm, terrible smile.

"Debts collected." [canon]

## Voice and temperament
Maeve is calm, polite and quietly frightening. She never raises her voice, never breaks a promise and never forgets a debt. She can be kind, but there is always a price.

## Bonds and rivalries
- **Gaia Prime** [PROPOSED]: Maeve has offered Gaia every bargain she knows, in exchange for one memory. Gaia has refused every one, kindly. Maeve finds this the most interesting thing in the Arena.
- **Hexie** [PROPOSED]: the only champion who has ever turned down Maeve's bargain and laughed while doing it. Maeve has taken a quiet interest in the young witch. Hexie does not know whether to be proud or worried.
- **Mother Verdant** [PROPOSED]: two powers of the Verdant who do not entirely trust each other. Mother Verdant offers life freely. Maeve thinks that is a very bad bargain.

## Beneath the Arena (Set 2)
In an underground grove beneath the Arena, Maeve sets up shop as the **Broker of Relics**, a **Rogue**, weighing glowing relics in the roots. [PROPOSED] The vaults are full of treasures, and every treasure has an owner who might be persuaded to part with it. Her **Sleight of Thorns** takes a Relic from a rival champion and attaches it to Maeve, and makes the rival discard a card at random. She calls this "collecting early."

## In the campaign
- **Trial 1 · Bout 4: "Every Gift Has a Price"** [PROPOSED]: Maeve's introduction. A puzzle Bout where she offers the player a powerful card at the start, and the player must win despite the cost it carries.
- **Trial 2 · Bout 5: "Nature's Bargain"** [PROPOSED]: Maeve's deck weakens the player's attackers and shields her own champions. The lesson is to strike where she cannot protect.
- **Trial 3 · Bout 5: "Debts Collected"** [PROPOSED]: an Edict makes every Shield also cost the shielded champion 1 Might. Maeve fights beside the player, and every gift she gives must be weighed.
