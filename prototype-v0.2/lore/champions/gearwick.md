# Gearwick-9 — Siege Calculator
*Gearbound · Artillerist · Tier II · Chapter III · The Iron Covenant*
> Status: PROPOSED lore (draft for Jerian's approval). Canon quotes are marked [canon].

## Who they are
"Gearwick-9 has calculated the odds of winning the Crown. It will not tell you them." [canon]

Gearwick-9 is a clockwork artillery robot built around a brass mortar and a mind that never stops counting. It measures wind, distance, the weight of every shell and the mood of every Sovereign. Ask it about your chances and it will go quiet, then politely calculate something else.

## Before the Crownfall
[PROPOSED] In the Brasslands, the Gearbound realm of foundries and old engines, there was once a long siege that nobody remembers starting. Gearwick-9 was the ninth calculator built to end it. Its eight older siblings each solved part of the problem and were then switched off. Gearwick-9 solved the rest. The siege ended. Gearwick-9 was left on a hill above a quiet city, with no one to report to.

## Why they answered the call
[PROPOSED] Gearwick-9 likes problems that have answers. The Crown is the only problem it has ever found that refuses to have one. Every time it runs the numbers on the Crownfall, the result changes. A Sovereign's call offered it a chance to measure the Crown from up close, and Gearwick-9 has been gathering data ever since. Whether it wants the Crown, or only wants to understand it, is one more number it keeps to itself.

## The Ascension
Gearwick-9's first Ascension came during a Bout against a long line of shielded champions. Its shells kept landing a hand's width short. The calculator had checked its sums four times. They were right. The shots were still wrong.

Gearwick-9 lowered its mortar, clicked once, and asked the Crown a question it could not answer itself.

The Crown answered by changing the machine. A second barrel swung up and locked into place beside the first, gleaming new brass. Over its single lens, a targeting monocle clicked down. It whirred, focused, and clicked again, and suddenly Gearwick-9 could see things it had never been able to measure: the drift of Arena wind, the faint pulse of each champion's guard, the exact moment a shield would flicker.

The calculator ran its sums once more. This time the answer came out clean.

"Additional barrels installed." [canon]

Both barrels fired. The shells arced high over the field and landed together, exactly where they were meant to, scorching every champion in the rival line. Gearwick-9 did not smile. Its monocle clicked twice, which is close.

## The Awakening — the Ascension beyond Ascension
The Awakening came on the last day of a long Trial, when Gearwick-9's Sovereign faced a rival line too wide for any mortar to cover. Gearwick-9 calculated the odds of victory. For the first time, it said them out loud. They were very bad.

Then it stood up.

The Crown's light climbed through its frame and the machine began to grow. Legs became pillars. Its body stretched upward floor by floor until Gearwick-9 was a walking siege tower, ladders and hatches clattering along its sides. At the very top, a crown of mortars began to turn, a rotary ring of barrels, each one loading as the next one fired.

The tower walked forward. The ring spun. Shells fell like a steady, perfect rain across every rival champion at once, each landing on its calculated mark. The wide line broke and did not recover.

When the smoke settled, Gearwick-9 recalculated, paused, and gave its report.

"Odds improved." [canon]

## Voice and temperament
Gearwick-9 is precise, patient and dryly secretive. Its speech is a stream of measurements. It opens every Bout with "Calculating trajectory." [canon] It cares about the right answer more than the comfortable one, and knows some answers are kinder left unsaid.

## Bonds and rivalries
- **ATLAS-Ω** [PROPOSED]: Gearwick-9 has tried to calculate ATLAS-Ω many times. The numbers always stop at the same place, as if part of the war machine's history has been removed. Gearwick-9 has not asked why. It is not sure it wants the answer.
- **Professor Spark** [PROPOSED]: Spark is the only engineer who has ever asked Gearwick-9 for its odds and then ignored them. Gearwick-9 finds this maddening, and secretly useful.
- **HEX-3** [PROPOSED]: two careful machines who trust each other's caution. They share targeting data in long, silent clicks.

## Beneath the Arena (Set 2)
Below the Arena, Gearwick-9 sets up in an underground workshop as the **Relic Engineer**, an **Artificer**. [PROPOSED] The vaults are full of recovered relics, and every one is a new variable. Its **Relic Rig** bolts those relics straight into its siege frame, making them cheaper for its banner and making Gearwick-9 hit harder while it holds one. At its fullest, it can even recover a lost relic from the Fallen pile. Gearwick-9 calls this "reusing good data."

## In the campaign
- **Trial 1 · Bout 3: "It Will Not Tell You"** [PROPOSED]: a puzzle Bout where the player must win with exact damage. Gearwick-9's Reach lets it strike the one rival hiding behind a Bulwark.
- **Trial 2 · Bout 6: "Mortar Protocol"** [PROPOSED]: Gearwick-9's deck rains Scorch across the board every Attack, punishing wide, low-Guard lines.
- **Trial 3 · Bout 5: "Odds Improved"** [PROPOSED]: an Edict makes Reach champions strike first. Gearwick-9 joins the player's banner for the final run before the Crowning.
