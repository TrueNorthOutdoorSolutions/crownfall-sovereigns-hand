# Veilwalker — The Anomaly
*Riftborn · Assassin · Tier IV · Chapter VII · Storm and Rift*

> Status: PROPOSED lore (draft for Jerian's approval). Canon quotes are marked [canon].

## Who they are

"The Veilwalker is what happens when a door between worlds learns to walk." [canon]

The Veilwalker is not quite a person. It is a living tear in the world, wearing a tattered shape so that others have something to look at. It has no face. It seldom speaks. Where it goes, the air opens, and things on one side end up on the other.

## Before the Crownfall

PROPOSED: No one knows where the Veilwalker began, including the Veilwalker. The Riftborn tell a story about a door that stood between two worlds for so long that it began to wonder what was on either side. It opened itself one day to look, and found it could not close again. So it went for a walk.

Since then it has wandered through every reality it can find. It simply arrives, watches and leaves.

## Why they answered the call

PROPOSED: Nobody is sure the Veilwalker answered a call at all. One Sovereign swears it simply stepped out of the air beside them during the Arena's opening ceremony and stayed.

The Riftborn think that, to a door, the Crownfall must feel like the biggest doorway of all. Perhaps the Veilwalker came to find out what it opens onto.

## The Ascension

PROPOSED: A rival Sovereign had built a wall. Champions with heavy shields stood shoulder to shoulder, and behind them the rival's Shards glittered, untouchable.

The Veilwalker stood in front of the wall and tilted its tattered head.

Then its edges began to come apart. Not breaking: opening. At the rim of its shape the Arena faded, and other skies showed through. A night sky full of strange stars. A sea under a green sun. A city upside down.

The shield-wall champions stepped back. One of them dropped a shield, and it fell into the edge of the Veilwalker and did not land.

More of the Veilwalker frayed into somewhere else. The space around it grew thin as paper. For a moment, the Arena seemed to be missing a piece, and the missing piece had a shape.

"* more of the world goes missing *" [canon]

Then it was gone from in front of the wall, and the rival's Shards were already falling behind it.

## The Awakening — the Ascension beyond Ascension

PROPOSED: Its Sovereign later said the Veilwalker had gone very still before it happened, as if listening to something far away.

The rival Sovereign had sent its best champion forward: a hunter who could see through every trick. The hunter raised a spear, aimed at the tattered shape, and threw.

The spear went into the Veilwalker and did not come out.

Because there was no longer a shape. There was only a hole in the world, standing upright in the middle of the Arena, the edges calm and dark. Through it the crowd could see a hundred other places, all at once. And in the middle of the hole, held by nothing at all, was a blade.

The voice that came out of it was the first anyone had ever heard it use.

"Between here — and there." [canon]

The hunter fell. Several weaker rival champions vanished from the field at the same moment and reappeared, dazed, in the stands.

Then the hole folded quietly in on itself.

"* closes *" [canon]

## Voice and temperament

Silent, patient and strange. When it speaks, it does so through what happens around it. It is never cruel.

- "* the air tears open *" [canon]
- "Between here — and there." [canon]

## Bonds and rivalries

- **Veyra Sol** (PROPOSED): Veyra hunts things that escape between worlds. The Veilwalker is the most famous of all such things. She has never hunted it. Some say she cannot; others say she has decided not to. The Veilwalker follows her around the Arena, at a distance, like a cat.
- **Captain Blacktide** (PROPOSED): Blacktide considers it the worst toll-dodger in existence. He sent it a bill once. The bill vanished.
- **Zyrak** (PROPOSED): Zyrak shines questions at the Veilwalker. Sometimes, colours from far-off skies flicker back through its edges. Zyrak counts this as conversation.

## Beneath the Arena (Set 2)

**The Rift Below · Rogue.** Beneath the Arena, the Veilwalker finds that the deeper it goes, the thinner the world becomes. Its Set 2 card shows it stepping out of a tear in the cavern air behind a Guardian.

It becomes a rogue below, a thief of secrets. Its ability, **Between the Veils**, makes your rival discard a card at random whenever it attacks: something from their hand slips through a tear and is gone. Awakened, it also takes a Relic from a rival champion and keeps it on the far side of the veil.

Where those lost things end up, no one knows. The Veilwalker is not telling.

## In the campaign

- **Trial 1 · Bout 8 — "Something in the Doorway."** The Veilwalker's introduction. It appears unannounced mid-Bout on your side of the field, with Ambush. Win, and it is simply there in your collection afterwards.
- **Trial 2 · Bout 7 — "The Missing Piece."** It tests your deck with a Flight-and-Ambush deck that strikes where you are weakest.
- **Trial 3 · Bout 7 — "Many Skies."** The last gauntlet Bout before the Crowning, under a proposed Edict, *Thin Places*: every champion has Flight. Afterwards, the Veilwalker turns to face the dark at the edge of the Arena, where Veyra Sol is waiting.
