# HEX-3 — Arcane Prototype
*Gearbound · Arcanist · Tier II · Chapter III · The Iron Covenant*
> Status: PROPOSED lore (draft for Jerian's approval). Canon quotes are marked [canon].

## Who they are
"Prototypes one and two exploded. HEX-3 is careful. HEX-3 is very, very careful." [canon]

HEX-3 is an experimental robot with a glass chamber full of raw mana where its heart should be. It checks every step and watches its own reactor like a cliff edge. And yet, when HEX-3 finally lets its power go, it hits with a force that makes other champions wonder why the first two prototypes did not simply win.

## Before the Crownfall
[PROPOSED] HEX-3 was built in the Brasslands, the Gearbound realm of foundries and buried engines, by a workshop that wanted to join magic and machinery. The idea was simple: catch mana in glass, the way an engine catches steam. Prototype one lasted a day. Prototype two lasted a week. HEX-3 has lasted years, mostly by being frightened of itself.

## Why they answered the call
[PROPOSED] HEX-3 has one question it cannot answer: is it stable, or only lucky? The Crown, it has heard, raises champions into more of themselves. If HEX-3 can survive an Ascension, then the power in its chamber truly belongs to it, and it will never have to be afraid again. If it cannot, it would rather find out in the Arena than in an empty workshop. It answered a Sovereign's call with a short, honest report: "Reactor stable. Mostly." [canon]

## The Ascension
HEX-3 had been holding back for an entire Trial. Every Bout, it released only a trickle of power, enough to sting the defender and no more. Its Sovereign never complained. HEX-3 complained enough for both of them.

Then the rival line surrounded it, and the careful prototype ran out of careful choices.

It opened its chamber. The Crown, high above, answered at once, and HEX-3 felt the glass around its heart flood with light. It braced for the explosion. The explosion did not come. Instead, glowing runes spread across its plating like frost on a window, locking every seam, binding every bolt. The mana inside its chamber turned from a nervous blue to a deep, steady violet.

HEX-3 measured the new readings three times. They were higher. They were also calm.

"Core output increased." [canon]

A ring of violet force rolled outward from its chest, scorching every rival champion around it. HEX-3 stood in the smoke, checking itself over and over, unable to believe it was whole.

## The Awakening — the Ascension beyond Ascension
HEX-3's Awakening came in a Bout where its Sovereign had nothing left to hold back. HEX-3 understood this. For once, it did not check its readings first.

It let go.

The Crown's light lifted the prototype off the ground. Its legs folded away, no longer needed, and HEX-3 rose to hover above the Arena floor. The single glass chamber in its chest split into three, and the three cores slipped free, orbiting the machine like small violet moons. Each one hummed with a different note. Together they sang a chord the whole Arena could feel.

It had become a levitating arcane engine, and every one of its cores was steady.

HEX-3 turned to the strongest rival champion and released a beam of pure violet force. When it faded, the rival had fallen, and HEX-3 was still floating, still whole, still humming.

There was a long pause while it checked itself one last time.

"Did not explode." [canon]

## Voice and temperament
HEX-3 is anxious, honest and quietly brave. It speaks in technical status reports, always with a small warning attached. It worries about everything, including worrying too much. Beneath the caution is a deep wish to prove that being careful is not the same as being weak.

## Bonds and rivalries
- **Pip** [PROPOSED]: Pip is everything HEX-3 fears, a happy explosion on two legs. Pip keeps asking about prototypes one and two. HEX-3 keeps changing the subject.
- **Professor Spark** [PROPOSED]: Spark once offered to "improve" HEX-3's reactor. HEX-3 still has nightmares about it. They are, despite this, good friends.
- **Hexie** [PROPOSED]: the young witch from the Greenwall is fascinated by a machine that carries magic in a jar. HEX-3 has asked her, many times, to stop tapping the glass.

## Beneath the Arena (Set 2)
In a cable-strung vault under the Arena, HEX-3 becomes the **Core Conduit**, a **Channeler**. [PROPOSED] The deep places of the Crownfall are thick with old power, and HEX-3 has learned to draw it in safely through cables strung from wall to wall. Its **Core Surge** shields it every time its banner casts a Tactic, and at its fullest, that surge spills out to scorch the rival line. HEX-3 calls this "controlled release." It is still very, very careful.

## In the campaign
- **Trial 1 · Bout 4: "Reactor Stable. Mostly."** [PROPOSED]: a puzzle Bout where HEX-3's Attack must be used at the perfect moment to finish the defender without exposing itself.
- **Trial 2 · Bout 7: "Very, Very Careful"** [PROPOSED]: HEX-3's own deck strikes hard at single defenders. The player learns to spread out their threats.
- **Trial 3 · Bout 3: "Core Output Increased"** [PROPOSED]: an Edict raises every Scorch by 1. HEX-3 fights at the player's side, and the danger of explosion is part of the fun.
