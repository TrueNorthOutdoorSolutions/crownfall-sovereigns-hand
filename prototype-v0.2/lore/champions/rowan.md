# Rowan — Longbow of the Realm
*Kingdom · Ranger · Tier I · Chapter I · The First Light*

> Status: PROPOSED lore (draft for Jerian's approval). Canon quotes are marked [canon].

## Who they are

"Rowan has never missed at a hundred paces. At a thousand, he says, the wind argues." [canon]

Rowan is a kingdom archer with a yew longbow and a calm, easy smile. He is the kind of person who talks to the weather as if it were an old friend who sometimes owes him money.

He is not chasing glory. He likes the Arena because it is the widest open space he has ever seen, and he likes the Crown because it is the farthest target he has ever had.

## Before the Crownfall

Rowan grew up in the Kingdom realm, on the edge of the great royal forest where the king's foresters keep the deer and the roads safe. His grandfather taught him to shoot with a bow taller than he was. By sixteen he was a royal forester himself, wearing the green tabard and walking the long forest roads.

Life was quiet and good. He guarded travellers, chased off wolves, and won the summer archery fair so often they asked him, politely, to stop entering.

## Why they answered the call

The Kingdom sent champions to the Crownfall, and Rowan was not on the list. He went anyway, as an escort for those who were.

He answered a Sovereign's call partly out of duty to the realm and partly out of plain curiosity. He wants to know how far he can really shoot, and what the wind says up there.

## The Ascension

It happened on a bright, gusty afternoon. Rowan's banner was pinned down, and the rival's strongest champion stood at the far end of the Arena, well beyond any normal range. Everyone told him the shot was impossible. The wind was howling across the sand.

Rowan licked a finger, held it up, and listened.

For the first time in his life, the wind went quiet. It was not still; it was waiting.

His plain kit shimmered and became a royal green tabard, crisp as new leaves. His yew bow grew silver limbs that caught the light like moonlit water. He drew, held, and smiled.

"A thousand paces, now." [canon]

The arrow flew straight across the whole Arena and struck true. The rival champion fell. The crowd was silent for a moment, then loud. Rowan only nodded and thanked the wind under his breath. He did not know whether the Crown had helped him or simply let him be what he already was. He has decided not to mind.

## The Awakening — the Ascension beyond Ascension

Later in the season, Rowan's Sovereign faced a rival with flying champions, high above the reach of any ordinary archer. Rowan was out of arrows, his quiver empty, his friends below him holding a shrinking line.

A falcon landed on his shoulder. He had never seen it before. It looked at him as if it had known him for years.

He drew his empty bow, and an arrow of light formed on the string. A cloak of gold settled around his shoulders, heavy and warm, shaped like a lion's mane. The falcon screamed and took off ahead of the shot, showing him the way.

Arrow after arrow of light rose into the sky, each one finding its mark. When the last flyer fell, Rowan lowered his bow. The falcon came back to his arm and stayed.

"Clean shots." [canon]

He named the falcon Breeze. He says she argues less than the wind.

## Voice and temperament

Rowan is relaxed, friendly and dryly funny. He speaks slowly and never wastes words. His greeting is "The wind is kind today." [canon] The one thing he does not joke about is a shot taken carelessly.

## Bonds and rivalries

- **Kael** — Rowan and Kael are both humble Kingdom champions, and they became friends in their first week. Rowan thinks Kael takes everything too seriously. He follows him anyway, because someone should watch his back.
- **General Varr** — Varr wants archers in rows. Rowan prefers to wander off and find a better angle. They argue every bout. Varr has never once said Rowan was wrong afterwards.
- **Kira Volt** — A storm champion who can call the wind and lightning. Rowan finds her fascinating and slightly unfair. They have a standing contest about who can hit the farthest target.

## Beneath the Arena (Set 2)

Beneath the Arena, there is no wind. Rowan felt it the moment the banners went down the great stair. The air was still and old, and for the first time his arrows had nothing to talk to.

He stayed anyway. Below, he became the **Archer of the Deep Gate**, a Sharpshooter who holds the great gate to the under-halls with his bow drawn into the dark. His ability, **Gatewatch Volley**, sends steady arrows into whatever comes through, every time he attacks.

He has started talking to the silence instead. He swears it is starting to answer.

## In the campaign

- **Trial 1 · Bout 5 — "A Hundred Paces":** A tutorial Bout teaching Reach. The rival plays flying champions, and only Rowan can block them. Keep him alive to win.
- **Trial 2 · Bout 4 — "The Longbow's Test":** Rowan tests the player with a Kingdom ranger deck full of Reach and small Scorch effects. He picks off weak champions every turn, so the player must play sturdy ones.
- **Trial 3 · Bout 2 — "The Wind Argues" (Edict in play):** The Edict gives each champion with Reach −1 Might, but +1 Might for every turn it has stayed on the field.
