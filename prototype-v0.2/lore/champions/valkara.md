# Valkara — Wing of Judgment
*Astral · Duelist · Tier III · Chapter VIII · The One Who Refused*

> Status: PROPOSED lore (draft for Jerian's approval). Canon quotes are marked [canon].

## Who they are

"Valkara judges. The verdict is usually "from above"." [canon]

Valkara is a winged celestial warrior with a spear-sword and a very firm sense of right and wrong. She is stern, honest and fair, and she has a dry sense of humour that she believes nobody notices. Everybody notices.

## Before the Crownfall

PROPOSED: In the Astral realm, disputes between the great star-houses are brought before the Wings, celestial judges who hear both sides and decide. Valkara was the youngest Wing in a thousand years. She listened carefully, ruled fairly and, when one side refused to accept the ruling, delivered the verdict herself, at speed, from a great height.

She never once judged unfairly. That is not the same as never once judging wrong, and Valkara knows the difference better than anyone. There is one case she has never forgotten, a verdict she has turned over in her mind ever since, but she will not speak about it.

## Why they answered the call

PROPOSED: Valkara came to the Crownfall to judge the Crown.

She has heard every story about it. The sunrise stolen. The dragons who died for it. The one who won it and walked away. Valkara wants to hear the evidence for herself. If the Crown is worthy, she will fight for whichever Sovereign it accepts. If it is not, she will say so, to its face, from above.

More than anything she wants to understand Crownless. A champion who won and refused is a case she cannot close.

## The Ascension

PROPOSED: The Bout came down to a single rival champion, and that champion had cheated. Everyone saw it: a hidden blade, a broken rule, a Shard taken while its guard was turned away.

Valkara's Sovereign looked up at her. The crowd looked up at her.

Valkara spread her wings and climbed. High above the Arena, she could see every move both sides had made, laid out below like the pages of a case.

She weighed them carefully, the way she had weighed every case she had ever heard. Then she decided.

Starlight gathered under her wings and became her wings, broad and shining. A gilded war-helm settled over her brow. She folded her new wings and fell.

"My wings are sharper." [canon]

She struck the cheating champion with a single, perfect blow. Then she landed beside them, offered them her hand and helped them to their feet.

"Next time," she said, "play fair."

## The Awakening — the Ascension beyond Ascension

PROPOSED: Valkara Awakened when she judged herself.

It was a Bout she should have won, and lost because of her own mistake. She had ruled too quickly. Her Sovereign's champions had paid for it.

That night she climbed above the Crownfall, higher than she had ever flown, up toward the slow-turning shards of the Crown. She hung there in the dark and heard her own case, honestly, the way she would hear anyone's.

The verdict was fair. She accepted it.

When the next Bout came, she was different. Six wings unfolded from her back, white and gold. A halo of blades turned slowly behind her head, each one a verdict she had given and stood by.

She dived. The sky split open behind her like torn silk, and the dive ended in a single bright crash in the middle of the rival line.

When the dust settled, she rose and lowered her spear-sword.

"The verdict stands." [canon]

## Voice and temperament

Formal, fair and quietly funny. She never lies and she never judges without listening.

- "Judgment comes from above." [canon]
- "The verdict stands." [canon]

## Bonds and rivalries

- **Elysian** (PROPOSED): Valkara's oldest friend and fiercest argument. Elysian believes no one deserves to fall in the Crownfall. Valkara believes some do, after a fair hearing. They have debated this for centuries and expect to debate it for centuries more.
- **Lady Volt** (PROPOSED): Two champions who strike from nowhere. They have agreed never to test which is faster, because neither could bear to lose.
- **Crownless** (PROPOSED): The one case she cannot close. She has asked Crownless to explain the refusal. Crownless has not explained. Valkara has not judged. Both of them seem to be waiting.

## Beneath the Arena (Set 2)

**Judge of the Depths · Seer.** Beneath the Arena there are monsters that have never faced a judge. Valkara intends to change that. Her Set 2 card shows her diving from a cavern's darkness, wings blazing, to pass judgment on a monster.

Below, she becomes a seer, judging before the crime. Her ability, **Foresight Verdict**, makes your Schemes cost less to spring. When she Ascends, she defeats each rival champion with low Guard; Awakened, her verdict reaches further still.

"In the dark," she says, "you cannot see your enemy coming. So you must see them first."

## In the campaign

- **Trial 1 · Bout 2 — "Fair Hearing."** Valkara's introduction. A puzzle Bout: your rival has broken an Edict, and you must win without breaking it yourself. Valkara joins your banner, satisfied.
- **Trial 2 · Bout 2 — "From Above."** Valkara tests your deck with a Flight deck that Scorches on Ascension.
- **Trial 3 · Bout 2 — "The Case She Cannot Close."** A gauntlet Bout under a proposed Edict, *The Court Is in Session*: each player may only attack once per turn. Afterwards Valkara looks toward the dark at the end of the chapter, where Crownless waits, and says nothing.
