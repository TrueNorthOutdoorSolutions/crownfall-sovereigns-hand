# Solenne Ash — Knight of the Burning Dawn
*Dawnforged · Vanguard · Tier II · Chapter I · The First Light*

> Status: PROPOSED lore (draft for Jerian's approval). Canon quotes are marked [canon].

## Who they are

"Solenne swore to burn away every shadow in the Crownfall. She has started a list." [canon]

Solenne Ash is a flame knight in rose-gold plate, and she is exactly as direct as she looks. She keeps her list in a small, battered notebook.

She charges first and asks questions while she is charging.

## Before the Crownfall

Solenne was born in the Dawnforged realm, in a border fort on the western cliffs where the sun sets and the night comes in from the sea. Her family were Dawn Wardens, who light the beacon fires there every dusk.

She was the youngest Warden ever to stand a night watch alone. On that watch, something came out of the dark that the beacon could not frighten away. Solenne held the cliff until morning. She never said what it was. The next day, she bought the notebook.

## Why they answered the call

The Crownfall draws champions from every realm and age, and with them come shadows from every realm and age. To Solenne, the Arena looks like one enormous watchtower with every darkness in the world gathered under it. She answered a Sovereign's call because it was the quickest way inside.

## The Ascension

It was a night bout, which the Arena allows only rarely. Torches ringed the sand. Solenne's Sovereign had put her in front, as always, and the rival had sent something made of shadow and teeth straight at her line.

She raised her blade. The torches guttered. For a moment, every light in the Arena went out, and Solenne stood in perfect dark, exactly as she had on the western cliff years ago.

She was not afraid this time. She was annoyed.

Her plume caught fire first, a crest of real flame streaming back from her helm. Then her blade went white, so hot the sand beneath it turned to glass. The darkness flinched.

"The dawn burns brighter!" [canon]

She charged before the torches relit, and they relit because of her. When it was over, she took out her notebook in the glow of her own sword and crossed off one line. Her hands were shaking. She told herself it was from the heat.

## The Awakening — the Ascension beyond Ascension

The second time the Crown raised her, there was no darkness at all. It was noon, the hardest light of the day, and her own line was breaking. Friends were falling. There was no shadow to burn, only a rival who was simply stronger.

She understood then that she had been fighting the wrong thing. The dawn is not only what drives the night away. It is what makes people stand up in the morning and try again.

Her plate rang like a forge. It became sun-forged armour, gold and rose and white. Blazing wings opened behind her, and when she moved, a trail of dawnfire followed her across the sand, warming everyone it passed. Her friends rose. Her charge carried all of them.

When the rival's last champion fell, she lowered her blade and said one word.

"Cleansed." [canon]

That night she opened her notebook and, for the first time, added something to a new page titled *Things worth protecting*.

## Voice and temperament

Solenne is blunt, loyal and impatient. She speaks in short, hard sentences and hates waiting. She has a dry sense of humour that most people miss. Her battle greeting is "Shadows, beware." [canon]

## Bonds and rivalries

- **Solara** — The young Dawnforged novice follows Solenne around, asking to see her list. Solenne pretends this is irritating. She has quietly added "anything that hurts Solara" to the top of page one.
- **Nyx** — A name on the list. Solenne has fought Nyx three times and lost the trail each time.
- **General Varr** — Varr taught Solenne that a charge is only as good as the line behind it. She respects him more than almost anyone, and argues with him constantly.

## Beneath the Arena (Set 2)

When the banners went down beneath the Arena, Solenne was the first to volunteer. The deep places were the darkest she had ever seen, which meant they needed her most.

Below, she became the **Lantern of the Underways**, a Delver whose blade lights the tunnels so that others can follow. Her ability, **Lantern Path**, shows the way forward: every time she arrives or rises, her banner learns a little more about the dark ahead, and at her brightest she shields everyone walking behind her.

Her list has grown very long. Some of the new entries have no names, only drawings.

## In the campaign

- **Trial 1 · Bout 2 — "Night Watch":** A puzzle Bout. The rival has only shadow-type champions with Ambush. The player is given Solenne and must use Charge to strike before the ambushers do. Teaches Charge.
- **Trial 2 · Bout 3 — "The List":** Solenne tests the player with an aggressive Dawnforged deck full of Charge and Scorch.
- **Trial 3 · Bout 1 — "Burn the Dark" (Edict in play):** An Edict prevents Shields this Bout. Solenne fights beside the player as an ally card handed out at the start, and the player must win before the rival's numbers wear her down.
