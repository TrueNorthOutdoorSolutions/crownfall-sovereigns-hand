# Crownless — The One Who Refused
*Crownless · Ascendant · Tier V · Chapter VIII · The One Who Refused (the Crowning, Act I finale)*

> Status: PROPOSED lore (draft for Jerian's approval). Canon quotes are marked [canon].

## Who they are

"Once, someone won the Crownfall. They refused the Crown. Nobody has explained why." [canon]

Crownless is a figure in broken regalia whose face is always hidden. He is the only champion of his own kind: no realm claims him, and his origin is simply his name. He says almost nothing. When a Sovereign calls him, his whole answer is a silence.

"…" [canon]

He is one of the eight legends the Crown has raised highest, and the last Crowning of Act I.

## Before the Crownfall

PROPOSED: Nobody knows who Crownless was before. The stories disagree, and he corrects none of them.

Some say he was a king of a realm that no longer exists. Some say he was a nobody, a stable-hand or a farmer's child, who fought through the whole Crownfall on stubbornness alone. The regalia he wears is broken, but it is real regalia. Whose it was, he does not say.

What every story agrees on is the ending. Long ago, someone won the Crownfall, and the Crown was theirs to take. They turned it down. The stories say that someone was Crownless.

## Why they answered the call

Nobody has explained why. [canon]

That is the truth, and the stories keep it. Champions in the Crownfall have theories, and they argue about them often:

- Valkara thinks there must have been a reason that would survive a fair hearing. She cannot decide what it was.
- Astryon thinks no one who could win would ever walk away, so something must have been wrong with the Crown. Or with the winning.
- Elysian thinks Crownless was protecting someone.
- Veyra Sol says nothing, which some take to mean she knows. She may not.

Crownless has listened to every one of these theories. He has agreed with none and denied none. He still answers a Sovereign's call, still fights in the Arena, still walks to the end of the Trials. He still does not take the Crown.

## The Ascension

PROPOSED: The rival Sovereign had built a perfect board. Every champion stood where the rules said it was strongest. Every Edict favoured them. By any rule of the Arena, the Bout was over.

Crownless walked out to meet them anyway, broken regalia catching the light.

A rival champion struck him across the chest. Where the blow landed, his armour cracked, and through the crack came light: a thin bright line of Crown-light, gold and white, the same light that hangs above the Arena.

Then another crack. And another. The light spread through his armour like frost across a window, and where it ran, he grew stronger.

The rival Sovereign's champions stepped back. In the stands, people were standing up.

Ascension is supposed to cost a champion time. Crownless did not seem to have heard.

"The rules bend." [canon]

He was ready again before the crowd had finished gasping. The perfect board did not stay perfect for long.

## The Awakening — the Ascension beyond Ascension

PROPOSED: It is said that Crownless Awakened only once in front of a crowd. Those who were there disagree about what they saw.

The Bout was the final one of a long Trial. The Arena had piled Edict upon Edict against him, rule after rule after rule, until it seemed the Crownfall itself did not want him to win.

Crownless stood still in the middle of the field. Then the cracks in his armour began to widen, and the light inside grew so bright that his armour was no longer armour. It was the Crown's own power, standing in the shape of a person, wearing broken regalia like an old coat.

Around him, the Arena began to fracture. The stones split in lines of light. The air cracked like ice. The Edicts hung above the field in glowing script, and one by one, they broke.

He raised his hand.

"I refuse your rules." [canon]

When it was over, the rival Sovereign's banner was down. High above, the Crown's shards had stopped turning, all at once, as if watching. Crownless looked up at them for a long moment, and then he looked away.

"Still not taking it." [canon]

## Voice and temperament

Almost silent. When he speaks, it is short, calm and final. He is never cruel and never cheerful.

- "…" [canon]
- "The rules bend." [canon]
- "Still not taking it." [canon]

## Bonds and rivalries

- **Valkara** (PROPOSED): The case she cannot close. She has asked him to explain himself. He has not. She has not judged. Both of them seem to be waiting for something.
- **Astryon** (PROPOSED): Astryon has asked him to explain, and then to duel. Crownless declined both.
- **Veyra Sol** (PROPOSED): She watches him like quarry. He does not seem to mind. They have spoken once, alone. Neither will say what about.
- **Elysian** (PROPOSED): She offered to heal the cracks in his regalia. He said nothing, and the cracks remained.

## Beneath the Arena (Set 2)

**The Unbound · Ascendant.** In Act II, Crownless goes down further than anyone. His Set 2 card shows him standing in an empty vault deep beneath the Arena, the place where, the card says, the Crown once rested, refusing it still. What the vault was, when the Crown rested there and why it is empty now are left as mysteries. Crownless knows where it is. He has not said how.

His ability, **UNBOUND**, weakens every rival champion's Might the moment he arrives. Ascended, he readies at once, as he always has. Awakened, he lends his strength to every champion beside him, and Scorches the defender when he strikes.

## In the campaign: the Crowning of Act I

Chapter VIII is named after him, and every Bout in it leads toward him. After Elysian's last Bout, the Arena goes quiet. The announcer does not speak. A single figure in broken regalia walks to the centre of the field and waits.

- **The Crowning: Crownless (Contender).** The finale of Act I. Crownless plays his standard printing with a deck built to break the player's expectations: champions that Ascend and ready at once, and Ambush where the rules say no attack should come. Win, and the Crown's shards stop turning for a moment. Crownless looks at the player's Sovereign, and then up at the Crown, and says "Still not taking it." [canon]. Act I ends there. He cannot be challenged again until Act II is complete.
- **Masterwork Crowning.** Reopens after Act II. Crownless plays his **Masterwork** printing (from Set 1) with a stronger deck and a proposed Edict, *The Rules Bend*: once per turn, his first champion to Ascend ignores one rule of the player's choosing.
- **Omega Crowning.** Crownless plays his black-and-gold **Omega** printing with his hardest deck and a proposed Edict, *I Refuse Your Rules*: all Edicts in play are removed at the start of his turn, and none can be added. It is the hardest fight in Act I. Beating it grants the top prestige reward (a title and card back, wording to be approved). What, if anything, Crownless says after this defeat is deliberately left unwritten: it must never explain the refusal.
