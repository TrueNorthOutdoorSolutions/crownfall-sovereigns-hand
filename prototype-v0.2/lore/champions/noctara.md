# Noctara — Empress of Nothing
*Umbral · Ascendant / Arcanist · Tier V · Chapter VI · The Court of Night (the Crowning)*

> Status: PROPOSED lore (draft for Jerian's approval). Canon quotes are marked [canon].

## Who they are

"Noctara ruled an empire until it disappeared. She is not sure she did not do it." [canon]

Noctara is an empress robed in the space between stars. Where light should fall on her gown, it falls *through*, into a deep and endless dark scattered with tiny pinpricks of distant light. She is calm, still, and utterly sure of herself in every way but one.

She does not know what happened to her empire.

[MYSTERY] What happened, what the Nothing is, and whether Noctara caused it are not answered here, and must not be. She does not know. The Arena does not say. Champions argue about it. That is all.

## Before the Crownfall

[PROPOSED] Once there was an empire in the Umbral realm, the greatest it had ever known. It had cities of black glass and towers that reached the stars, and its empress was Noctara.

[PROPOSED] Then, one night, it was gone. Not conquered. Not burned. Not ruined. *Gone*, as if it had never been, and in its place lay a vast, quiet emptiness that the Umbral folk call the Nothing. Noctara stood alone at the edge of it, in her robe of starless night, and could not remember what had happened in the moment before.

[PROPOSED] The stories disagree. Some say her empire was swallowed by something from outside the realms, and Noctara alone survived. Some say she reached for a power too large and it took everything she loved. Some say Dreadmouth ate it, which Noctara has never confirmed or denied. Some say she simply made a wish she should not have. Queen Vespera, who was there, says nothing at all.

[PROPOSED] Noctara has spent every day since trying to answer one question: *did I do it?* She is not sure she wants to know.

## Why they answered the call

[PROPOSED] Noctara has never said why she came to the Crownfall. Champions guess.

[PROPOSED] Some think she wants the Crown to bring her empire back. Some think she wants the Crown to tell her the truth of what she did. Some think she wants the Crown itself, because a power that seems to watch and choose and defend itself is the only thing in all the realms that might be as large as the Nothing. And a few, quietly, think she has come to make sure no one else ever makes the same mistake she did, whatever it was.

[PROPOSED] Her own words do not help: "All things end in me." [canon] Whether that is a threat, a warning or a confession, she does not explain.

## The Ascension

[PROPOSED] Noctara Ascended in her first Bout in the Arena, before a crowd that had come to see whether the rumours were true.

She did nothing at first. She simply stood on the sand, still as a statue, while the rival Sovereign's champions charged. The crowd waited for her to move.

Then a small dark circle appeared in the air beside her: no bigger than a coin, perfectly round, perfectly black. Then another. Then a dozen. They drifted around her slowly, like moons around a planet, and where they passed, light simply went out.

Her thin crown darkened at its centre until it was an eclipse, a circle of nothing with a burning ring around it. Her eyes did not change. Her voice did not rise.

The rival champions slowed. Then stopped. The tiny voids hung in the air between them and the empress, and nobody wanted to walk into one.

Noctara tilted her head, as if listening to something far away.

"The void deepens." [canon]

The crowd went home very quietly that night.

## The Awakening — the Ascension beyond Ascension

[PROPOSED] Noctara Awakened on the one night she allowed herself to remember the edge of the Nothing.

The Bout had gone badly. Her Sovereign's champions were beaten; the rival legion was closing in. Noctara closed her eyes and thought of the place where her empire used to be.

And she opened.

There is no gentler way to say it. Noctara became a doorway. Not a door with a frame and a handle, but a shape of an empress filled with the deep endless dark of the space between stars, and through her, anyone who dared to look could see *nothing at all*. The sand of the Arena darkened under her feet. The walls dimmed. The sky above the Crownfall went black and starless, until the whole battlefield seemed to float in the void.

The rival champions did not fall. They faded, gently, one by one, back to their Sovereign's banner, unmade from the Bout as if they had never entered it.

Noctara stood alone in the darkness and spoke two words, softly, almost sadly.

"Be unmade." [canon]

When the light returned, she was simply an empress again. She was not smiling.

## Voice and temperament

Low, slow, absolute. Noctara speaks like the end of a story, and she knows it.

- "All things end in me." [canon]
- "The void deepens." [canon]
- "Nothing remains." [canon]

[PROPOSED] Her lines all sound like certainty. Her lore is all doubt. That gap is the character: an empress who sounds as though she knows everything, and who does not know the one thing that matters.

## Bonds and rivalries

- **Queen Vespera** [PROPOSED]. Vespera bows lower to Noctara than to anyone. Noctara has never asked her why. Perhaps she is afraid of the answer, because Vespera was there when the empire disappeared, and Vespera has never told her what she saw.
- **Dreadmouth** [PROPOSED]. Dreadmouth will not go near Noctara. Some say it ate her empire; some say it is the only creature that knows what really did. Noctara watches it with something close to curiosity. Neither has ever explained.
- **Sol** [PROPOSED]. "Before anything, there was Sol. Sol would like it back that way." [canon] Noctara says "All things end in me." The First Light and the Empress of Nothing stand at either end of every story, and each seems to regard the other as a puzzle that must not be solved. They are never placed on the same side.

## Beneath the Arena (Set 2)

In Act II Noctara becomes **Shadow Beneath the Crown**, an **Ascendant** and a **Seer**. [canon scene] She is seen beneath "a black hole opening in the cavern ceiling directly beneath the Crown."

[PROPOSED] Far beneath the Arena, in a great cavern lying directly under the hanging Crown, a circle of darkness has begun to open in the ceiling. Noctara was found standing beneath it, looking up. She says she did not make it. She says she does not know who did. She says it has been opening for a long time. Whether it is the Nothing, or something like it, or something else entirely, nobody can say, and Noctara has stopped guessing out loud.

[MYSTERY] Whether this darkness is connected to the Crown, the Nothing or Noctara herself is not answered.

As a **Seer**, Noctara now sees what others hide. Her ability, **ECLIPSE SIGHT**, makes your Schemes cheaper to spring, and on arrival she breaks one of your rival's face-down Schemes. At her fullest, your Schemes cost nothing, every hidden Scheme your rival holds is broken, and every rival champion with Guard 4 or less is defeated at once. Nothing stays hidden from someone who has looked into the Nothing.

## In the campaign

Chapter VI · The Court of Night. Noctara is the chapter's last Bout.

- **Trial 1 · Bout 4 — "The Edge of the Map"** [PROPOSED] The player finds an old Umbral map with a great blank space in the middle. Every champion of the chapter has a different story about what used to be there.
- **Trial 3 · Bout 4 — "The Doors of Night"** [PROPOSED] The final gauntlet: Nyx, Shade and Vespera each test the player once more. At the end, Vespera bows low before the doors to Noctara's hall, and does not follow.

**Chapter VI · The Crowning: Noctara** [PROPOSED]

The doors open on a hall with no walls, only darkness and distant pinpricks of light. Noctara waits. "All things end in me." The Bout begins.

- **Contender** — Noctara plays her standard deck with her normal printing. Her arrival scorches every one of the player's champions; the challenge is keeping a board alive long enough to break her Shards. Win line: "Nothing remains."
- **Masterwork** — Reopens after Act II. Noctara plays a stronger deck with her **Masterwork** printing, and an Edict in her favour, **The Nothing Spreads** [PROPOSED]: at the end of each round, the player's weakest champion fades back to their hand. The board slowly empties, as her empire did.
- **Omega** — The hardest Crowning of the chapter, with the **Omega** printing. The Bout is fought on a darkened board where the player cannot see the Might of Noctara's champions until they attack. If the player wins at Omega, Noctara speaks one line that appears nowhere else [PROPOSED, text only, no voice]: *"Ask me again, when I know."* She does not say what she means.

At no difficulty does anyone learn what happened to her empire.
