# Borin Ironbelly — Forgekin Hearthbreaker
*Titanborn · Bruiser · Tier II · Chapter III · The Iron Covenant*
> Status: PROPOSED lore (draft for Jerian's approval). Canon quotes are marked [canon].

## Who they are
"The Forgekin of Titan's Deep sing to metal until it agrees to become a hammer. Borin sings loudest." [canon]

Borin Ironbelly is a broad Forgekin smith with basalt skin, a braided beard of copper wire and a hammer that glows like a coal. He laughs from his belly, sings while he fights and swings while he sings.

## Before the Crownfall
The Titanborn are "Mountains wearing people" [canon], and the Forgekin are the smiths among them, living in the hot heart of Titan's Deep where rivers of molten rock light the caverns. [PROPOSED] Forgekin do not hammer metal into shape. They sing to it, long rolling songs, until the metal decides to become what they ask. Borin's voice was so loud that ore from the next cavern over would sometimes arrive at his anvil uninvited. He once made a shield rim for a quiet young Titanborn named Torren, and has never stopped mentioning it.

## Why they answered the call
[PROPOSED] Borin heard that the Crown hangs in pieces above the Arena, its shards orbiting where it broke. To a smith, a broken thing is an invitation. He does not expect to reforge it; he is boastful, not foolish. He simply wants to stand close enough to hear what kind of metal it is.

## The Ascension
Borin's first Ascension happened in the middle of a song. A rival champion's armour had turned aside every blow of his hammer, and the old smith had started singing louder and louder, the way he did at the anvil when a stubborn bar would not bend.

The Arena joined in.

Or it seemed to. The Crown above began to hum in the same low note, and the hum sank into Borin's arms. The basalt along his forearms split open, not in pain, but like the shell of a furnace, revealing veins of glowing magma beneath. The head of his hammer burst into flame and stayed that way: a live forge, roaring, with coals glowing in its heart.

Borin looked at it, delighted, and laughed so hard the sparks flew.

"Hammer sings louder!" [canon]

He swung. The hammer struck the rival's armour with a sound like a bell, and the armour gave way. A wave of heat rolled out across the rival line and scorched every champion in it.

## The Awakening — the Ascension beyond Ascension
Borin's Awakening came in the coldest Bout he ever fought, under an Edict that froze the Arena floor. His banner's champions were slowing, stiffening, falling one by one in the chill. Borin did the only thing a Forgekin knows how to do with cold. He lit a fire.

He started with himself.

He sang the oldest forge-song of the Deep, and the Crown answered with heat. Borin's whole body glowed, red, then orange, then gold. His copper beard melted into flowing molten metal that never dripped. Every step he took left glowing slag behind, and the frozen Arena floor hissed and steamed around his feet. He had become a walking furnace, and his allies gathered close to his warmth.

The rival line came at him. Borin met them with a hammer swing that rang across the whole Arena, and a wave of heat rolled outward, scorching every champion it touched.

When the steam cleared, the ice was gone and Borin was grinning.

"Well struck." [canon]

## Voice and temperament
Borin is warm, loud and generous. He boasts constantly, but always shares the credit, and he loves a good fight the way he loves a good song. He greets every Bout with "The forge is hot!" [canon] He has never held a grudge longer than it takes a hammer to cool.

## Bonds and rivalries
- **Torren** [PROPOSED]: his quiet cousin from the edge of the Deep. Borin teases him endlessly and would walk into fire for him, which, for Borin, is not much of a sacrifice.
- **Knuckle** [PROPOSED]: Borin once forged Knuckle a pair of iron gauntlets. Knuckle broke them in the first fight. Borin took it as a challenge, and they have been friendly rivals ever since.
- **Professor Spark** [PROPOSED]: two inventors who disagree on everything. Spark uses voltage; Borin uses song. They argue loudly across the Iron Covenant's camp, every night, and both enjoy it.

## Beneath the Arena (Set 2)
Beneath the Arena, Borin finds a roaring forge that has been cold for an age and lights it again. There he becomes the **Forgemaster of the Deep**, an **Artificer**, reforging a Guardian's broken relic. [PROPOSED] Borin will not say what the relic was, only that it "sang back." His **Deep Forge** recovers fallen Relics, and while Borin holds one, he hits harder and stands sturdier than ever.

## In the campaign
- **Trial 1 · Bout 7: "The Forge Is Hot"** [PROPOSED]: Borin's introduction. A puzzle Bout: the player must use Borin's Attack Scorch to clear three small rival champions in the right order.
- **Trial 2 · Bout 2: "Molten Hammer"** [PROPOSED]: Borin's deck of heavy hitters tests the player's Guard. Every Attack spreads Scorch across the player's line.
- **Trial 3 · Bout 7: "Well Struck"** [PROPOSED]: an Edict reduces every champion's Guard by 1. Borin joins the player's banner, and his heat becomes a weapon on a brittle field.
