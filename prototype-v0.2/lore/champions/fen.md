# Fen — Blood of the Pack
*Wildkin · Duelist · Tier I · Chapter II · Blood and Ember*

> Status: PROPOSED lore (draft for Jerian's approval). Canon quotes are marked [canon].

## Who they are

"Fen tracks weakness. He learned it from the winters, and the winters were thorough." [canon]

Fen is a lean grey wolf warrior with bone-hilted claws and eyes that never stop moving. When he finds a weakness, he goes for it. He does not apologise.

He is not cruel. To Fen, hunting is not about hate. It is about surviving, and about making sure the pack gets home.

## Before the Crownfall

Fen was born in the Wildkin realm, in the deep northern forests where winter lasts most of the year. His pack lived by the old rules: hunt together, eat together, leave no one behind. Fen was the runt of his litter, small and thin, and the winters nearly took him more than once.

He survived by learning. He learned which deer were tired, which ice was thin, which storms would pass and which would kill. By the time he was grown, the pack followed his nose more than the alpha's.

Then came the longest winter anyone remembered. Fen led the pack through it. Not all of them made it. He still counts the ones who did not.

## Why they answered the call

The Crownfall is a long way from the northern forests. Fen came because the Wildkin elders said the Crown could make a realm strong.

He answered a Sovereign's call because a lone wolf cannot claim anything. Fen understands packs. A Sovereign, to him, is just a new alpha, one who needs to earn his trust.

## The Ascension

It was a night bout under a red moon. The Arena sometimes turns the sky strange, and that night the moon hung low and crimson above the Crown. Fen's banner was losing badly. His Sovereign had only two Crown Shards left, and the rival had five.

Fen did not panic. He watched. He saw the rival champion who was tired, the one guarding the left too much, the one who had stopped paying attention because they thought they had already won.

He howled once, long and low.

Crimson war paint began to glow across his muzzle as if the red moon had painted it there. Scars along his arms hardened, and hide armour wrapped around his shoulders. He felt every one of the old winters standing behind him.

"The pack grows." [canon]

He struck from the shadows, where no one was looking. The tired champion fell first. Then the careless one. His banner was still behind, but Fen was strongest when it was. That was how he had always lived.

## The Awakening — the Ascension beyond Ascension

The second red moon came late in the season, during a bout against champions far bigger than Fen. He was hurt and slowing. His Sovereign was losing, and the rival's champions were closing in on every side. He thought of the long winter and the wolves he could not bring home.

Something ran beside him.

It was a wolf made of red light, larger than any wolf alive, silent and swift. Every time Fen leapt, the spirit-wolf leapt with him. Every time Fen struck, the spirit struck too. He did not know if it was one of his lost pack, or the moon, or the Crown. He did not stop to ask.

He was an alpha of the blood moon now. He brought down one champion and was ready again before it hit the sand, then another, then another.

When the bout ended, the spirit-wolf turned once, looked at him and faded.

"Good hunt." [canon]

## Voice and temperament

Fen is quiet, watchful and blunt. He speaks in short phrases and rarely smiles. He is fiercely loyal to his pack, including any banner that has earned his trust. His greeting tells you exactly what he is thinking: "I smell weakness." [canon]

## Bonds and rivalries

- **Grizz** — The enormous bear and Fen are both Wildkin, and they are an odd pair. Fen hunts; Grizz naps. Fen thinks Grizz is lazy. Grizz thinks Fen is grumpy. In battle, they fight back to back without needing a word.
- **Rask** — The pit champion of Sszar counts in threes. Fen counts tired breaths. They respect each other's skill and do not trust each other at all. Every time they meet, each tries to find the other's weakness.
- **Pyrax** — Fen once tracked Pyrax for two days through the Arena's outer gardens, thinking she was a monster. When he found her, she offered him half her lunch. He has never quite recovered from the embarrassment.

## Beneath the Arena (Set 2)

When something stirred beneath the Arena, Fen smelled it first. It was a new scent, old and huge and hungry. He went down the tunnels before anyone told him to.

Below, he became the **Pack Hunter**, a Hunter running the dark tunnels with the scent of a monster ahead. His ability, **Scent of Prey**, makes him stronger against bigger things: Monsters, Guardians, and champions who have already Ascended. At his strongest, every kill tells him something new.

Fen says it is like the winters again. He says it like that is a good thing.
