# Solara — Novice of the Dawn
*Dawnforged · Mystic · Tier I · Chapter I · The First Light*

> Status: PROPOSED lore (draft for Jerian's approval). Canon quotes are marked [canon].

## Who they are

"Her order believes the Crown is the first sunrise, stolen. She has come to return it." [canon]

Solara is the youngest voice of a very old faith. She arrives in the Crownfall with a simple brass staff, a travelling cloak that still smells of temple incense, and a certainty that most champions lost years ago. She does not want the Crown for herself. She wants to carry it home, the way you would carry back a lantern someone borrowed without asking.

## Before the Crownfall

Solara grew up in the Dawnforged realm, a land of high terraces where the sun is greeted every morning with bells. Her order keeps the Dawn Temple, a house of white stone built to face east.

The elders taught her that the world once had a first sunrise, brighter than any since, and that it was taken. They point to the Crown hanging over the Arena and say: there it is. Solara believed this the way children believe the sky is blue. Then she was chosen to go and see for herself.

## Why they answered the call

Her order sent her, and she went gladly. She answered the call of a Sovereign because a novice cannot claim the Crown alone. Privately, she has a second reason: she wants to know if the elders were right. She has never told anyone that she is not sure.

## The Ascension

It happened in her third bout, on the eastern edge of the Arena, where the light comes first. Her side was losing. A knight of her own banner had fallen, and Solara was kneeling over him with nothing left to give. The sun had not yet cleared the Arena wall.

She did not pray for victory. She prayed that the light would reach him in time.

The shards around the Crown turned, all at once, and a single beam fell across the sand. It struck her staff and the brass changed. It became a disc of gold, humming like a struck bell. Her plain robes ran with gilt thread, as if someone were stitching them in light. The fallen knight drew breath.

Solara stood up. The crowd had gone very quiet.

"The light answers me now." [canon]

She said it softly, almost as a question. Then she raised the sun-disc staff and every champion on her side was wrapped in a warm, bright shield. The cost was small but real: from that day, she could no longer tell where her own faith ended and the Crown's answer began.

## The Awakening — the Ascension beyond Ascension

Months later, at the end of a long day, the Arena was nearly dark. Shadows stretched across the sand, and a rival Sovereign's champions pressed in from every side. Solara's friends were tired. She was tired too.

She remembered the bells of the Dawn Temple, the ones rung every morning whether anyone was listening or not.

So she began the morning hymn, in the evening.

Light gathered above her head: one sun, then a second, then a third, turning slowly in a halo. Her vestments became radiant robes, and from her shoulders opened wings of morning light, soft and pale gold. The shadows did not flee. They simply stopped being shadows.

Every champion on her banner stood a little taller, their guard firmer, their wounds closing. When the bout ended, she walked among them and touched each one on the shoulder.

"Rest. The sun rises." [canon]

She is still a novice in her order's eyes. She no longer minds.

## Voice and temperament

Solara is gentle, earnest and stubborn. She speaks in short, kind sentences and rarely raises her voice. She blesses people before battle without asking if they want it. Her greeting is "The dawn walks with you." [canon]

## Bonds and rivalries

- **Sol** — Solara's order has a hymn about the first light given a body. When she first saw Sol in the Arena, she dropped her staff.
- **Solenne Ash** — A fellow Dawnforged champion and the closest thing Solara has to an older sister in the Crownfall. Solara has asked to see Solenne's list. Solenne has refused.
- **Elara** — Two healers from different worlds. They work side by side well.

## Beneath the Arena (Set 2)

When the banners went down beneath the Arena, Solara found the first place she had ever been where the sun simply did not reach. It frightened her more than any rival. She lit a small dawn-lamp from her staff and found that it held.

Below, she became the **Lamp of the Lost**, a Restorer who walks the dark side-passages looking for explorers who took a wrong turn. Her ability, **Lamplight**, shields a friend when she arrives and, as she grows, mends what the depths have broken.

## In the campaign

Solara is the very first champion most players meet, and her card is in the starter deck.

- **Trial 1 · Bout 1 — "The First Morning":** A tutorial Bout. Solara guides the player through placing a champion and using Shield.
- **Trial 1 · Bout 3 — "Hold the Light":** A puzzle Bout. The player must keep Solara on the field for five turns while the rival only attacks her. Win by shielding her in time.
- **Trial 2 · Bout 2 — "The Novice's Test":** Solara tests the player with her own deck of Dawnforged healers and shields.
