# Vessimer — The Tinctor
*Hexbound · Saboteur · Tier IV · Chapter IV · The Greenwall*
> Status: PROPOSED lore (draft for Jerian's approval). Canon quotes are marked [canon].

## Who they are
"Nobody has seen Vessimer's face. Vessimer is not sure he still has one." [canon]

Vessimer is a masked chemist with a bandolier of glass vials across his chest and a mind that never stops experimenting. He speaks politely, works carefully and treats every Bout as a laboratory. Every potion he throws is a test. Every result is a note in his book. What lies behind his mask, even Vessimer cannot say for certain, and he seems to find that the most interesting question of all.

## Before the Crownfall
The Hexbound are "Cursed and cursing" [canon]. [PROPOSED] Most Hexbound practise old witchcraft in the misty Hexmire along the Greenwall's edge. Vessimer chose a stranger path. He wanted to understand curses, not just cast them: to measure them, mix them and, one day, perfect them. He built a laboratory in a sunken tower at the edge of the marsh and spent years brewing tinctures no one else dared touch. One of those experiments changed him. He put on the mask that night, and he has never taken it off.

## Why they answered the call
[PROPOSED] Vessimer believes the Crown is the greatest formula in any realm: many ingredients, broken apart, waiting to be understood. He wants to study it. He also hopes, though he says this only to himself, that somewhere in the Crownfall there is a tincture that can show him what he used to be. He answered a Sovereign's call with genuine delight. "A new experiment!" [canon]

## The Ascension
Vessimer's first Ascension came on the day an experiment failed. He had thrown his strongest tincture at a rival champion, and the champion had simply shrugged it off. Vessimer tilted his mask, consulted his notes and frowned beneath it.

"Insufficient," he murmured. "More apparatus required."

The Crown, high above, seemed to agree.

Light gathered at his back, and with a hiss of steam, brass apparatus unfolded there: pipes, valves, little pressure gauges with trembling needles, a coiled copper still that bubbled softly as it worked. Tubes snaked over his shoulders and fed directly into the vials on his bandolier, refilling them as fast as he could throw. The whole contraption hissed and whistled like a kettle.

Vessimer adjusted a valve with one gloved finger and checked the readings.

"The formula improves." [canon]

His next tincture burst across the rival line in a bright cloud. Every champion it touched was scorched, and his own allies were wrapped in a thin, shimmering shield. Vessimer noted the result in his book with great satisfaction. It was the first time anyone had heard him hum.

## The Awakening — the Ascension beyond Ascension
Vessimer's Awakening came during the grandest experiment of his life. His Sovereign was one Shard from defeat, and Vessimer had one idea left. It was a very large idea.

He opened every vial at once.

The Crown answered in a rush of light, and his laboratory came to him. Flasks rose from his bandolier and hung in the air around him, dozens of them, glowing every colour. Glass tubes formed between them, carrying shimmering liquids from flask to flask. Burners flickered beneath them. The whole floating laboratory turned slowly around Vessimer as he stood at its center, conducting it with both hands.

He raised his arms.

"Tinctures — away!" [canon]

The flasks flew. They burst across the field in a storm of colour, scorching every rival champion and shielding every ally. When the colours faded, the rival line had broken.

Vessimer took out his book and wrote a single word. Then he read it aloud.

"Results: conclusive." [canon]

## Voice and temperament
Vessimer is courteous, curious and quietly strange. He speaks like a scientist reading notes aloud, calm and precise, with sudden bursts of excitement when something surprising happens. He is never cruel; he simply cares more about the result than the risk. Behind the mask, he is lonely, and a little afraid of what he might find if he ever takes it off.

## Bonds and rivalries
- **Professor Spark** [PROPOSED]: an inventor from the Iron Covenant who uses voltage where Vessimer uses tinctures. Their rivalry is polite, curious and full of small, smoking accidents.
- **Hexie** [PROPOSED]: the young witch's spells are sloppy, by Vessimer's standards. He secretly admires how often they work anyway. They trade ingredients and insults.
- **Thorn** [PROPOSED]: Vessimer would very much like a sample of Thorn's poison. Thorn keeps saying no. Vessimer keeps asking politely.

## Beneath the Arena (Set 2)
In a hidden laboratory deep under the Arena, Vessimer becomes the **Alchemist of Relics**, an **Artificer**, dissolving old relics into bubbling tinctures. [PROPOSED] He believes every relic holds the essence of the one who made it. If he can distil that essence, perhaps he can learn whose face is missing beneath his mask. His **Transmute** makes his banner's Relics cheaper and recovers fallen ones, and at its fullest it draws him new cards to experiment with.
