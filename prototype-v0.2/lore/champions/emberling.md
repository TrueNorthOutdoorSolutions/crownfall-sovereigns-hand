# Emberling — Spark That Learned to Speak
*Elemental · Arcanist · Tier I · Chapter II · Blood and Ember*

> Status: PROPOSED lore (draft for Jerian's approval). Canon quotes are marked [canon].

## Who they are

"She was a candle once. Then someone read a spell too close to her." [canon]

Emberling is a small living flame with curious coal eyes and a voice like crackling kindling. She is about the size of a teacup, floats wherever she likes and wants to know what everything feels like when it is on fire. She is cheerful, impatient and astonishingly brave for someone who can be put out with a cup of water.

## Before the Crownfall

Emberling was a candle on a scholar's desk in a tower of the Elemental realm, a land where fire, water, stone and storm have voices of their own. For years she simply burned, quietly, lighting pages of spell books and going out at bedtime.

One night the scholar read aloud a spell of awakening, much too close to the candle. Something caught. The flame flickered, blinked and said its first word. The word was "Oh."

The scholar fainted. Emberling explored. She learned to speak from the scholar's books, to float from watching moths, and to throw small fireballs from watching the hearth.

## Why they answered the call

Emberling heard about the Crownfall from a travelling fire elemental and immediately wanted to go. Everyone said she was too small. She took that as a challenge.

She answered a Sovereign's call because she wants to see everything, and the Crownfall has everything. If asked about the Crown, she says she wants to know if it is warm.

## The Ascension

It was her first real bout. Emberling had been told to stay at the back. Instead she floated straight to the front, where a big rival champion was about to crush one of her friends.

"Hey!" she said. Nobody heard her. She was very small.

So she got bigger.

She did not know she could do it. She just burned harder than she ever had, until the air around her crackled. Her flame turned blue at the tips, then all over the top, and a crown of blue-hot embers formed on her head. She was a crackling fire sprite now, bright and furious.

"I'm a real fire now!" [canon]

The rival champion stepped back, surprised. Emberling threw her first proper fireball, and it hit, and it hurt. Her friends cheered. Emberling did three loops in the air.

That night she asked the Crown if it could make her even bigger. The Crown did not answer. She decided that meant "maybe."

## The Awakening — the Ascension beyond Ascension

Emberling's Awakening came during a bout against water champions, which she had always been secretly afraid of. One splash and she would be gone. She knew it. Everyone knew it. Her friends had told her to stay home.

The rival sent a great wave across the Arena towards her banner. Her friends could not get out of the way in time.

Emberling did not run. She took a deep breath, which flames are not supposed to be able to do, and burned.

She became a miniature sun-spirit. The air around her warped and shimmered with heat. When the wave reached her, it turned to steam and drifted away. Her fireballs flew out in spiralling trails, curling across the Arena, scorching every rival champion at once.

When the steam cleared, she was still there, glowing, very pleased with herself.

"Warm, warm, warm!" [canon]

She is still afraid of water. She is just less afraid than she was.

## Voice and temperament

Emberling is bubbly, curious, impulsive and kind-hearted. She talks fast and asks a lot of questions, most of them about fire. Her greeting is always "Can I burn something?" [canon] The answer is usually no. She asks anyway.

## Bonds and rivalries

- **Ignara** — The great fire elemental warrior. Emberling thinks Ignara is the most amazing person who ever lived and follows her everywhere. Ignara finds this embarrassing and has quietly taken Emberling under her protection.
- **Grizz** — Emberling once tried to set Grizz's fur on fire to see what would happen. He laughed so hard he fell over. Now they are best friends, and she sometimes rides on his head.
- **Mirella** — The water mage of the tides is very kind to Emberling, which Emberling finds terrifying. They are slowly becoming friends. Emberling keeps a respectful distance of three paces.

## Beneath the Arena (Set 2)

When the banners went down beneath the Arena, Emberling found the forge-vents: hot shafts of air rising from the deep forges, full of sparks. She flew into one and did not come out for a day.

When she did, she had changed. She became the **Ember Conduit**, a Channeler, a living spark drifting through the forge-vents with sparks spiralling up into her. Her ability, **Ember Echo**, means that every time her banner casts a Tactic, a little bit of fire leaps out and Scorches a rival. At her strongest, it scorches all of them.

She says the forges sing. She has been trying to learn the song.

## In the campaign

- **Trial 1 · Bout 4 — "Too Close to the Spell":** A tutorial Bout teaching Scorch. Emberling Scorches a rival as she arrives, and the player learns to remove small champions without fighting them.
- **Trial 2 · Bout 4 — "The Spark's Test":** Emberling tests the player with an Elemental fire deck full of small Scorch effects.
- **Trial 3 · Bout 4 — "Fire and Water" (Edict in play):** The Edict gives champions with Shield +1 Guard. Emberling and Mirella both fight on the player's side and must learn to work together.
