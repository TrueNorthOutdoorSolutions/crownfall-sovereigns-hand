# Astryon — The Seven-Star Blade
*Astral · Duelist · Tier IV · Chapter VIII · The One Who Refused*

> Status: PROPOSED lore (draft for Jerian's approval). Canon quotes are marked [canon].

## Who they are

"Astryon has cut down seven stars. He intends the Crown to be the eighth." [canon]

Astryon is a silver celestial swordsman, proud and graceful, who has done something no other champion has: he has duelled the stars and won. Seven times. He fights like a dancer and speaks like a king, and he believes, with complete sincerity, that he is the greatest blade in any realm.

## Before the Crownfall

PROPOSED: In the Astral realm, some stars are not gentle. They burn too hot, swing too low and threaten the worlds beneath them. When such a star cannot be reasoned with, the Astral houses send a blade.

Astryon was that blade. He climbed the sky seven times and fought seven stars, each one larger and older than the last. Each time he came back. Each time the star did not.

After the seventh, there were no more stars that needed cutting. Astryon found peace very boring.

## Why they answered the call

Astryon intends the Crown to be his eighth. [canon reason, from his lore]

Exactly what that means is the subject of great argument. Some say he means to win it; some say to cut it down. Astryon, when asked, only smiles and says the Crown "will understand when we meet."

What he does not understand, and what troubles him deeply, is Crownless. Someone reached the end of the Crownfall and turned away. To Astryon, who has never turned away from anything, that is the one thing in all the realms he cannot make sense of.

## The Ascension

PROPOSED: Astryon's first Bout in Chapter VIII was against three rival champions at once. He found this insulting. He told them so.

They attacked together. He met them with one blade, and for a moment, even Astryon was hard-pressed. His sword could not be in three places at once.

He stepped back, raised his blade toward the sky and called to it as he used to call to the stars he fought.

The answer came in gold and white. Armour bloomed across his silver frame. Behind his shoulders, two more blades appeared, celestial and shining, floating at his back as if held by hands nobody could see.

Astryon smiled.

"My blades multiply." [canon]

He moved. Three blades struck at once, and each strike left a trail of tiny constellations hanging in the air behind it, like sparks from a forge. The three rival champions were down before the last trail had faded.

Astryon bowed to the stands, only slightly.

## The Awakening — the Ascension beyond Ascension

PROPOSED: Astryon Awakened in the greatest duel of his life: against a rival who fought as well as he did.

For the first time since the seventh star, he was not sure he would win.

He did not retreat. He stood in the centre of the Arena and let the starlight come, and it came as never before. Cosmic armour, deep as the night sky, spread across him. Around him, six spectral swords rose and began to orbit, slow and silver, each one the ghost of a star he had cut down.

Then the Arena itself began to change. The stones faded. The walls fell away. The crowd gasped as the whole Arena turned to open space, black and endless and full of stars, with Astryon at its heart.

He raised his own blade, the seventh among the six.

"Seven stars — fall!" [canon]

They fell. When the Arena returned, his rival was kneeling among the stones.

"Another star cut down." [canon]

He did not look up at the Crown. Not yet.

## Voice and temperament

Grand, proud and theatrical, but honourable. He never strikes an opponent who has yielded, and he always thanks a worthy rival.

- "The eighth star awaits." [canon]
- "My blades multiply." [canon]

## Bonds and rivalries

- **Orion** (PROPOSED): The endless argument: is it better to fire a star or to cut one down? Orion says firing lends a star to the sky; Astryon says cutting one finishes its story. They are secretly great friends.
- **Ronin Zero** (PROPOSED): Ronin watches Astryon fight, searching for a familiar cut. Astryon is flattered.
- **Crownless** (PROPOSED): The riddle he cannot cut. Astryon has challenged Crownless to explain the refusal, and then to a duel. Crownless declined both. Astryon took that harder than any defeat.

## Beneath the Arena (Set 2)

**Blade That Fell Beneath · Gladiator.** Beneath the Arena, there are no stars, only darkness. Astryon falls into it, and for the first time in his life, he has nothing above him to aim at. His Set 2 card shows him in the dark with seven blades orbiting him like fallen stars.

He becomes a gladiator below. His ability, **Starfall Duel**, gives him extra Might while attacking a champion. Ascended, each champion he defeats draws you a card; Awakened, he readies after a defeat and strikes again.

Down here, he has started to wonder whether some stars fall on their own.
