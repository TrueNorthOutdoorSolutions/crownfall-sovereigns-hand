# General Varr — The Unbroken Line
*Kingdom · Commander, Vanguard · Tier IV · Chapter I · The First Light*

> Status: PROPOSED lore (draft for Jerian's approval). Canon quotes are marked [canon].

## Who they are

"Varr has held every line he was given. He was never given the Crown." [canon]

General Varr is a grizzled kingdom commander with a tower shield, a grey beard and a voice like a falling wall. He has fought in more wars than most champions have lived years. He is not flashy and does not try to be. He stands where he is told to stand, and nothing gets past him.



## Before the Crownfall

Varr was born in the Kingdom realm to a family of farmers. He joined the royal army at fifteen because the harvest had failed and the army paid in bread. He never left.

He held the Bridge of Tolls for nine days against an army five times his size, and a hundred lines after it. Each time, he was given a medal, a handshake and a new line to hold.

He was never made a lord. He was never offered a crown. He says he never wanted one. People who know him well are not so sure.

## Why they answered the call

The Kingdom sent its best to the Crownfall, and Varr was among them, as always. Officially, he answered a Sovereign's call to protect Princess Aurelia and the Kingdom's younger champions.

He has spent his whole life holding lines for crowns other people wore. Somewhere in the back of his mind, he wants to stand in front of the one crown that cannot be inherited and simply ask it: was that worth something?

## The Ascension

It was a brutal bout. The rival had three champions with Charge, all aimed at Varr's line. His Sovereign's banner was down to its last Crown Shard. Behind Varr stood young Kael and a half-trained archer, Rowan.

Varr set his tower shield in the sand and planted his feet.

"Form up!" [canon]

The first charge hit him and stopped. The second hit him and stopped. On the third, something changed. The weight of every war he had fought seemed to settle on his shoulders, and it did not crush him. It steadied him.

His plain uniform shone with medals, one for every war he had held, each glinting gold in the sun. A banner-spear rose in his hand, the Kingdom's colours snapping above him. Every champion behind him felt a shield fall over them like a warm blanket.

"The line does not break." [canon]

It did not. Afterwards, Kael asked him how he did it. Varr shrugged and said, "Same as always. I stayed."

## The Awakening — the Ascension beyond Ascension

Years of battle came together in a single bout. The Kingdom's banner faced a Sovereign whose champions seemed endless. Varr was wounded. Elara had begged him to sit it out. He refused.

As the rival's final assault came, Varr raised his shield and felt the Arena go very quiet.

Beside him, figures began to form out of the dust: shieldbearers, translucent and glowing faintly, in the uniforms of every war he had fought. Some he recognised. Some he had buried. They raised their shields beside his without a word.

He did not ask how. He did not ask why. He simply gave the order he had given a thousand times.

"Hold the line!" [canon]

Every champion behind him stood behind a wall of phantom shields, and the rival's assault broke on it like a wave on a cliff. When the dust settled, the phantoms were gone. Varr lowered his shield.

"Line held." [canon]

## Voice and temperament

Varr is gruff, patient and steady. He speaks in short, plain sentences and never boasts. He is gentle with the young and stern with the careless. He remembers every soldier he lost. His orders never change: "Form up!" [canon] And when it is over, only two words: "Line held." [canon]

## Bonds and rivalries

- **Aurelia** — Varr served her family for three generations and taught her to hold a shield. He would give his life for her. He would also tell her, to her face, when she is wrong.
- **Elara** — The field surgeon who has patched him up more times than either can count. He calls her the finest soldier in the Kingdom.
- **Rowan** — Varr wants archers in rows. Rowan wanders off to find better angles. Varr grumbles every time and has never once been wrong to trust him.

## Beneath the Arena (Set 2)

When the banners went down beneath the Arena, somebody had to stay at the gate. Varr did not need to be asked.

Below, he became the **Holder of the Gate**, a Sentinel holding the last gate between the Arena and what lies beneath. His ability, **Last Gate**, weakens anything that tries to reach his banner's Crown: rival champions attacking it are weaker, Kingdom allies around him are tougher, and at his strongest he shields every champion he has.

He has not seen what is down there. He does not need to. His job is that it does not come up.
