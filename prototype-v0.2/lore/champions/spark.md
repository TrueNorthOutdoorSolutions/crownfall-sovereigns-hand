# Professor Spark — Arc-Engineer Extraordinaire
*Gearbound · Summoner · Tier III · Chapter III · The Iron Covenant*
> Status: PROPOSED lore (draft for Jerian's approval). Canon quotes are marked [canon].

## Who they are
"Professor Spark was expelled from four academies for "excessive voltage"." [canon]

Professor Spark is a wild-haired engineer with a wrench in one hand, a tool-pack on his back and a head full of ideas that crackle. He builds turrets the way other people doodle. He is brilliant, cheerful and completely unable to leave a machine alone. If it explodes, Spark wants to know why, and then build a bigger one.

## Before the Crownfall
[PROPOSED] Spark was born in the Brasslands, the Gearbound realm of foundries and buried engines, and the Gearbound are "Machines of every age" [canon], but also the people who build them. He studied at four great academies in turn. At the first, he lit up the library. At the second, he lit up the river. At the third, he built a turret that would not stop building smaller turrets. The fourth academy expelled him before he unpacked.

## Why they answered the call
[PROPOSED] No academy would have Spark, so Spark went looking for a laboratory big enough to hold him. He found it in the Crownfall: a floating kingdom held together by a power nobody understands. To Spark, the Arena of Ascension is the greatest experiment in any realm. He wants to measure the Crown and, if anyone lets him, improve it. No one has.

## The Ascension
Spark's first Ascension happened in the middle of a repair. His Arc Turret had taken a hit, and he was kneeling beside it, wrench in one hand, screwdriver in his teeth, trying to fix the barrel and dodge the rival's strikes at the same time.

"I need," he mumbled around the screwdriver, "another hand."

The Crown gave him one.

Light poured into his tool-pack, and something stirred inside it. With a whir and a clank, a mechanical arm unfolded from the pack, jointed brass with a little three-fingered grip on the end. It reached over Spark's shoulder, took the screwdriver from his mouth and finished the repair on its own. His goggles began to spark with tiny blue arcs, and through them, every machine on the field glowed with lines he could read like writing.

Spark stared at the new arm. The arm stared back. Then it handed him a wrench.

He leapt to his feet, beaming.

"Version two, even better!" [canon]

A second Arc Turret rose from the Arena floor, and Spark's banner had a fortress where a moment before it had a broken gun.

## The Awakening — the Ascension beyond Ascension
Spark's Awakening came during a Bout so fierce that he did not have time to build anything. Every turret he set down was struck before it could fire. He was running out of parts, out of ideas, and out of floor.

So the Crown gave him a workshop that could walk.

Its legs came first, long brass legs with wide feet. Then the floor, the benches, the shelves of tools, the crackling coils, all assembling around him in a rush of light. Spark found himself sitting at the controls of a walking workshop, and the mechanical arm was already busy at the bench behind him. It built a turret. Then another. It set them down on either side of the workshop as it marched, a little fortress growing with every step.

Around the turrets, his allies stood a little straighter, sheltered by the crackling arcs. The rival line could not keep up. Spark leaned out of his window, goggles blazing.

"Science wins again!" [canon]

## Voice and temperament
Spark is boundless enthusiasm wrapped around a sharp mind. He talks fast, laughs loudly and is genuinely puzzled by complaints. "Excessive voltage? Never!" [canon] He is kind to his creations, careless with his own safety and never discouraged for long.

## Bonds and rivalries
- **Pip** [PROPOSED]: Spark's most eager student and most dangerous assistant. Spark gives Pip parts; Pip gives Spark explosions to study.
- **HEX-3** [PROPOSED]: Spark wants to improve HEX-3's reactor. HEX-3 would like Spark to stay on the other side of the room. They are, oddly, close friends.
- **Vessimer** [PROPOSED]: the masked chemist of the Hexbound is the only inventor in the Arena whose work Spark cannot explain. Their rivalry is polite, curious and full of small, smoking accidents.

## Beneath the Arena (Set 2)
Under the Arena, Spark sets up a lamp-lit lab and becomes the **Relic Inventor**, an **Artificer**. [PROPOSED] To him, every relic in the vaults is a battery with a story. His **Prototype** wires those relics straight into his work: he arrives already carrying one, makes Relics cheaper for his banner, and when he Ascends, new Arc Turrets crackle into life around him. Spark insists every turret he builds down here is "version two."

## In the campaign
- **Trial 1 · Bout 5: "Excessive Voltage"** [PROPOSED]: a puzzle Bout. The player must protect Spark's Arc Turret for two turns so it can finish a fragile rival line.
- **Trial 2 · Bout 8: "Deploy Turret"** [PROPOSED]: Spark's deck floods the board with Arc Turrets. The player learns to deal with wide boards and summoned tokens.
- **Trial 3 · Bout 4: "Version Two, Even Better"** [PROPOSED]: an Edict makes tokens arrive with +1 Guard. Spark fights at the player's side, and his turrets become the backbone of the final run.
