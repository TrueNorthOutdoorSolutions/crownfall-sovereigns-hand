# Bolt — Unit B-07
*Gearbound · Vanguard · Tier I · Chapter III · The Iron Covenant*
> Status: PROPOSED lore (draft for Jerian's approval). Canon quotes are marked [canon].

## Who they are
"Bolt was built to guard a door. The door is gone. Bolt has decided you are the door now." [canon]

Bolt is a compact combat automaton with one amber eye and a single, simple purpose. It guards. Once Bolt has chosen what it protects, nothing in the Arena will move it. It does not chat; it reports. When a Sovereign calls, Bolt steps in front of them, and from that moment on, that Sovereign is the door.

## Before the Crownfall
[PROPOSED] Bolt was made in the Brasslands, the Gearbound realm of foundries and buried engines, as one of a series of door-wardens. Each B-unit was given one door. B-07's door stood at the end of a long hall in a forgotten archive. For longer than Bolt can count, it turned away anyone without the right key. Nobody ever had it. Then one day the hall shook, the roof fell, and when the dust settled the door was simply not there anymore. Bolt checked its orders. They had not changed.

## Why they answered the call
[PROPOSED] A guard without a door is a strange thing. Bolt walked out of the ruined archive looking for something worth guarding. When a Sovereign's call rang across the Brasslands, Bolt calculated that a Sovereign who means to claim the Crown will need, above all else, someone standing between them and harm. It reported for duty.

## The Ascension
The rival was a pack of fast striking champions, and they were going around Bolt, not through it. Every turn, Bolt stepped left; every turn, they slipped right. Its single amber eye flickered red. Bolt's armour was dented, its gears were grinding, and its Sovereign's Shards were taking hits it had been built to stop.

Bolt stopped stepping. It planted both feet and sent one quiet request upward to the Crown: more.

The answer came as a hum through its frame. Plates of reinforced metal slid out of nowhere and locked across its chest. Its small hands folded away, and in their place swung two heavy piston fists. In the middle of its body, a gear core began to spin, faster and faster, until the air around Bolt seemed to pull toward it. The rival champions found their feet dragging, as if the little automaton had become the center of the Arena.

Bolt raised one piston fist and checked its systems.

"Upgrade accepted." [canon]

After that, nobody went around Bolt. Nobody got past it either.

## The Awakening — the Ascension beyond Ascension
Bolt's Awakening happened in a Bout it was losing on purpose. Its Sovereign had a single Shard left, and Bolt had been ordered to stand aside so a stronger champion could hold the line. Bolt considered this order. Then, for the first time in its long life, it overruled one.

It walked to the Shard and stood in front of it.

The Crown poured light into the small machine. Its plating turned gleaming brass. A reactor heart flared to life in its chest, steady and gold, and behind its shoulders, wide shield vanes folded open like the leaves of a great door. Each vane caught the light. Each one held. The rival's final strike hit those vanes and stopped as if it had struck the side of a mountain.

Around Bolt, the other champions of its banner felt the vanes' shadow fall over them, and their own guard grew stronger. The whole line had become a door.

Bolt's amber eye dimmed to a calm, patient glow.

"Door secured." [canon]

## Voice and temperament
Bolt is loyal, literal and quietly stubborn. It speaks in short status reports, beginning with "Unit B-07 online." [canon] Underneath the reports, Bolt is lonely, and glad to have someone to guard again.

## Bonds and rivalries
- **Titan Rho** [PROPOSED]: two guardians who outlived what they guarded. Bolt finds Titan Rho's endless watch admirable. It has never told Titan Rho that its city is gone. Bolt understands why.
- **Torren** [PROPOSED]: the Titanborn calls a shield a promise; Bolt calls it a door. They agree it is the same thing.
- **Pip** [PROPOSED]: Pip keeps trying to stand behind Bolt during his biggest blasts. Bolt keeps allowing it. Bolt considers this a mystery.

## Beneath the Arena (Set 2)
Under the Arena, Bolt finds a scrapyard cave full of broken relic plates and becomes the **Scrap Smith**, an **Artificer**. [PROPOSED] Without a forge, Bolt welds what it finds straight onto its own chassis. Its **Scrapforge** makes Relics cheaper for its whole banner, and when Bolt carries one, it guards even harder. Bolt says it is "adding doors." Nobody has the heart to correct it.

## In the campaign
- **Trial 1 · Bout 1: "The Door Is Gone"** [PROPOSED]: the Iron Covenant's opening Bout. Bolt must keep a single Crown Shard alive for four turns with only Shield and Bulwark.
- **Trial 2 · Bout 4: "You Are the Door Now"** [PROPOSED]: Bolt's deck of Guard and Shield effects teaches the player to attack through a Bulwark rather than around it.
- **Trial 3 · Bout 2: "Upgrade Accepted"** [PROPOSED]: an Edict grants a free Shield each turn to the defending side. Bolt fights beside the player and is key to outlasting the clock.
