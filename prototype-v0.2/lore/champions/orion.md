# Orion — The Constellation Sniper
*Astral · Ranger · Tier III · Chapter VIII · The One Who Refused*

> Status: PROPOSED lore (draft for Jerian's approval). Canon quotes are marked [canon].

## Who they are

"Orion names every star he fires. He is running out of names." [canon]

Orion is a star-powered sniper with a crescent longbow, a sharp eye and a terrible problem: every arrow he looses is a star, and every star deserves a name. He has named thousands. He is starting to repeat himself, and it bothers him far more than missing ever has.

## Before the Crownfall

PROPOSED: Orion was a star-charter in the Astral realm, one of those patient scholars who map the sky and keep the great book of names. He knew every star by heart.

Then the Astral realm needed an archer, and the only arrows sharp enough were stars. Orion was the one who could aim them. He agreed on one condition: that every star he fired would be written into the great book with a name, so no star would ever be forgotten.

He kept that promise. The book is enormous now. He carries a copy, and adds to it every night.

## Why they answered the call

PROPOSED: Orion has noticed the same thing Seren has: stars across the sky are drifting, slowly, toward the Crownfall. He wants to chart them. He wants to know whether the Crown is pulling them.

And he has a quieter hope. Somewhere in the Crownfall, among champions from every realm and age, there may be names he has never heard.

## The Ascension

PROPOSED: The target was across the whole Arena, half hidden behind a pillar, and Orion's Sovereign had nearly given up hope.

Orion did not draw. He closed his eyes and listened to the sky.

Above him, faint in the daylight, the stars he had already fired and named were still out there somewhere. He felt them: hundreds, then thousands, each one a name he had written in the book.

When he opened his eyes, they were on his armour. Little constellations, glowing across his chest and shoulders, each line a memory. His crescent bow had changed too. When he drew it back, the string pulled starlight out of the air.

He had never felt so sure of a shot.

"The constellations align." [canon]

The arrow crossed the Arena in a long silver line and found its mark behind the pillar.

That night Orion wrote a new name in the book. It was the first time in a year he had not had to think about it.

## The Awakening — the Ascension beyond Ascension

PROPOSED: The rival champion was enormous, a giant from some old realm, with armour that turned aside every arrow Orion had. The Bout was nearly lost.

Orion lowered his bow. Then he raised his eyes to the sky.

High above the Crownfall, a comet was crossing. He had watched it every night since he arrived. He had never named it, because it had never been his to name.

He asked it.

The comet turned. It came down out of the sky in a long, bright arc, and settled onto his bowstring like a bird landing on a branch. Orion drew. The bow sang.

When the comet left the string, the stands went white. It crossed the Arena with a sound like the whole sky tearing, and struck the giant squarely, and the giant sat down, very surprised, among the scattered pieces of its armour.

Orion lowered his bow, opened his book and wrote carefully.

"Named and claimed." [canon]

## Voice and temperament

Quiet, scholarly and dryly funny. He is gentle with almost everyone, but he will lecture at length about any star you ask about.

- "I have a star with your name." [canon]
- "The constellations align." [canon]

## Bonds and rivalries

- **Astryon** (PROPOSED): Astryon has cut down seven stars; Orion has fired thousands. They argue constantly about whose count matters more. Orion says firing a star is lending it; Astryon says cutting one is finishing it. Neither will budge.
- **Seren** (PROPOSED): Seren catches the stars he fires and returns them with names on. He pretends to be embarrassed. He keeps every name she gives him.
- **Zyrak** (PROPOSED): Orion once asked to name Zyrak's prism. The answering flash blinded him for an hour. He has written "Zyrak: no" in the book.

## Beneath the Arena (Set 2)

**Star-Charter of the Deep · Sharpshooter.** Under the Arena, Orion finds constellations painted on a cavern ceiling, ancient and very accurate, mapping a sky nobody alive has seen. His Set 2 card shows him charting them, bow resting on his shoulder.

He becomes a sharpshooter below. His ability, **Charted Shot**, Scorches a rival champion when he attacks. Awakened, his first shot as he Ascends Scorches every rival champion, and he gains Flight, rising up into the dark to see the ceiling more clearly.

Some of the painted stars have names beside them, in a hand Orion does not recognise. One of them is his.
