# Rask — Pit Champion of Sszar
*Wildkin · Duelist · Tier II · Chapter II · Blood and Ember*

> Status: PROPOSED lore (draft for Jerian's approval). Canon quotes are marked [canon].

## Who they are

"Rask counts in threes. So do his opponents, briefly." [canon]

Rask is a reptilian gladiator with a notched gladius, cool green scales and a slow, unblinking stare. He fights in a rhythm only he can hear: step, step, strike. One, two, three.

He is a showman and a professional. He loves the crowd, but he does not fight for it. He fights because it is the thing he is best at in all the world, and he has never been anything else.

## Before the Crownfall

Rask comes from the Wildkin realm, from the hot desert city of Sszar, where the reptile clans settle their arguments in great sand pits instead of wars. The pit fighters of Sszar are its heroes.

Rask was born to nobody in particular. He fought his first pit bout at fourteen for a bowl of rice and a blanket. He lost. He fought again the next day, and the day after that. Slowly, he learned the rhythm: patience, timing, three clean strokes. By twenty, he was undefeated. By twenty-five, they called him the Pit Champion of Sszar.

He grew bored. The best fighters of Sszar had all faced him. None of them had made him count past three.

## Why they answered the call

When word reached Sszar of an Arena floating above the realms, where champions from every age fought for a Crown, Rask packed his gladius and his trophy belt and left that night.

He answered a Sovereign's call because the Crownfall is the biggest pit there has ever been. He wants to know if anyone there can make him count to four.

## The Ascension

It was his tenth bout in the Arena. He was behind, as he often let himself be; Rask fights better when the crowd thinks he is losing. His Sovereign had two Crown Shards left. The rival's champions were grinning.

Rask began to count.

One. He stepped forward, and the first rival fell.

Two. He turned, and the second rival fell.

Three. He looked up, and the crowd had started chanting.

They were chanting his name. They did not even know why. Something in the Arena, or the Crown, had heard the rhythm and joined in.

His plain pit armour shone gold, polished like the shields of the old Sszar champions. A trophy belt settled around his waist, heavy with tokens from every bout he had ever won.

"The crowd chants my name." [canon]

He said it quietly, almost surprised. Then he went back to work, ready again after every win.

## The Awakening — the Ascension beyond Ascension

Rask's Awakening came in the longest bout he had ever fought. His rival was a champion who did not fall on three, or on four, or on five. Rask was tired, his rhythm broken, his scales scratched. For the first time in years he wondered if he might lose.

He smiled. He had come all this way to wonder that.

His scales turned to gold, every one, gleaming like coins in the sun. Across his shoulders fell a long cloak, stitched from the colours of every champion he had ever bested, a patchwork of banners from every corner of the realms.

He breathed slowly, in and out, and listened. He found the rhythm again. Not one, two, three. Something deeper, slower, older.

When it was over, he bowed to his fallen rival, helped them up and walked off the sand to the roar of the crowd.

"Undefeated." [canon]

He has added the rival's colours to his cloak.

## Voice and temperament

Rask is calm, confident and a little theatrical. He speaks slowly, with a faint hiss, and counts aloud before fighting: "One. Two. Three." [canon] He respects skill above everything and is courteous to opponents who fight well. He is quietly lonely, though he would never say so.

## Bonds and rivalries

- **Fen** — The wolf hunter who tracks weakness. Rask and Fen keep trying to find each other's weak spots. Neither has.
- **Kael** — The young Kingdom swordsman once spared a rival in a bout Rask was watching. Rask did not understand it. He went to ask Kael why. They talked for hours. Rask still does not understand, but he respects it.
- **Grizz** — Grizz loves the crowd, and so does Rask. They once wrestled in the outer gardens for an audience of three Sovereigns and a gardener. Rask lost count. He considers it the best fight of his life.

## Beneath the Arena (Set 2)

When the banners went down beneath the Arena, Rask found something that made him laugh out loud: a pit. Deep under the Crownfall, in a cavern full of blood-red sand, there were old fighting pits, older than Sszar.

Below, he became the **Champion of the Underpits**, a Gladiator roaring in the red sand with a monster's skull at his feet. His ability, **Pit Fighter**, makes him stronger whenever he attacks a champion. When he wins, he is shielded, and at his best he is ready to fight again straight away.

He has started bringing a crowd down with him.
