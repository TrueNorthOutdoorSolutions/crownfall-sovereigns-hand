# Veyra Sol — Reality Hunter
*Riftborn · Ascendant, Assassin · Tier V · Chapter VII · Storm and Rift (the Crowning)*

> Status: PROPOSED lore (draft for Jerian's approval). Canon quotes are marked [canon].

## Who they are

"Veyra hunts things that escape between worlds. The Crown is the biggest one yet." [canon]

Veyra Sol is a huntress with portal-rings on her wrists and a patience that has outlasted whole realities. She is calm, precise and unhurried. She does not boast and she does not chase. She waits at the right door, and sooner or later her quarry walks through it. She is one of the eight legends the Crown has raised highest.

## Before the Crownfall

PROPOSED: The Riftborn tell many stories about Veyra, and she confirms none of them. In one, she was a tracker from a small world nearly swallowed by something that slipped in through a crack. In another, she was the one who escaped, long ago.

When asked which is true, Veyra says, "Yes."

What everyone agrees on is this: some things do not belong where they are. They cross between worlds and cause harm. When they do, Veyra is the one who comes for them.

## Why they answered the call

Veyra believes the Crown is something that has escaped between worlds. [canon reason, from her lore]

She does not say what she means by that, or where she thinks it escaped from, and she does not take kindly to guesses.

She answered a Sovereign's call because a hunter must get close to her quarry. Whether she means to catch the Crown, return it or something else entirely, she has never said.

## The Ascension

PROPOSED: The story is told of a Bout in which Veyra's quarry was not a champion at all. A thing had slipped into the Arena through a crack between worlds during the fighting: small, quick and hungry, and it went for the stands.

Veyra stepped away from the Bout without a word.

She followed it through one portal, then another. The crowd saw only flashes of her: in the stands, on the rim, briefly upside down above the Crown's shards. Each time she reappeared, her cloak had changed. It was no longer cloth. It was a window, and through it shone a desert, then an ocean, then a city of glass.

She caught the thing at the edge of the Arena, in the shadow of a pillar, and folded it gently back where it came from.

When she returned to the Bout, the window in her cloak still showed other worlds. She did not seem surprised.

"I hunt in all of them now." [canon]

## The Awakening — the Ascension beyond Ascension

PROPOSED: Nobody saw Veyra Awaken. Everyone saw what came after.

It was a Bout like any other. Veyra stood on her Sovereign's side of the field, rings glinting at her wrists. Then the rival champions began to fall, and Veyra had not moved.

Others had. At the edges of the Arena, in the shadows under the stands, in the gaps between the Crown's shards, there were huntresses. Dozens of them. All with portal-rings. All with the same calm face. A Veyra from every reality she had ever hunted in, and every one of them was hunting.

The rival Sovereign could not hide from them.

When the last rival champion fell, the hunters stepped back into their own worlds, one by one, until only one Veyra was left standing in the middle of the Arena.

"Between worlds!" [canon]

She looked up at the Crown for a long moment. Then she lowered her eyes.

"Caught." [canon]

She did not say what she had caught.

## Voice and temperament

Quiet, dry and certain. She speaks seldom and never wastes a word.

- "Every world has a back door." [canon]
- "I hunt in all of them now." [canon]

## Bonds and rivalries

- **Veilwalker** (PROPOSED): The most famous thing that walks between worlds, and the one thing Veyra has never hunted. She will not say why.
- **Dax Meridian** (PROPOSED): She told him once that she knows which reality he lost in. She has never told him which. He asks constantly.
- **Crownless** (PROPOSED): Veyra watches Crownless the way she watches quarry. Crownless does not seem to mind. They have spoken once, alone. Neither will say what about.

## Beneath the Arena (Set 2)

**Hunter of Guardians · Ascendant, Hunter.** Below the Arena, the Guardians stir, and Veyra recognises something in them. Her Set 2 card shows her stepping through a portal onto a Guardian's back, blade raised.

Her ability, **THE HUNT ETERNAL**, makes her stronger against Monsters, Guardians and every champion the Crown has already raised. Ascended, each champion she defeats draws you a card; Awakened, she readies after a defeat, just as **BETWEEN WORLDS** did above.

Some say she went below to hunt the Guardians. Others say she went to warn them.

## In the campaign: the Crowning of Chapter VII

Chapter VII ends with Veyra Sol. After the Veilwalker's last Bout, the Arena darkens, portals open around its rim, and Veyra steps through all of them at once.

- **The Crowning: Veyra Sol (Contender).** Act I. Veyra plays her standard printing with a Riftborn Flight-and-Ambush deck. Beat her and she says only, "Caught." [canon] She cannot be challenged again until Act II is complete.
- **Masterwork Crowning.** Reopens after Act II. Veyra plays her **Masterwork** printing (from Set 2) with a stronger deck and a proposed Edict, *Every Back Door*: her champions with Ambush cannot be targeted on the turn they arrive.
- **Omega Crowning.** Veyra plays her black-and-gold **Omega** printing with her hardest deck and a proposed Edict, *All of Them Are Hunting*: whenever one of her champions is defeated, she summons a copy of it with 1 Guard. Prestige reward: a portal-ring Sovereign cosmetic.
