# Zyrak — Shard of the Far Lattice
*Riftborn · Arcanist · Tier III · Chapter VII · Storm and Rift*

> Status: PROPOSED lore (draft for Jerian's approval). Canon quotes are marked [canon].

## Who they are

"Zyrak speaks in light. Most listeners go briefly blind; Zyrak considers this rude." [canon]

Zyrak is a crystal being from very far away, a walking cluster of facets with a single prism floating beside it. It does not speak in words. It speaks in colour, flashes and soft hums, and it is extremely polite. Zyrak is also, quietly, one of the most dangerous casters in the Crownfall.

## Before the Crownfall

PROPOSED: Zyrak comes from the Far Lattice, a reality made of crystal and light where every being is one facet of something larger. In the Lattice, thinking and shining are the same thing.

Zyrak was a small facet, one of millions, until something went wrong. A rift opened in the Lattice and Zyrak was shaken loose and sent tumbling between worlds.

It has been trying to be understood ever since.

## Why they answered the call

PROPOSED: Zyrak cannot find its way home. But it has noticed something about the Crown's shards above the Arena: they shine in a way that is almost, but not quite, the language of the Lattice. Zyrak cannot read them. It is sure they are saying something.

It answered a Sovereign's call to get close enough to listen.

## The Ascension

PROPOSED: Zyrak's first Bout went badly, and not because of the fighting. Every time it tried to speak to its own Sovereign, a flash of light made the little creature cover its eyes. Zyrak dimmed itself and stayed dim. It fought half-lit, and it was losing.

Then the shards above the Arena caught the evening sun, and for a moment their light fell across Zyrak's facets.

Zyrak understood one word. Only one, and only a little. But it was a word in the language of home.

Zyrak hummed, very softly. New facets pushed out from its body like frost growing on glass. Crystal shards lifted from its shoulders and formed a slow, turning halo above it. The light inside grew warmer and brighter, but this time, somehow, it did not hurt to look at.

Its Sovereign uncovered its eyes and stared.

"* shines brighter *" [canon]

Every rival champion on the field was caught in the glow at once, and none of them liked it. Zyrak thought that was rude of them.

## The Awakening — the Ascension beyond Ascension

PROPOSED: Zyrak Awakened in the quiet hour after a long Bout, when the Arena was empty and the shards turned slowly in a dark sky.

It had come out alone to listen again. It shone a question up at the Crown. The Crown did not answer, but the shards caught the question and threw it back, broken into a hundred colours.

Zyrak shone back. The shards shone back again. And with every reflection, Zyrak grew.

Facet on facet, arch on arch, it rose until it stood as tall as a tower, a walking cathedral of prisms, bending light into colours that had no names in any realm. Watchers on the far side of the Crownfall saw it and thought a second dawn had come.

In the next Bout, its first flash of Prismatic Collapse lit every rival champion at once.

"* hums in triumph *" [canon]

Zyrak still does not know what the shards were saying. It is fairly sure it was not "no."

## Voice and temperament

Gentle, curious, a little prim. Zyrak treats every conversation as precious, because so few succeed.

## Bonds and rivalries

- **Seren** (PROPOSED): The only champion who understands Zyrak without squinting. Seren talks to starlight all the time and says Zyrak's light is "just a very bright star with good manners." Zyrak glows pink whenever she says it.
- **Orion** (PROPOSED): Orion names every star he fires and is running out of names. He asked if he could name Zyrak's prism. Zyrak's answering flash blinded him for an hour. They are still not quite friends.
- **Captain Blacktide** (PROPOSED): Blacktide thinks Zyrak is worth a fortune. Zyrak hums at him in a way his crew agree sounds like laughing.

## Beneath the Arena (Set 2)

**Shard of the Broken Vault · Channeler.** Deep beneath the Arena, Zyrak finds a vault of shattered crystals. Every one of them is a piece of the Lattice. Its Set 2 card shows it humming among them, surrounded by the voices of home.

Below, Zyrak becomes a channeler, a champion who grows stronger as its Sovereign casts. Its ability, **Shard Resonance**, draws a card whenever you cast a Tactic. Awakened, every Tactic also Scorches each rival champion, and its Ascension lights them all again.

How pieces of the Far Lattice came to be buried under the Crownfall, Zyrak does not know. It is the first question it has ever been afraid to ask.
