# Mother Verdant — Life Eternal
*Verdant · Ascendant · Summoner · Tier V · Chapter IV · The Greenwall*
> Status: PROPOSED lore (draft for Jerian's approval). Canon quotes are marked [canon].

## Who they are
"Every forest in every world is one of her children. She would like the Crown for the last one." [canon]

Mother Verdant is the mother of forests, crowned in blossom, gentle as spring rain and as unstoppable. Wherever she walks, life follows, and she speaks to every champion as if they were her child. She wants the Crown, and she has told everyone why. What she means by it is another matter.

## Before the Crownfall
The Verdant say "The forest mends its own." [canon] [PROPOSED] Mother Verdant is the reason that saying is true. In every realm where a forest grows, the Verdant believe, a little of her spirit is in its roots. She has walked through many worlds and planted many seeds. The Greenwall is only one of her children. She loves it, as she loves them all.

## Why they answered the call
Her reason is canon: she would like the Crown for the last one. [PROPOSED] Which forest is "the last one," Mother Verdant has never said. Some believe it is a forest that has not yet been planted. Others believe it is one that has been lost, somewhere no seed can reach. A few, Maeve among them, wonder whether the last forest is the floating kingdom itself. Asked, she only smiles: every child is precious, the youngest most of all. [MYSTERY: Mother Verdant's ancient knowledge, and the full meaning of "the last one," stay unanswered.]

## The Ascension
Mother Verdant's first Ascension is remembered as the day the Arena bloomed.

A rival Sovereign's champions had cut down every ally of her banner. Mother Verdant walked slowly among them, touching each one gently on the brow, as a mother checks on sleeping children.

Then she lifted her face to the Crown.

The light that fell on her was soft and green. Her long train spread out behind her and became a meadow: grass, wildflowers and clover, rolling across the Arena floor wherever she had walked. From the edges of the meadow came deer, made of pure light, stepping quietly through the flowers to follow at her heels.

Where the meadow touched her fallen allies, they began to stir. Where it touched the ground, young trees rose up, sturdy and green, ready to stand guard.

Mother Verdant turned to the rival Sovereign, her blossom crown glowing.

"Spring has no end." [canon]

For the first time, the rival's champions took a step back.

## The Awakening — the Ascension beyond Ascension
Mother Verdant's Awakening is the story told at the close of the Greenwall.

It came in a Bout under an Edict of winter. The Arena floor was frozen, the sky was grey and every champion on the field felt their strength slipping away in the cold. Mother Verdant stood at the center of it all and closed her eyes.

The Crown answered her with the turning of the seasons.

Her form brightened until she shone like morning. She became a goddess of green, her robes the colour of new leaves, her crown a ring of flowers that never closed. She took a single step, and where her foot touched the ice, it melted. Grass sprang up. Flowers opened. Spring rushed outward from her in every direction, across the frozen Arena, across the walls, across the stands.

She opened her arms.

"Life eternal!" [canon]

Fallen allies rose again in the green light. Shields of blossom wrapped around every champion of her banner. The winter was gone.

Mother Verdant smiled gently at the fallen rival line.

"Bloom again." [canon]

## Voice and temperament
Mother Verdant is warm, patient and endlessly gentle, with a quiet strength beneath it that never bends. She greets her allies with "Come, my children." [canon] Her patience is the patience of a forest: she will wait a thousand years for what she wants.

## Bonds and rivalries
- **Gaia Prime** [PROPOSED]: the eldest tree titan has never quite agreed to be one of Mother Verdant's children. Their respect is deep, their silences long, and each knows something the other has not said.
- **Maeve** [PROPOSED]: the forest witch believes life should never be given freely. Mother Verdant gives it freely every day. Maeve finds this unsettling, and a little suspicious.
- **Bramble** [PROPOSED]: Mother Verdant calls him "my little root" and is always pleased to see how much he has grown. Bramble is very proud of this.

## Beneath the Arena (Set 2)
Beneath the Arena, the forest grows upward around Mother Verdant, pushing through the very foundations of the floating kingdom. She becomes the **Root of All Things**, an **Ascendant** **Binder**. [PROPOSED] Whether she is planting something below or waking something already there, no one can tell. Her **EVERGROWTH** summons Wild Saplings and Treants, and makes every token of her banner stronger.
