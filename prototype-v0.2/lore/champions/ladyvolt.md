# Lady Volt — Thunder in Heels
*Voltborn · Duelist · Tier IV · Chapter VII · Storm and Rift*

> Status: PROPOSED lore (draft for Jerian's approval). Canon quotes are marked [canon].

## Who they are

"Lady Volt has never lost a duel. She considers most battles to be several duels at once." [canon]

Lady Volt is the most elegant champion in the Crownfall and knows it. She fights with a slender rapier, salutes every opponent and treats a battlefield like a ballroom where each dance partner must be dismissed in turn. She is never rude, and never beaten.

## Before the Crownfall

PROPOSED: Among the Voltborn, the great storm-houses settle their quarrels by formal duel rather than war. Lady Volt was the duellist of the smallest house, which had never won anything. She won her first duel at fourteen, her hundredth at twenty, and soon the larger houses stopped quarrelling with hers at all.

That bored her terribly. A duellist without challengers is a dancer without music.

## Why they answered the call

PROPOSED: Lady Volt says she came for the company. The truth is a little sharper. She has never lost, and she has begun to wonder whether that is because she is the best or because she has never met the right opponent. If the right opponent exists anywhere, it is in the Crownfall.

What she will do if she finds them, she has not decided.

## The Ascension

The Bout was a crowded one: four rival champions on the far side, all advancing together. Lady Volt's Sovereign hesitated. She did not.

She stepped forward, raised her rapier in salute and spoke to the first of them as if no one else were there.

Then the second. Then the third. Each duel took a breath, a feint and a single touch of the blade. The fourth champion was larger and slower, and Lady Volt was smiling when she turned to face it.

That was when the storm in the sky above the Arena answered her. Silver light came down like rain and settled on her shoulders, and where it touched her it became armour, storm-silver and light as silk. Her hair lifted and crackled. Sparks ran down the rapier to its point.

The fourth champion stopped advancing.

Lady Volt raised her blade in salute once more.

"Lightning, en garde." [canon]

The duel was very short. She thanked her opponent afterwards, which somehow made it worse.

## The Awakening — the Ascension beyond Ascension

PROPOSED: Lady Volt Awakened in the one duel she thought she might lose.

The champion across from her was quick, quicker than anyone she had faced. Her first touch missed. So did her second. For the first time in her life, Lady Volt felt something cold in her chest that might have been fear, and might have been joy.

She stopped thinking about the blade. She stopped thinking about her feet. She thought only about being where her opponent was not.

And then she was. The crowd saw a flash of white across the Arena, a line drawn from one side to the other, and Lady Volt standing at the end of it with her rapier lowered. A heartbeat later, the thunder arrived, rolling over the stands so loudly the banners shook.

She had not run. She had not leapt. She had simply become lightning carrying a sword.

"Thunderstep!" [canon]

Her opponent's blade lay on the stones. Lady Volt was still undefeated. She looked, for a moment, almost disappointed.

## Voice and temperament

Gracious, teasing and supremely confident. She speaks to opponents with old-fashioned courtesy and to friends with dry wit.

- "Shall we duel?" [canon]
- "Touché." [canon]

## Bonds and rivalries

- **Ronin Zero** (PROPOSED): Their one duel is the most argued-about moment in Voltborn gossip. She has never lost. He has never claimed to win. She invites him to a rematch every season. He bows and says, "Not yet."
- **Kira Volt** (PROPOSED): Lady Volt finds the young gunner delightful and calls her "little spark," which Kira hates.
- **Valkara** (PROPOSED): Two champions who strike from above and from nowhere. Valkara judges. Lady Volt duels. They have agreed never to settle which is faster, because neither could bear the answer.

## Beneath the Arena (Set 2)

**Storm in the Deep · Sharpshooter.** In the flooded caverns, Lady Volt discovers that lightning travels very well across water and very badly through rock. So she stops closing the distance. Her Set 2 card shows lightning arcing off the wet cavern walls as she strikes from across the dark.

Her ability becomes **Chain Lightning**: a bolt that Scorches a rival champion and, once she has Awakened, leaps to a second. Lady Volt insists this is still duelling. "Two duels," she says, "at a very polite distance."

## In the campaign

- **Trial 1 · Bout 4 — "Several Duels at Once."** Lady Volt's introduction. Face four small rival champions with Lady Volt alone. A puzzle Bout: use Thunderstep to ready her after each defeat. She joins your banner with a curtsey.
- **Trial 2 · Bout 3 — "The Gloves."** Lady Volt tests your deck with a Voltborn duel deck.
- **Trial 3 · Bout 4 — "Thunder After."** A gauntlet Bout under a proposed Edict, *Storm Court*: each champion may attack only one rival champion per turn.
