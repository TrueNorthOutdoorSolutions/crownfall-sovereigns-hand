# Elara — Field Surgeon of the Realm
*Kingdom · Mystic · Tier II · Chapter I · The First Light*

> Status: PROPOSED lore (draft for Jerian's approval). Canon quotes are marked [canon].

## Who they are

"Elara has saved more soldiers than any general has led." [canon]

Elara is a battlefield healer with a satchel of salves, rolled-up sleeves and a voice that cuts through noise. She is not a priestess and does not pretend to be one. Her magic is real, but she trusts clean bandages and steady hands just as much.

She does not care much about the Crown. She cares about who is still standing when the bout is done.

## Before the Crownfall

Elara grew up in the Kingdom realm, the daughter of a village herbalist. When the Kingdom went to war on its northern border, she followed the army as a helper in the field tents, boiling water and rolling bandages. Within a year she was running the tents.

She learned her light magic late, from an old book left behind by a travelling healer.

The war ended. Elara did not go home. She went wherever soldiers still got hurt.

## Why they answered the call

The Crownfall is full of champions who fight, fall and fight again. To Elara, it looked like the biggest field hospital in history, with no one properly in charge. She answered a Sovereign's call because she wanted a place on the field, not in the stands.

She also has a quieter reason. She has heard that the Crown can raise champions into more than they were. She wants to know if it can be taught to heal.

## The Ascension

The bout had gone badly. Three champions of her banner lay on the sand, and the rival was pressing hard. Elara was on her knees between them with one hand on a wound and the other holding her tome open on the ground.

"No," she said, to nobody in particular. "Not today."

The book began to glow brighter than it ever had, so bright it lit her face from below. Her dusty field coat became white-and-blue royal vestments, clean as a fresh sheet. The light from the tome spilled across the sand and found each fallen champion, one by one, and lifted them.

"No one falls today." [canon]

She stood. Her banner stood with her.

Afterwards, Elara sat alone for a long time, the tome closed on her knees. She had always believed that healing came from skill and care. Now the Crown had answered her. She was grateful. She was also, just a little, afraid of how much she wanted it to answer again.

## The Awakening — the Ascension beyond Ascension

Her Awakening came in a bout that should have been a disaster. The rival's champions had swept the field, and Elara was the last of her banner left standing. Everyone she had mended was gone from the field.

She did not run. She opened her tome to the last page, the one that had always been blank.

Light poured out of it. It rose like a column, then spread like wings, and Elara stood in the middle of it, looking for a moment like an angel of the field hospital. The light found the fallen of her banner and called them back to her, two of them at once. Then it wrapped every champion she had in a bright shield.

The rival's attack broke against that shield like rain on glass.

When the bout was over, Elara walked the field and counted heads, the way she always does.

"Everyone accounted for." [canon]

## Voice and temperament

Elara is brisk, practical and warm underneath. She gives orders to generals without noticing. She has no patience for heroics that get people hurt. Her first words on any field are the same: "Who needs mending?" [canon]

## Bonds and rivalries

- **General Varr** — Elara has patched up Varr more times than either can count. He calls her the finest soldier in the Kingdom. She tells him to sit still.
- **Solara** — Two healers from different worlds. Solara prays while she heals; Elara stitches while she talks. They have learned a lot from each other, though Elara would never admit to praying.
- **Grizz** — Grizz once wandered into her field tent with a thorn in his paw and refused to leave until he was given a biscuit. He now visits after every bout. Elara pretends to be annoyed.

## Beneath the Arena (Set 2)

When the banners went down beneath the Arena, the climb back up became the hardest part. Explorers fell in the tunnels, and some halls collapsed with champions still inside.

Elara went down after them. Below, she became the **Healer of the Fallen Deep**, a Restorer tending the wounded by lantern light in a collapsed hall. Her ability, **Field Mending**, shields friends when she arrives and mends what the deep breaks. At her strongest, she still does what she always did: she brings the fallen back into the fight.

She has set up a field tent under the Arena.

## In the campaign

- **Trial 1 · Bout 6 — "Triage":** A puzzle Bout. The player starts with three champions in the Fallen pile and Elara in hand. Return the right champion at the right time to win in four turns. Teaches the Fallen pile.
- **Trial 2 · Bout 5 — "The Surgeon's Test":** Elara tests the player with a Kingdom deck that keeps bringing champions back.
- **Trial 3 · Bout 4 — "No One Falls" (Edict in play):** The Edict says the first champion each player loses each turn returns to their hand. Elara is an ally card. Win with every champion still alive for a bonus reward.
