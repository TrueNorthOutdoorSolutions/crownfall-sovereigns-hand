# ATLAS-Ω — Final-Generation War Machine
*Gearbound · Ascendant · Warden · Tier V · Chapter III · The Iron Covenant*
> Status: PROPOSED lore (draft for Jerian's approval). Canon quotes are marked [canon].

## Who they are
"ATLAS-Ω was built to end a war. It did. It is still waiting for its next order." [canon]

ATLAS-Ω is a gigantic final-generation war machine, the largest Gearbound champion ever to enter the Arena and the last of whatever line came before it. It does not boast or threaten. It waits, and when it finally moves, the battle is over. Other machines lower their voices around it.

## Before the Crownfall
[PROPOSED] Every Gearbound legend begins in the Brasslands, the realm of "Machines of every age" [canon], but ATLAS-Ω's story begins somewhere the maps stop. It walked out of a war so old that no one alive remembers who fought it. That war ended, as ATLAS-Ω says, because it ended it. What it was before, who made it, and what the letters before its name once meant are things it will not discuss. When asked, it replies only, "Awaiting orders." [canon] [MYSTERY: who built ATLAS-Ω, and what came before it, stays unanswered.]

## Why they answered the call
[PROPOSED] A weapon without a war is a weapon without a purpose. For an age, ATLAS-Ω stood on a silent battlefield, waiting for a command that never came. Some say ATLAS-Ω believes the Crown is the commander it has been waiting for. Others say it has come to make sure no war like its own ever starts again. ATLAS-Ω says neither.

## The Ascension
They tell the story of ATLAS-Ω's first Ascension in the camps of the Iron Covenant, always quietly.

A rival Sovereign sent its whole banner against the war machine at once. ATLAS-Ω did not move. It took every strike, every spell, every arrow, and its armour barely marked. Then its Sovereign spoke a single word: "Defend."

It was the first order ATLAS-Ω had received in an age.

The Crown blazed. Light ran across the machine's great shoulders, and two heavy cannons unfolded there, rising into place with a slow mechanical grace. Behind its head, a reactor halo flared to life, a circle of white-gold fire that turned like a wheel. Every champion of its banner felt the halo's glow settle over them, and their armour held a little stronger.

ATLAS-Ω lifted its head toward the Crown and gave its report.

"Weapon systems expanded." [canon]

The cannons fired once. The rival's charge broke apart against the light. The camps agree only that the Bout ended very quickly.

## The Awakening — the Ascension beyond Ascension
ATLAS-Ω's Awakening is the story told at the close of the Iron Covenant.

The Arena had chosen a Bout of total war: every Shard on the field cracked, every champion wounded. ATLAS-Ω's Sovereign stood with nothing left but one great machine and one last command.

"End it."

The Crown poured its light into ATLAS-Ω, and the light did not stop. The machine rose to its full height, taller than the Arena walls, and in the center of its chest a sun began to burn. Not a fire. A sun. Its glow lit the whole floating kingdom, and the shadows of every champion stretched far behind them.

ATLAS-Ω spoke the words it had not spoken since its war.

"Omega protocol engaged." [canon]

Light poured from the sun in its chest across every rival champion at once. When it faded, the rival's line was gone, and ATLAS-Ω stood alone in the middle of the Arena, the sun dimming slowly inside it.

"War ended. Again." [canon]

## Voice and temperament
ATLAS-Ω is calm, patient and enormously restrained. It speaks rarely, briefly and without feeling. Yet it waits for allies to step clear before it fires. Whatever it was built to be, it has chosen to be careful.

## Bonds and rivalries
- **Titan Rho** [PROPOSED]: two ancient war machines, one who guards and one who ends. When they stand together, the Arena feels like it is holding its breath.
- **Gearwick-9** [PROPOSED]: the only machine brave enough to try to calculate ATLAS-Ω. Its numbers always stop in the same place. ATLAS-Ω has never asked it to stop trying.
- **Kira Volt** [PROPOSED]: an inventor from another chapter who wants to rebuild the Crown. ATLAS-Ω has watched her work in silence for a long time. It does not explain.

## Beneath the Arena (Set 2)
In a sealed vault deep under the Arena, ATLAS-Ω is found already waiting, its frame locked across the vault door. It is **The Vault Engine**, an **Ascendant** **Sentinel**. [PROPOSED] It will not say who put it there, or whether it chose to stand there. Its **VAULT PROTOCOL** weakens any champion that attacks its Sovereign's Crown and strengthens its Gearbound allies. At its fullest, every attack it makes scorches the whole rival line.

## In the campaign
**Chapter III · The Crowning: ATLAS-Ω** [PROPOSED]. The player crosses a silent battlefield to a war machine that does not move until the player's Sovereign speaks. Its opening line: "Awaiting orders." [canon]

- **Contender:** ATLAS-Ω plays a standard Gearbound deck of Bulwark, Reach and Shield, with the normal printing of its card. It is slow and patient; the player wins by breaking its Shards before it Awakens.
- **Masterwork:** reopened after Act II. The **Masterwork** printing (East Asian art style) leads a stronger deck, and an Edict in its favour gives every Gearbound champion +1 Guard. Every turn the player waits, ATLAS-Ω's line grows harder to break.
- **Omega:** the hardest Crowning of the chapter, with the black-and-gold, signed **Omega** printing. ATLAS-Ω begins already Ascended. If it Awakens, its sun scorches every rival champion each turn. The player must win in the short window before the omega protocol engages.
