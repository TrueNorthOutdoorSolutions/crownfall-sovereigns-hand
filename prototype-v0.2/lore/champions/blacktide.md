# Captain Blacktide — Terror of the Rift Seas
*Riftborn · Artillerist · Tier III · Chapter VII · Storm and Rift*

> Status: PROPOSED lore (draft for Jerian's approval). Canon quotes are marked [canon].

## Who they are

"Blacktide sails seas that exist only between realities. He charges tolls." [canon]

Captain Blacktide is a dimensional pirate with a hand cannon, a booming laugh and a ledger he takes very seriously. He is loud, greedy and theatrical, but he has a code: he never takes more than the toll, and he never leaves a sailor stranded between worlds.

## Before the Crownfall

PROPOSED: Between the realities there are seas. Most people never see them; they step through a rift and come out the other side without noticing the dark water in between. Blacktide noticed. He has sailed those seas for longer than he admits, captain of a ship that is never quite all there and a crew that is mostly spray and memory.

Anyone crossing between worlds passes through his waters, and Blacktide believes passage should be paid for. Those who refuse hear the cannons.

## Why they answered the call

PROPOSED: The Crownfall is the busiest crossing of all. Blacktide came to collect.

He has a second, quieter reason. He believes the Crown passed through his seas once, long ago, without paying. He has never told anyone what he means by that, and he gets angry if pressed. It may be a pirate's tall tale. It may not.

## The Ascension

PROPOSED: Blacktide's first Bout in the Arena was a disaster. His rival's champions rushed him from three sides, and his single hand cannon could only point one way at a time.

He backed up against the Arena wall. For the first time in years, he thought about his crew. They had not come with him. Nobody crosses into the Crownfall with a whole ship.

Then he smelled salt.

Seawater began to drip from his coat, though there was no sea for a hundred miles. Behind him, faint and grey, shapes rose out of the air: sailors in old coats, ropes over their shoulders, hauling cannons that should not have fit on the Arena floor. His crew. Every one of them.

Blacktide's laugh shook the stands.

"Crew, to the cannons!" [canon]

The broadside lit the Arena from end to end. When the smoke cleared, the rival champions were scattered, and his ghostly crew were saluting.

He wiped his eye and blamed the smoke.

## The Awakening — the Ascension beyond Ascension

PROPOSED: Blacktide Awakened on the night he finally caught a toll-dodger he had been chasing for years: a rival Sovereign whose champions had slipped through his seas a dozen times without paying.

Their champions stood in a line, smug and ready. Blacktide stood alone, his spectral crew behind him.

Then the Arena floor rippled like water. Something huge rose behind him, slow and creaking: the prow of a ship, then a great carved figurehead, black and gold, its wooden eyes glowing. The ghost galleon itself had come through.

The stands went silent. Blacktide did not turn around. He did not need to.

He raised his hand cannon. The galleon's guns rolled out in a row above his head.

He fired, and they fired with him.

When the thunder faded, the rival line was gone, and Blacktide was leaning on his cannon, writing in his ledger.

"Plunder!" [canon]

The toll, he said, was now paid in full.

## Voice and temperament

Booming, greedy, sentimental and secretly kind.

- "Toll's due, landlubber." [canon]
- "Crew, to the cannons!" [canon]

## Bonds and rivalries

- **Dax Meridian** (PROPOSED): Blacktide's favourite debtor. Dax insists he has never used Blacktide's seas. His page in the ledger grows anyway.
- **Veilwalker** (PROPOSED): A door between worlds that walks around on its own. Blacktide considers it the single worst toll-dodger in existence. He has tried to send it a bill. The bill vanished.
- **Zyrak** (PROPOSED): Blacktide is convinced Zyrak's crystals are worth a fortune. Zyrak hums at him in a way the crew agree sounds like laughing.

## Beneath the Arena (Set 2)

**Plunderer of the Vault · Rogue.** Beneath the Arena lie the Guardians' treasure vaults, and Blacktide has never seen a treasure he would not like to collect a toll on. His Set 2 card shows his ship wedged in a rift above a Guardian's hoard.

Below, he trades cannons for cunning. His ability, **Plunder**, takes a Relic from a rival champion and attaches it to Blacktide. Ascended, every champion he defeats on your turn draws you a card; Awakened, he instead takes the Relic the moment he Ascends, and his crew's broadside still Scorches every rival champion when he attacks.

"I'm not stealing," he says. "I'm collecting back-tolls. With interest."

## In the campaign

- **Trial 1 · Bout 6 — "Toll's Due."** Blacktide's introduction. He blocks the path and demands a toll: you begin the Bout with one fewer card in hand, but if you win he returns it and joins your banner, laughing.
- **Trial 2 · Bout 1 — "Broadside."** Blacktide tests your deck with an artillery deck that Scorches every rival champion on attack.
- **Trial 3 · Bout 5 — "The Galleon."** A gauntlet Bout under a proposed Edict, *High Tide*: champions with Reach get +1 Might. Win to see the ghost galleon rise.
