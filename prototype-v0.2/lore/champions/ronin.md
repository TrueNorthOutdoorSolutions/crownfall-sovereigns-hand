# Ronin Zero — The Frame That Wanders
*Voltborn · Duelist · Tier III · Chapter VII · Storm and Rift*

> Status: PROPOSED lore (draft for Jerian's approval). Canon quotes are marked [canon].

## Who they are

"Ronin Zero forgot his master's name. He did not forget the technique." [canon]

Ronin Zero is a wandering swordsman of metal and current, with a straw-coloured visor and a blade he keeps sharper than his memories. He speaks rarely and briefly. He bows before every duel and after it, win or lose. "Ronin Zero" is what the Voltborn call him: the wanderer, number nothing.

## Before the Crownfall

Ronin does not remember being made. His earliest memory is a quiet courtyard of raked gravel and a hand on his shoulder, correcting the angle of his sword. A voice saying, "Again." The same cut, ten thousand times. He cannot remember the face.

Then the courtyard was gone and Ronin was walking. People who saw him draw asked where he had learned it. He told them the truth: he did not know.

Over the years the master's name faded, then the courtyard, then the voice. The technique stayed perfect. Ronin thinks this is the cruellest thing that ever happened to him, and also the kindest.

## Why they answered the call

PROPOSED: Ronin believes that somewhere among the Crown's champions is someone who learned from the same master. A style that perfect, he reasons, cannot belong to only one student, and another student may remember what he cannot.

He fights each duel hoping his rival will stop and say, "Where did you learn that?" No one has yet.

## The Ascension

The rival was another swordsman, fast and showy, and the crowd loved him. Ronin did not mind the crowd. He minded that the rival's third cut looked almost like his own: almost the same angle, almost the same footwork.

He lowered his blade. "Who taught you?"

The rival laughed and attacked.

Ronin moved. He did not decide to; his frame decided for him, the way it always had. And as he moved he heard, very faintly, a voice he had not heard in years: "Again."

Lines of arc-light traced themselves along his frame, shoulder to wrist, as if someone were drawing the correct path for him one more time. His blade began to hum, a low, steady note like a held breath.

The cut was perfect. The rival's sword spun away across the stones.

Ronin stood very still, listening. The voice did not come back. He did not know whether the Crown had given him the memory or only lent it.

"Faster than memory." [canon]

He bowed. The arc-lines stayed.

## The Awakening — the Ascension beyond Ascension

Ronin Awakened in a duel he was losing.

His opponent was not faster than him, but stronger, and every block cost him. Sparks poured from a cracked plate on his arm. The crowd had gone quiet. Ronin sheathed his sword.

Watchers thought he was giving up. In truth, he had remembered the first lesson. Before the cut there is the draw. Before the draw there is stillness.

He waited. His rival charged.

Lightning gathered in his scabbard, crackling at his hip, so bright the stones around him went white. Then he drew.

The crowd saw three of him, then five, then a blur of afterimages flickering across the Arena, each one a heartbeat behind the real Ronin, who was already standing on the far side with his blade held out and level.

Behind him, his rival sat down very slowly on the stones.

Ronin sheathed his blade with a soft click.

"The technique remains." [canon]

## Voice and temperament

Calm, formal and polite; short sentences, never a boast. When asked to teach, he always begins with "Again."

- "Frame ready." [canon]
- "The technique remains." [canon]

## Bonds and rivalries

- **Lady Volt** (PROPOSED): They have crossed blades once. Neither will say how it ended. She has never lost a duel; he has never claimed to win it.
- **Kira Volt** (PROPOSED): She wants to open his frame "just to look." He refuses. He keeps a little box of the small repairs she leaves him.
- **Astryon** (PROPOSED): Ronin watches the star-swordsman fight with great care, looking for one familiar cut. He has not found one. He has not stopped looking.

## Beneath the Arena (Set 2)

**Blade Beneath the Arena · Gladiator.** In a flooded training hall under the Arena, Ronin finds a relic sword whose hilt bears the same marks as his own. His card shows him alone in the tunnel, crackling with current, the relic drawn.

Below, he fights head-on. **Arena Draw** gives him extra Might while attacking a champion; once Ascended he readies again after each defeat, as above; Awakened, his first cut also Scorches the defender.

The hall has a hundred empty sword racks. Ronin does not yet know whose they were.
