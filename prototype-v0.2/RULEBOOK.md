# Crownfall: Sovereign's Hand — Paper Prototype Rules

Rules version 0.2-paper-1 · 2 players · about 15–20 minutes · not for sale

## The goal

**Break all five of your rival's Crown Shards.** Deploy champions, Ascend them from ★ to ★★★, and attack your rival's Crown. A champion that attacks can't guard your Crown on your rival's turn, so every attack leaves you open.

## What you need

- One starter deck each: 30 cards, plus 8 Ascension cards (★★ and ★★★ forms).
- A Command counter each (the Command track on the token sheet with a coin, or a ten-sided die).
- Shield tokens and a few coins to mark "this turn" changes.
- Opaque card sleeves are recommended, so face-down cards stay hidden.

## Your play area

| Zone | What goes there |
| --- | --- |
| **Deck** | Your face-down 30-card deck. |
| **Crown** | Your 5 Crown Shards: face-down cards from your own deck. |
| **Ascension Pile** | Your 8 ★★/★★★ cards, face-down. You never draw from it. |
| **Champion Zones (4)** | Your champions in play. |
| **Scheme Zones (3)** | Your face-down Schemes. |
| **Fallen pile** | Defeated champions (with their whole stack) and used Tactics and Schemes, face-up. |

## Setup

1. Shuffle your deck. Put the top 5 cards face-down in your Crown, without looking. These are your **Crown Shards**.
2. Flip a coin. The winner goes first.
3. The first player draws 5 cards. The second player draws **6**.
4. Once each, you may shuffle your hand back and draw the same number again.
5. The first player skips their draw on their first turn. The second player gets **+1 Command** on their first turn.

## Your turn

### 1. Rise

Stand all your sideways cards back up (**ready** them). Raise your Command dial by 1, to a maximum of 8, and refill your Command to that number.
*From round 10:* "The Crown grows impatient." Break one of your own shards before you do anything else.

### 2. Draw

Draw 1 card. If your deck is empty, break one of your own shards instead.

### 3. Main

Do any of these in any order, as long as you can pay:

- **Deploy** a ★ champion: pay its cost and put it in an empty Champion Zone. It can block straight away, but it can't attack this turn unless it has **Charge**.
- **Cast a Tactic**: pay its cost, do what it says, then put it in your Fallen pile.
- **Set a Scheme**: put it face-down in a Scheme Zone for free. You can't spring it during the turn you set it.
- **Ascend**, once per turn (see below).

### 4. Battle

Attack with your champions, one at a time (see Combat).

### 5. After battle

If you haven't Ascended this turn, you may Ascend now.

### 6. End

All "this turn" changes end. Discard down to 8 cards. Command you didn't spend **stays on your dial**, and you can use it on your rival's turn to spring Schemes and cast Swift Tactics.

## Ascending (★ → ★★ → ★★★)

Your ★★ and ★★★ cards wait in your Ascension Pile, so you never need to draw them.
Once per turn, either during your Main step or after battle:

1. Choose a champion that was already on the field when your turn began, and whose next form is in your Ascension Pile.
2. Pay that form's **Ascend cost** (the ↑ number).
3. Place the new form on top of the champion. Its Shield stays.
4. **Turn the champion sideways.** Ascending uses up its action, so it can't attack or block until your next turn. A champion that already attacked is sideways anyway, so it can still Ascend.
5. Resolve its **Ascend:** text, if it has any.

The real choice: Ascend before battle to use the Ascend effect now, Ascend after battle with a champion that has already attacked, or keep the champion standing so it can guard your Crown.

## Combat

1. **Attack.** Choose one of your ready champions that can attack. Choose a target: your rival's **Crown**, or **any rival champion**. Turn your attacker sideways and resolve its **Attack:** text.
2. **Block** (only if the Crown is attacked). Your rival may block with one of their ready champions, turning it sideways (a champion with **Bulwark** stays standing). A champion with **Flight** can only be blocked by Flight or Reach. Resolve the blocker's **Block:** text.
3. **Respond.** The defender may spring one Scheme or cast one Swift Tactic. Then the attacker may cast one Swift Tactic.
4. **Clash** (if there is a defender). Compare at the same time:
   - If the attacker's **Might** is equal to or greater than the defender's **Guard**, the defender is defeated.
   - If the defender's **Might** is equal to or greater than the attacker's **Guard**, the attacker is defeated.
   - A champion with 0 Might defeats nothing.
5. **Break shards.**
   - An unblocked Crown attack breaks **1** shard, or **2** if the attacker is ★★★ (shown by the ♛2 symbol on the card).
   - **Breakthrough:** if the attacker defeats its blocker and survives, it still breaks 1 shard.

A defeated champion goes to its owner's Fallen pile with its whole stack. Shields on it are lost.

## Breaking a shard

Take the top shard into your **hand**. It was always one of your own cards. If it has **Shardfall**, you may cast it or set it immediately for free, even on your rival's turn.
**You lose the moment your last shard breaks.**

## Terms

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Shield** | Put a Shield token on the champion. The next time it would be defeated, remove the token instead. A champion holds at most 1 Shield. |
| **Scorch X** | The champion gets −X Guard this turn. |
| **Mend 1** | Put a card from your hand face-down into your Crown as a new shard (5 shards maximum). |
| **Charge** | Can attack the turn it is deployed. |
| **Bulwark** | Blocking doesn't turn this champion sideways. |
| **Ambush** | When this attacks a sideways champion, it strikes first. If the defender is defeated, it doesn't strike back. |
| **Flight / Reach** | Flight can only be blocked by champions with Flight or Reach. |
| **Swift** | This Tactic can also be cast in a response window, on either player's turn. |
| **Ascendant** | A Tier V champion. A deck may hold only one. |
| **Arrival: / Ascend: / Attack: / Block:** | Happens when the champion is deployed / Ascends into this form / attacks / blocks. |
| **Round** | Both players have taken one turn. |

## Quick answers

- **Can I attack a champion instead of the Crown?** Yes, any rival champion, standing or sideways. No shards break, but you can clear a blocker away.
- **Does damage stay on a champion?** No. Every clash is a fresh comparison.
- **Can a champion that just arrived block?** Yes.
- **Who acts first in a response window?** The defender, then the attacker. Each side gets one response per attack.
