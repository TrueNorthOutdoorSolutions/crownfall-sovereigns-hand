# Set 2 re-list: rarity per card (owner direction 2026-10-11)

Rarity is how much a card **does in play**, chosen per card, not by tier. Cards are numbered by rarity: Commons first, Mythics last.

| Rarity | What it means | Cards | Numbers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Common | Basic. Plain stats or one simple, narrow effect. | 117 | 001–117 |
| Uncommon | A little better: a useful effect in the right situation. | 81 | 118–198 |
| Elite | Strong: a real advantage or a deck-building enabler. | 31 | 199–229 |
| Epic | Game-swinging: a big payoff for building around it. | 21 | 230–250 |
| Mythic | Game-defining: rule-bending power, clearly beyond its Set 1 version. | 16 | 251–266 |
