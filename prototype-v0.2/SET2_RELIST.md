# Set 2 re-list: rarity per card

Rarity is how much a card **does in play**, chosen per card and per star form, never by tier. It follows the rarity rubric adopted by Jerian on 2026-10-12 (`RARITY_RUBRIC.md`); every change from the first re-list (2026-10-11) is in `AUDIT_CHANGES.md`. Cards are numbered by rarity: Commons first, Mythics last. Printed cards (001–033, 118, 251, 252) never change number.

| Rarity | What it means | Cards | Numbers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Common | Basic. Plain stats or one simple, narrow effect. | 99 | 001–099 |
| Uncommon | A signpost: a useful effect that tells you what its Class or Origin does. | 78 | 100–177 |
| Elite | Strong: repeatable advantage or the heart of a deck. | 50 | 178–227 |
| Epic | Game-swinging: a big payoff for building around it. | 22 | 228–249 |
| Mythic | Game-defining: it bends a rule, clearly beyond its Set 1 version. | 17 | 250–266 |

17 Mythics is above the rubric target of 10–14 (a documented exception): printed cards 251 and 252 must stay inside the Mythic block, which forces at least 16 Mythics (251–266), and ATLAS-Ω ★★★ was promoted to Mythic (audit priority 1), so the block is 250–266. See `AUDIT_CHANGES.md`.
